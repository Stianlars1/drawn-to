## The favorites: what to learn and what to protect

### Payper: a form beside its visible consequence

[Four onboarding states](posts/disarto_max-2093019047851913475.md), owner: **elsker**.

The first view introduces a tangible layered app mark; later views replace it
with the profile the person is creating. The action and result occupy stable
neighboring planes. Near-black on the left and a slightly lighter right pane
separate jobs without two floating containers. White buttons dominate; green
progress provides orientation. In the username view, the preview repeats the
entered handle and the active field has a localized outline/glow.

**Keep:** the spatial relationship, narrow form, stable preview and progression
from brand object to user-created artifact. **Change for production:** preserve
readable helpers and labels, show a textual step count, handle actual validation
and test keyboard/mobile behavior. The stills do not prove live mirroring.
**Do not apply:** a requirement to change the illustration device in every step;
repetition is essential to understanding this sequence.

### Basit: still words inside a moving collection

[The circular image collection](posts/basit_designs-2095862418518008252.md), owner: **elsker**.

A shared circular aperture makes stone, foliage, fabric, warm objects and bright
reflective surfaces belong together. The two phone presentations explore an
orbit-like arrangement and a vertical stream. Scale and edge cropping add depth;
copy and a black pill action remain stationary. At larger inspection, the copy
uses darker leading words and a quieter trailing phrase. The effect comes from
the relationship between still anchors and moving content, not from adding an
entrance animation to the headline.

**Keep:** aperture consistency, heterogeneous image content, a protected reading
zone and a useful static composition. **Watch:** some large circles approach the
bottom action; inspect the complete motion envelope. A collection needs original
relevant imagery. A field of random stock images is not equivalent.
**Do not infer:** touch input, CSS perspective, WebGL or a seamless loop from
this recording alone.

### Glaux: paper, serif and a place to inhabit

[Three illustrated footer panels](posts/AdityaSur11-2096160317458030911.md), owner: **sykt kult**.

The landscape is connected terrain: hills, cypress, buildings, bridge and water.
White building faces and dark foliage establish the larger shapes; small window,
stone and leaf marks reward close inspection. Terrain rises from the bottom and
leaves quiet cream sky for navigation. Serif appears in headings and body, not
as one token word inside a technical sans system. One blue ink relates the tiny
brand mark to the largest illustrated area.

**Keep:** connected scenery, negative space shaped by terrain, two detail scales
and type that belongs to the illustration. **Watch:** fine marks and light gray
links need testing after resizing. These tall panels do not establish a mobile
breakpoint or a single continuous panorama. Create an original scene with a
wide/narrow crop plan; keep links as actual text.

### Moein: a character physically belongs to the interface

[Pricing](posts/DesignByMoein-2095937596665024817.md) and
[footer](posts/DesignByMoein-2096289484577071567.md), owner: **veldig kult** for both.

The same orange box-headed character inhabits cream surfaces. In pricing, the
body is behind the card edge while the hands sit in front; contact shadow sells
the ledge. In the footer, plant, books, cushion and mug make a small lived-in
scene. Handwritten notes occupy spare corners without disrupting the regular
pricing/link grid. The figure changes pose while the information remains still.

**Keep:** one character/material system, contact and occlusion, restrained
annotation and separation of expressive art from practical UI. **Watch:** do not
obscure prices, links or newsletter input. A subtle eased/boomerang character loop
is legitimate; a law that every repeat must move linearly would damage it.
The pricing emphasis is a dark CTA and small tinted chip, not a larger card.

### Basit: reflective color above quiet explanatory forms

[The ribbon hero](posts/basit_designs-2095821165306732974.md), owner: **veldig kult**.

The ribbon has a clear edge, narrow necks, dark folds and changing reflective
colors. Below, three dark cards show distinct dot/line forms. The shared page
grid and calm interface contain a very expressive material. A larger frame
also reveals color inversion in parts of the heading as the ribbon crosses it;
that interaction is visually interesting but requires legibility checks in
several phases, not approval from the most flattering still.

**Keep:** contrast between continuous colored material and restrained lower
geometry; dark troughs are as important as highlights. **Do not assume:** a
specific shader or automatic readability from a blend mode.

### Adrian and Dmitry: demonstrate a system at multiple scales

[Stepped brand board](posts/adriankuleszo-2096048196988580223.md), owner: **sykt kult**;
[product icon system](posts/DmitryLepisov-2093247212629139641.md), owner: **veldig kult**.

Adrian carries a violet bar motif and circular pixel mark across a headline,
app icon, favicon, wordmark and notification crop. Some letters take a pixel
construction that echoes the mark. Reveal, hold and exit make the relationship
readable. Dmitry pairs a large translucent 3D icon with its flat in-product
version: the small version preserves recognition while discarding expensive
material detail.

**Keep:** one recognizable atom, deliberate differences by output size and tests
in actual contexts. **Do not do:** shrink the glossy hero into an unreadable icon
or use identical brand art as the explanation for unrelated features.

## How this changes proposals

Offer different relationships before different skins: a scenic editorial close,
a character inhabiting practical UI, a live result beside a form, a kinetic
collection around still words, or a material-led hero. Select what serves the
actual product; do not offer all of them on every task.

The existing eight families remain useful seeds. Paper & Print now includes an
illustrated/serif-led direction; Soft Pastel Stage can be warm neutral with small
radii and character art; Tactile Instruments can inform a marketing render
without turning the real interface into hardware. These are extensions of the
vocabulary, not mandatory trends or numerical weights attributed to the owner.
