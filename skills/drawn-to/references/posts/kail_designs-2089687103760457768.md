- Contrast strategy: two sites are luminance-driven (saturated color on near-black), the third is ink-on-cream with 2px outlines everywhere - a deliberate opposites pair.

## Graphic language
- **handkit**: photoreal-ish 3D hands rendered as "ghosts" - dark translucent volume with colored rim light only; reads as neon x-ray. Sold via box-shot mockups (dark boxes + one white FREE box) at the bottom.

## Extractable rules
- Let a live interaction prove each feature claim: a "7 colors" card should recolor the real grid, not show a static palette.
- Make a light/dark flip one of the color options - the most surprising swatch is the one that changes the surface, not the content.
- Put copy-SVG / copy-JSX / download actions on tile hover (shape dims to ~30%, 2x2 pill group fades in ~150ms) and render the "Copied!" confirmation inside the tile, not as a global toast.
- Keep asset-grid tiles mute (bg ~#151719, radius 16-24px, no borders) so saturated content is the only color on the page.
- On dark asset pages, derive the entire palette from the assets' own glow; UI chrome stays #9AA0A8 at ~13-15px.
- Use instant (<150ms) state swaps for color-variant switching; reserve tweens for spatial motion.
- For playful light UI: cream #F8F8EE ground, single ink color #110340 for all strokes at 2px, radius 24-28px, hard 4px offset shadows instead of blurs.
- Peel the hero on scroll: scale to ~92% + rotate ~-2.5deg as one card, revealing a contrasting marquee wall behind it, scroll-linked not time-based.
- Hide one clickable easter egg in the footer (whirrls' lamp recolors the arc per click); keep foreground type a constant contrast-safe color across all variants.
- Stack testimonials as a rotated card deck (2-4deg offsets) and shuffle with a ~400ms rotate+translate ease-out on arrow click.
- Animate proof-of-format cards in loops: version numbers ticking upward (~800ms/step), a vector outline being resized by its bezier handles.
- Build the landing page out of the asset pack it sells - every decoration doubles as a product demo.
