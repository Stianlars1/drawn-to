## Why saved
Saved without comment; the post text is the only note. It earns a place for two things the corpus lacked. First, a light widget SET measured as a set: 13 cards, 12 different heights, one width, one radius, one padding, one accent - the proof that "three radius tiers, never one" (C8) means tiers across nesting depth, not variety across siblings; the 24 / 8 / pill ladder here is the light-mode answer to yurygok-2089981's card 20-24 / inner 4-8 / pill. Second, the inspector-as-sibling device: F4 Blueprint garnish that documents a component without drawing over it, with real timing for open (150 / 333 ms) and switch (117 ms out, 100 + 300 ms in). Positive exemplar for the card system and the demo grammar; partial as a page (it is a catalogue, not a composition) and it ships a few generated-UI tells listed below. It is also the corpus's first model-generated reference, useful as a calibration: what a strong generator gets right (data fidelity, restraint, one accent) and where it still leaks.

## Extractable rules
- Card token set: width 352, padding 24, radius 24, `background #fff`, `border 1px solid #e8e8e8`, `box-shadow 0 6px 16px -6px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`; heights content-driven, never equalised across a row.
- Grid: 3 columns on a 400 px pitch (48 gap), rows top-aligned, row gap 80 that holds a 13 px #6f6f6f label with its baseline 16 px above the next card.
- Radius ladder 24 / 8 / pill: 24 on the card, 8 on every inner image or thumbnail, full pill or circle on every control (32-40 px tall). Nothing at 12 or 16.
- Inner content grid: content width = card - 48; mosaics on 8 px gaps (2 x 148 + 8 = 304; tall tile = 2 x 116 + 8 = 240).
- Grey ramp for ink: #161616 titles, #6b6b6b body, #717171 meta; weights 400-500 only; sizes 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 16, display 32 / 40 / 48 at weight 400 with the unit demoted to 12-14 px #a2a3a5.
- Exactly one accent (#2aa4d4) used only for state: the unread dot, the progress line, the active bar. Status chips get their own tint pair (#fcf4ab fill, #4c4c06 text) and nothing else does.
- Invert exactly one card and let the inversion mean something (the running session). Dark card: #1c2232 base, lighter corner #212c40, ring ticks at 6 deg pitch, done arc #fff, remaining arc at 35% white.
- Put text on a colour field in the field's own hue (#c6e5fa on #90b5d0), not pure white.
- Switch type voice once per set, for the one card whose content is prose (26 px roman serif, #212121).
- Zoom-to-isolate: fade siblings to 0 in 170 ms, scale the card 1.38x to centre on a critically-damped spring (90% at 150-200 ms, settle 350-400 ms, no overshoot); return 90% in 83-100 ms.
- Card switch: out = opacity 1 -> 0 and scale 1 -> 0.90 in ~120 ms; in = opacity 0 -> 1 in 100 ms while scale 0.92 -> 1 eases out over 300 ms; start the incoming 1-2 frames after the outgoing.
- Inspector plate: a sibling the same size and radius as the card, 8 px lavender grid, padding zone tinted and dash-outlined, mono readouts 48 px to its right; open by sliding the card left half the (plate + 48) width over 333 ms, fade the plate in alongside, bring the readouts in 100 ms later at their final value.
- Make the readouts live (measure the DOM) - the demo's credibility is that RADIUS 24 PX is read, not typed.

## Limitations and transfer risks
- Nike card shows both a pause glyph and a play glyph side by side - one control, two states drawn at once.
- Four label / title duplicates ("Flight" over "Flight", "Inbox" over "Inbox", "Quick note", "Activity") - the catalogue label repeats the card's own title. Fine in an index, eyebrow spray on a product page.
- News thumbnails 1 and 2 are abstract gradient blobs (#ec821e orange, #2d6bb8 blue) - placeholder imagery next to a real photo in row 3.
- "News" label over a card titled "New stories" - a wording drift between index and content.
- Two card-scale gradients (Weather 352 x 212, Focus 352 x 304) with no visible grain or process (C9) - grain itself is not auditable from these JPEG frames, so this is a suspicion, not a measurement.

## Not measurable from these frames
Font families (only cap heights and stroke character), the exact box-shadow string, inner tile radius on the mood/shader mosaics (~8 by eye), gradient grain, the easing function beyond its sampled curve, hover states (the recording never hovers), and the true Flight height (grid reads 224, the single readout frame says 225).
