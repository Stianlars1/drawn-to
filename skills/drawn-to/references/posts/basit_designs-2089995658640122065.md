# Light brand boards with halftone gradient slabs

## What we see
Two static boards for a fictional form-builder brand "NORMA": a wide hero/header board and a triptych of portrait feature cards. Everything sits on near-white gray panels matted inside white frames; color arrives exclusively as rectangular slabs of one signature gradient (pale pink → magenta → red-orange → blue-violet), treated with print artifacts — black halftone dots, 45-degree sheen stripes, dot-grid paper texture. Tweet text: "Some visual exploration for the web-branding design project."

## Layout & composition
- Hero board: ~2.23:1 landscape (measured 2234x1000 px in source; call it 1440x645). Grid is a hard 2-column split: left ~56% dotted-paper panel with the wordmark, right ~44% clean white panel with a 4-line paragraph top-right.
- A full-width gradient band occupies the bottom ~41% of the hero (y 57%→98%). Its right ~43% is overlaid with a black halftone dot screen, and a solid blue-violet block (#765cd9) is butted into the top-right of the band as a color counterweight.
- Every board is matted: content panel inset ~12px inside a white card, with a ~1px hairline border (#ececec) around the card. No shadows detected — flat print-mount framing.
- Feature triptych: 3 equal portrait cards, ~3:4 ratio (measured 760x1000 px each), gap ~13px at design scale. Inner content padding ~28-32px.
- Radii: 0 everywhere on panels, bands, and cards — strictly sharp rectangles. The only rounding lives inside the illustration (arrow glyph corners, ~48px).
- Alignment: all text left-aligned, top-left anchored; big display lines bottom-left anchored inside the gradient slabs.

## Card anatomy
- Card 1 (AI Voice Forms): top ~53% gray #f4f4f4 — title + 2-line description at top, then deliberate empty space. Bottom ~47% gradient slab (violet → orange → hot pink → pale pink, top to bottom) containing a small stat lockup ("5 min → 60" + caption at ~70% opacity) and a 2-line display headline pinned to the bottom.
- Card 2 (Connected Workflow): inverted — gradient occupies top ~62% (pale pink top → magenta → red-orange → violet bottom, i.e. card 1's ramp mirrored) with 45-degree stripe texture and a tiny 2-line white annotation; bottom ~38% is the gray panel with title + description. The middle card flips the scheme so the row reads A-B-A.
- Card 3 (Team Collaboration): title top, large gray void, gradient slab bottom ~28% with a 2-line display headline only.
- Visual-to-text ratio ≈ 60:40; text never sits on a busy area — annotations sit on the calmest part of the gradient.

## Type & color
- One grotesque family throughout (SF Pro / Inter-like). Hierarchy by size and opacity, not by extra weights.
- Wordmark: uppercase, weight ~600, gray #636363 (not black), cap height ~60px at 1440 design width, with a small ® superscript.
- Display lines in gradient slabs: weight 400-500, white, ~48px on hero, ~30-34px on cards, line-height ~1.1, tracking ~-0.01em. Second phrase of the hero headline ("modern teams") dropped to ~55% opacity — hierarchy inside a single sentence.
- Card titles: ~20px, weight 600, #111. Body/description: ~13-14px, weight 400, #555, line-height 1.5. Stat captions on gradient: white at ~70% opacity, ~12px.
- Palette: page #ffffff; panel gray #f4f4f4; dotted paper #f8f8f8 with ~#c9c9c9 specks; ink #111/#636363. Gradient stops: #ffe8fc → #fe93f3 → #ff46d0 → #ff1866 → #ff422f → #825bc8/#765cd9. Halftone dots near-black #141414.
- Contrast strategy: 90% of every board is 2-4% gray deviation from white; 100% of the chroma is compressed into one slab per board. Deep contrast comes from black halftone ink over the hottest gradient zone.

## Graphic language
- Signature gradient: a single multi-stop ramp (pale pink → magenta → crimson → red-orange → blue-violet) reused in every board at different orientations and crops — horizontal on the hero, vertical and mirrored on the cards. It behaves like a brand asset, not a decoration.
- Halftone screen: black square-grid dots, pitch ~8px, dot ~3px (~30% coverage), no grid rotation, clipped to exact rectangles over the gradient — converts screen color into "printed ink".
- Sheen stripes: 45-degree soft-edged diagonal bands, ~60px wide, ~6-10% white alpha, running bottom-left to top-right over the gradient slabs.
- Paper texture: fine dark speck grid (~1px dots, ~14px pitch) on the wordmark panel only — one panel gets tactility, the rest stay clean.
- Ghost watermark: a giant rounded-corner arrow glyph (corner radius ~48px) fills the hero's right panel in near-white (#fff on #f4f4f4, 2-3% luminance shift), with the band's red/violet gradient bleeding softly up into it. Visible only on second look; cropped by the panel edges.
- No photos, no icons, no UI screenshots — the entire visual system is: gray field + gradient slab + print artifact.

## Why saved
Owner note: "something presentable in light tones with strong deep contrast images/shaders. Very pretty and cool; owner wants to be able to GENERATE this kind of visual for typical sections. Liked: how something can look so simple yet powerful."

Verified. The power-from-simplicity is engineered, not accidental: (1) chroma rationing — boards are 90% near-white gray, so one gradient slab per board carries all the energy; (2) the gradient is one fixed ramp reused at different crops/orientations, which makes three different cards read as one system; (3) print-craft overlays (halftone at exact pitch, 45-degree sheen stripes, paper specks, white mats + hairline borders) give digital gradients physical authority — this is the layer a generic AI page always misses; (4) figure-ground discipline — the ghost arrow at 2-3% contrast and the 55%-opacity headline tail reward a second look without adding elements; (5) zero radii, zero shadows, strict rectangular slabs — the restraint is what lets the color feel "deep" rather than loud.

## Extractable rules
- Keep 85-90% of the section near-white (#fff page, #f4f4f4 panels); confine all chroma to one sharp rectangular slab covering 28-47% of the container.
- Mat every content panel: inset it 10-14px inside a white card with a 1px #ececec border, 0 radius, no shadow.
- Define one brand gradient ramp (#ffe8fc → #ff46d0 → #ff1866 → #ff422f → #7a5ad0) and only rotate/mirror/crop it — never invent per-section gradients.
- Overlay black halftone dots (8px pitch, 3px dot, square grid) clipped to a precise rectangle over the hottest gradient zone for "printed ink" depth.
- Add 45-degree soft diagonal stripes, ~60px wide at 6-10% white alpha, over gradient slabs for sheen.
- Texture at most one gray panel per board with a ~14px-pitch 1px speck grid; leave the rest flat.
- Hide one oversized rounded glyph (radius ~48px) as a near-white watermark (2-3% luminance offset) with gradient bleeding into it from an adjacent slab.
- Set display type white 400-500 weight directly on the gradient, bottom-left anchored; drop trailing phrases to ~55% opacity for in-line hierarchy.
- Use one grotesque family: titles 20px/600 #111, body 13-14px/400 #555 at 1.5 line-height; render the wordmark in #636363, not black.
- In a 3-card row, mirror the middle card's gradient placement (top vs bottom) so the row reads A-B-A.
- Reserve 40-55% of each card as empty gray space between the title block and the color slab.
- Put small annotations only on the calmest (palest) region of a gradient, white at 70-100% opacity, ~12px.
