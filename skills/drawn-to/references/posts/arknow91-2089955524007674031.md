# Gooey PlayStation speed dial, dark mode

## What we see
A single "+" FAB on a charcoal canvas that fissions — via metaball physics — into a PlayStation-style button diamond: square (top), circle (left), triangle (right), with the + rotating 45° into the × hub. Hovering any satellite liquid-merges it with the hub into one gooey capsule whose seam lights up with that glyph's canonical PlayStation accent (green triangle, pink square, red circle). The whole surface continuously deforms toward the cursor, even at ~150 px distance, then everything collapses back into the + on close. 16.55 s clip, 2160×2160 @ 60fps, perfect loop.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: solid #1f2326, no texture, no vignette. Component dead-center, occupying only ~30% of frame width — extreme negative space.
- Buttons (in an 800 px reference frame): satellites ~52 px diameter, hub ~56 px. Fill is the SAME color as the background (#1f2326) — buttons are defined solely by a 1 px ring at #2e3235 (~+6% lightness) and a faint inner top-sheen (#24282b).
- Radial layout: satellites at ~92–105 px center-to-center from the hub; square at 12 o'clock, circle at ~10 o'clock, triangle at ~2 o'clock, hub bottom-center. A 3-over-1 arc, not a full ring.
- No shadows, no elevation — depth comes entirely from the glow accents and the liquid silhouette. Radius: everything is a circle or a metaball union of circles.

## Card anatomy
Not cards. Per-node anatomy: 52 px circle, ~16–18 px line-icon glyph at ~1.5–2 px stroke in #e1e5e6→#fbffff. Idle: monochrome. Hover: glyph stroke recolors to its accent, gains an outer bloom (~8–12 px, ~40% opacity), and the merged blob's concave seam picks up a 2 px gradient stroke in the same accent that fades to transparent along the silhouette.

## Type & color
No type. Palette is disciplined:
- Ground + button fill: #1f2326 (blue-tinted gunmetal)
- Hairline ring: #2e3235; inner sheen #24282b
- Icons: #e1e5e6–#fbffff
- Accents (only on interaction, matching PlayStation glyph canon): triangle green #50de88, square pink/magenta #d95dbb, circle red #bf4e6c (X/blue never triggered in clip)
Contrast strategy: near-invisible resting state (~1.2:1 ring on bg) so 100% of visual energy is reserved for the interaction color. Accents appear as emissive light, not as fills.

## Graphic language
Metaball/gooey rendering — classic gaussian-blur + alpha-contrast (SVG feGaussianBlur stdDeviation ≈ 8–12 with feColorMatrix alpha ~18/-7, or a canvas/SDF equivalent). The construction detail that elevates it: the accent is drawn as a gradient stroke that lives on the metaball SEAM — the concave bridge between two merging blobs — brightest at the pinch point and fading to transparent, so the color reads as liquid surface tension, not as a border. At f_073 the hub bridges square AND triangle simultaneously and each seam carries its own accent (pink left, green right) blending across one continuous surface. The cultural layer: four glyphs + four canonical colors = DualShock face buttons, delivered without a single logo ("certain people will feel something 🎮").

## Why saved
No owner note. Tweet text: "gooey speed dial - dark / certain people will feel something looking at this 🎮". Verified: this is exactly the owner's taste — a component that SHOWS its physics instead of describing anything. The craft moves a generic build would miss: (1) accent color rendered as a gradient stroke on the metaball seam instead of a glow slapped on the whole blob — the color behaves like light caught in surface tension; (2) buttons filled with the background color so the resting state is nearly invisible and the interaction owns all contrast; (3) cursor attraction active BEFORE hover, at distance, with the blob stretching past its own geometry toward the pointer; (4) dual-color seams when bridging two satellites at once; (5) ordered choreography on open/close (rotate icon → fission; rotate back → absorb → squash) rather than one simultaneous tween; (6) the wordless PlayStation homage carried purely by glyph + canonical color.

## Extractable rules
- Fill interactive nodes with the exact background color (#1f2326); define them only by a 1 px ring ~6% lighter (#2e3235).
- Reserve all saturation for interaction states; resting UI stays under ~1.3:1 ring contrast.
- Render gooey merges with blur ~10 px + alpha contrast (feColorMatrix ~18/-7); node diameter ~52 px, satellite orbit ~96–105 px.
- Paint hover accents as 2 px gradient strokes along the metaball seam, brightest at the pinch, fading to transparent — never as full-blob glows.
- Recolor the hovered glyph to its accent (+8–12 px bloom at ~40%) ~200 ms after the surfaces merge, not simultaneously.
- Start cursor attraction at ~150–200 px distance; deform the blob 10–15% of its radius toward the pointer with ~100–150 ms spring lag.
- Sequence the open: rotate + → × (45°, ~150 ms), then fission satellites with visible necks, settle with 4–6 px overshoot; total 450–550 ms.
- Sequence the close in reverse order: icon un-rotates first, then satellites absorb, land as a squashed oval, relax to circle; total 350–450 ms.
- Let released blobs snap back elastically (~250 ms) with the accent color decaying ~300 ms after separation.
- Allow multi-bridge states: adjacent seams keep their own accent hues on one continuous surface.
- Use canonical color-coding (PS glyph colors: #50de88 triangle, #d95dbb square, #bf4e6c circle) to smuggle brand nostalgia without logos.
- Loop demos frame-perfectly: end state must equal the first frame.
