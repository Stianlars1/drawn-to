# Analytics hero with living mist background

## What we see
A 6.7s concept clip (3126×2160, 60fps) of a landing hero for "Forbize", a real-time analytics product. A centered serif headline, frosted pill nav, email-capture row, and an iPhone mockup flanked by floating dark stat cards, all sitting on a heavily blurred cloud/fog video that drifts continuously from silver (top-right) through steel teal to indigo/near-black (bottom). Tweet text: "Something new is cooking. This is the first concept we've put together for our new design project. Would you take it further or keep it here?"

## Layout & composition
- Page canvas is inset in an off-white matte frame #F3F7F8, ~48px padding at 1440w, canvas corner radius ~24px.
- Single centered column, content max-width ~62% of canvas; vertical rhythm: nav → 90px → headline (2 lines) → 24px → subcopy (2 lines) → 40px → input+button row → 40px → device cluster cut by the fold.
- Nav: full-round frosted pill, ~67% canvas width, height ~48px, centered, ~36px from top. Logo left, 6 links center-left (~28px gaps), "Sign in" + black pill CTA right.
- Device cluster: phone (~306px wide) dead-center, bottom-cropped by the fold; left card stack (Organic Reach pill card ~200×76, 89% stat card ~150×110) and right stack (89% card ~137×85, Average Order card ~240×124) arranged asymmetrically at differing baselines - a loose orbit, not a grid.
- Card radii ~14–16px; phone inner cards ~16px; no visible borders, no drop shadows anywhere - separation comes entirely from luminance against the mist.
- CTA row: input pill ~360×44 + white button pill ~128×44, ~12px gap.

## Card anatomy
- Stat cards are text-only, no visual area: display-serif numeral first (≈40px, "89%^", "$3,583.67 USD"), then a 14px gray sans label below ("Total active users", "Average Order"); Average Order adds a right-aligned "+3.67^" delta and a dual sparkline (green ~#3FBF77 over red ~#D14B3C, ~1.5px strokes, no fill) bleeding to card edges.
- Organic Reach card: horizontal pill card - 56px lavender rounded-square icon (#C0A0EC) with white node-graph glyph, serif title, 12px sans metric line "56.50%^ · $27,380", caret glyph right.
- Phone screen: top third is the same mist (transparent "window" screen) with serif "Data Center 24%^" + 13px sans "Growth from Yesterday"; below, a 2-col grid of pure-black cards (#000) titled in 15px sans with ↗ arrows top-right, big bold sans numerals (75.8K / 1.24M, ~44px) bottom-left; a third "Ads Campaign (Live)" card is cropped by the fold.
- Fill values: dark stat cards ≈ rgba(10,14,18,0.85) (sampled #0D1116–#101317 over mist); input pill ≈ rgba(255,255,255,0.10) over teal; nav pill ≈ rgba(255,255,255,0.65)+blur.

## Type & color
- Display serif (Times/PP-Editorial-like, high contrast, old-style feel): headline ~60px/1.1 white with the phrase "real-time" in italic and a soft white outer glow (~0 0 24–40px rgba(255,255,255,0.3)); the same serif is reused for all hero metrics and card titles - serif carries every number, sans carries every label.
- Sans (Inter/SF-like): subcopy ~16px/1.5 in #C9D2D6; card labels 13–14px #98A2A8; nav links 14–15px near-black; buttons 15px semibold.
- The caret "^" is used as the up-delta glyph everywhere instead of an arrow icon - typographic, not iconographic.
- Palette: matte #F3F7F8, silver sky #CCD4D6, steel teal #125D7D / #13547A, indigo-violet #18154A, near-black #0A0A0A, card fill #0D1116, accent lavender #C0A0EC, sparkline green #3FBF77 / red #D14B3C, text white #FFFFFF.
- Contrast strategy: a diagonal light→dark field; the light frosted nav lives in the light zone, dark cards live in the dark zones, and white serif floats mid-field with glow to stay legible over the shifting mid-tones.

## Graphic language
No illustration, no 3D, no icons beyond one glyph - the entire visual identity is a single photographic/volumetric asset: a heavily blurred cloud/fog video used as environment. The killer construction detail: the phone screen shows the same mist as the page, so the device reads as a pane of glass in the scene rather than a pasted screenshot, and the dashboard cards float on weather. UI fragments (stat cards, phone dashboard) do the product storytelling; the caret-as-delta and serif numerals give it an editorial, almost financial-print register.

## Motion timeline
- t=0.0s: page is already fully composed - zero entrance choreography, no fades, no staggers.
- t=0.0–6.7s: one continuous ambient evolution of the mist. Measured on background strips: mean luminance delta ≈1.4/255 per 167ms between adjacent samples, accumulating to ≈5.4/255 over the full clip - slow, steady, roughly linear.
- Cross-correlation between frames shows (0,0) net translation: the fog does not pan; it morphs in place (density/opacity redistribution), like a defocused cloud timelapse at ~0.3–0.5× speed.
- The phone-screen mist evolves in sync with the page background (same source texture).
- No loop closure within the clip (frame 40 ≠ frame 1); presumably a long seamless loop. Easing: none/linear - constant ambient velocity. All type, cards, sparklines, and numbers are static throughout.

## Why saved
No owner note. What earns the save: this is ambient-first hero design - instead of entrance staggers, the single animated element is the environment itself, moving at ~1 luminance level per 150ms, motion you feel rather than watch. The craft moves a generic AI page would miss: (1) the mist continuing through the phone screen, turning the mockup into a window; (2) display serif reserved for numerals and product nouns with sans strictly for labels - an editorial inversion of the usual pairing; (3) the "^" caret as a typographic delta instead of an arrow icon; (4) zero borders/shadows - every surface separated purely by luminance against the gradient field; (5) the light frosted nav deliberately parked in the light corner of the diagonal light→dark field. Sits exactly at the owner's intersection of airy premium light UI and editorial dark UI.

## Extractable rules
- Build hero atmosphere from a blurred cloud video that morphs in place (zero pan); keep drift to ~1/255 luminance per 150ms - ambient, not animated.
- Continue the exact background texture inside device-mockup screens so hardware reads as glass in the scene, not a screenshot.
- Skip all entrance choreography when the background is alive; ship the page fully composed at t=0.
- Set headlines in a high-contrast display serif ~60px/1.1, italicize only the key phrase, add a white outer glow 0 0 24px rgba(255,255,255,0.3) for legibility over shifting mid-tones.
- Use the display serif for every metric and card title; use 13–14px sans #98A2A8 for every label - never mix roles.
- Type the up-delta as a caret "^" appended to the numeral instead of an arrow icon.
- Float dark cards as rgba(10,14,18,0.85), radius 14–16px, no border, no shadow; let luminance contrast against the field do the separation.
- Make the nav a full-round frosted pill (rgba(255,255,255,0.65) + ~20px blur, height ~48px) and place it in the lightest zone of the gradient.
- Compose email capture as dark translucent pill input rgba(255,255,255,0.10) + solid white pill button, both 44–48px tall, 12px gap.
- Frame the whole canvas in an off-white matte #F3F7F8 with ~48px padding and 24px radius.
- Run one diagonal color field: #CCD4D6 top-right → #135C7C mid → #18154A bottom-left → #0A0A0A bottom-right; position light UI in light zones, dark UI in dark zones.
- Limit hue accents to one: a lavender #C0A0EC icon tile; keep sparklines 1.5px unfilled green/red.
