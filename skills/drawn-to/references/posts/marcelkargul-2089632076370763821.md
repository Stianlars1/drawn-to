# Glowing underline tab switcher, dark UI

## What we see
A single component demo on a near-black canvas: two pill-shaped tabs, "Emails" (filled inbox icon) and "Attachments" (outline paperclip icon), sitting above an etched hairline divider. When a tab activates, a bright white underline bar grows from its center along the divider, the tab text ignites from gray to white, the pill picks up a bottom-lit gradient, and a soft cone of light spills through the divider into the content panel below - where a giant, heavily blurred watermark of the active tab's icon also fades in. The loop shows Emails activating, deactivating, then Attachments, then back to idle.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: 800×720 measured frame (source video 2400×2160, 3x), solid background #0F0F0F, no texture or grain.
- Tabs row horizontally centered, vertically at ~48% height; component is the entire composition (product-shot framing with generous negative space).
- Tab pills: Emails ~186×68px, Attachments ~284×68px (width hugs content), corner radius ~14px, inner padding ~24px horizontal, icon-to-label gap ~14px.
- Gap between the two tabs: ~28px.
- Divider: full-bleed etched groove at y≈402 - 2px dark line #0A0A0A with a 1–2px lighter edge #161616 beneath it (inset/embossed hairline, not a flat border), on #0F0F0F ground.
- Gap between pill bottom edge and divider/underline: ~18px.
- Active underline: ~3px thick, rounded caps, sits exactly on the divider and spans exactly the tab's width (190px under Emails, 281px under Attachments) - indicator width == trigger width, center-aligned.
- No shadows on the pills themselves; all depth comes from emitted light, not drop shadows.

## Card anatomy
Not cards - a single tab component. Per-tab anatomy: icon (20px, leading) + label, inside a pill whose resting fill is barely distinguishable from the ground (#151515 on #0F0F0F, ~2% lift) with a ~1px faintly lighter border. Active state adds three layers below the pill: the underline bar, its bloom, and the content-panel light cone.

## Type & color
- Label: ~26px at measured scale (~17px at 1x), medium weight, geometric-humanist sans (Inter/Geist/SF class), normal tracking, sentence case.
- Inactive label + icon: #8A8A8A (p95 sample #868686). Active label + icon: #F2F2F2 core reading as #FFFFFF where the bloom overlays.
- Palette: background #0F0F0F; resting pill #151515; pill border ~#1F1F1F; divider groove #0A0A0A / edge #161616; active pill gradient #1A1A1A (top) → ~#4E4E4E at the bottom edge nearest the underline; underline core #FFFFFF.
- Contrast strategy: monochrome; state is communicated entirely by luminance, not hue. Inactive sits at ~35% luminance contrast, active jumps to full white - a ~3-stop range on one axis.

## Graphic language
Light-as-state-metaphor. The underline is treated as a physical light source, and every other visual change is derived from it: the pill's bottom-lit gradient reads as the bar illuminating the tab from below; the divider "lets light through" into the panel as a radial cone (~400px wide, peaking ~#1F above ground, fading over ~250px); the label's white is hot in the bloom zone. The subtlest move: a giant (~350px) heavily gaussian-blurred copy of the active tab's icon sits in the content panel at ~3–4% opacity - an empty-state watermark that echoes the selection, only really visible as the glow fades. Icon set mixes fill (inbox) and 2px outline (paperclip) but keeps identical optical weight.

## Motion timeline
Loop 5.97s, 30fps source; times from frame N at t=(N−1)×0.166s. Both grow and shrink are center-anchored scaleX; nothing translates.
- 0.00–0.30 idle: both tabs resting, groove divider unbroken.
- 0.30 Emails activation begins: ~9px underline nub appears at tab center; the below-divider glow blob is already blooming (glow leads, not lags).
- 0.30–1.00 underline grows 9→81→147→177→190px (per-166ms samples): fast ramp then long decelerating tail - ease-out, ~cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), ~650ms, no overshoot. In parallel (~300ms): label #8A8A8A→#FFF, pill gains bottom-lit gradient, light cone + icon watermark fade in below. All properties on one clock, no stagger.
- 1.00–2.00 hold: fully static - glow-region mean luminance flat at 31.3–31.4 across 7 frames; no breathing/pulsing.
- 2.00–2.49 deactivation: underline shrinks 190→143→52→0, accelerating ease-in, ~400ms (exit faster than enter); glow, text, and gradient fade in sync; the blurred icon watermark lingers a beat longer than the glow.
- 2.49–2.98 idle gap ~500ms with the component fully at rest before the next tab fires.
- 2.98–3.81 Attachments activation: 0→27→117→224→273→281px, ~700ms, softer start (reads ease-in-out / critically damped spring); same parallel choreography.
- 3.81–4.64 hold ~830ms.
- 4.64–5.30 deactivation: 279→245→140→10→0, ~500ms ease-in.
- 5.30–5.97 idle tail; frame 36 matches frame 1 - seamless loop.

## Why saved
No owner note; tweet text is just "tabs animation." What earns the save: this is the "SHOW the feature" school applied to a primitive - a tab indicator rebuilt as a coherent lighting system. The craft moves a generic implementation would miss: (1) the underline is a light source and four secondary effects are all consistent consequences of it - pill under-lit gradient, divider light-bleed cone, hot label, blurred icon watermark below; (2) indicator width tracks trigger width exactly and grows from center with a ~650ms ease-out while the exit is a faster ~400ms ease-in - asymmetric timing that generic code (equal-duration ease-in-out both ways) never does; (3) the hold state is measurably dead still - confidence, no idle pulsing; (4) state is pure luminance on a monochrome palette, in editorial dark-UI (Linear-like) territory; (5) the divider is an etched groove (dark line + lower highlight), not a flat 1px border. It is a bookmarkable recipe for making selection feel physical in dark UI.

## Extractable rules
- Set dark-UI ground at #0F0F0F and resting surfaces at #151515 - a ~2% lift, not a visible card.
- Draw dividers as etched grooves: 1px #0A0A0A line plus 1px #161616 highlight below, never a flat gray border.
- Make the active-tab indicator exactly the trigger's width and center-align it; grow/shrink with transform: scaleX from center.
- Enter at ~650ms ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)); exit at ~400ms ease-in - exits faster than entrances.
- Treat the indicator as a light source: add a bottom-up gradient inside the active tab (to ~#4E4E4E at the near edge) and a radial glow cone bleeding past the divider into content.
- Encode selection with luminance only: inactive text #8A8A8A → active #FFFFFF; no hue change.
- Run all state properties (bar width, text color, pill gradient, glow) on one synchronized clock - no stagger inside a single state change.
- Keep the hold state perfectly static; no breathing or idle pulse on the glow.
- Echo the selection in the content area with a ~350px gaussian-blurred icon watermark at 3–4% opacity.
- Give the underline a hard 3px #FFF core with rounded caps plus a separate wide soft bloom (two layers, not one big blur).
- Leave ~500ms of full rest between consecutive state changes in a demo loop, and make the last frame equal the first.
- Match optical weight when mixing filled and outlined icons (2px strokes against solid glyphs of equal mass).
