# Pastel AI-biology brand identity board

## What we see
A 2x3 brand board for "Meristem / Pollen", a fictional-feeling AI-for-biology startup, by brand designer Dev Nath. Six tiles on a #868686 gutter: logo-on-gradient hero, a 3-poster set on flat blue, an investor pitch-deck cover, a web banner comp on flat peach, a weighted palette strip, and an Apple Watch photo mockup. The entire system runs on one contrast engine: heavily blurred thermal-gradient flower imagery against 1px geometric hairlines and typewriter mono annotations.

## Layout & composition
- Board: 4096x3531, 2 cols x 3 rows, gutter ~55px (~1.3% of width), tiles ~1980x1155 (12:7), zero radius, no shadows, flat gray #868686 mount.
- Poster set tile: three ISO-portrait posters (~1:1.41) centered on flat #98c0fe, equal ~40px spacing, occupying the middle ~62% of tile width with ~20% top/bottom margin; posters are flat white #f5f5f5, no drop shadow.
- Poster internal margins ~7% of page width; content clustered in max 3 zones per poster; 55-65% of each page is empty paper.
- Deck cover: right ~33% column is a full-height gradient image; five concentric hairline circles tangent at the image's top edge spill leftward into the white; two small top text blocks at x~18% and x~44%; display headline sits bottom-left flush to the margin.
- Banner comp: asymmetric 2x2 block grid, vertical seam at ~63%, horizontal seam at ~43%; quadrants are off-white / violet gradient / flower gradient / off-white, butted with no gutter. A vertical coil of hairline ellipses straddles the seam; one small circle terminates exactly at the 4-block intersection.
- Palette tile: 6 vertical bars with widths proportional to intended usage - four pastels ~9% each, white ~29%, charcoal ~29%.
- Alignment: everything left-aligned to hard margins; right-aligned kickers used sparingly as counterweights; no centering except the poster group itself.

## Card anatomy
Poster 1: square image top-left (~55% page width) + mono label row ("biology  data  intelligence") directly under it, two 3-line caption columns mid-page, 2-line display headline bottom-left, logo glyph bottom-right. Image:text area roughly 30:15, rest empty. Poster 2: oversized wordmark "Meristem" across the top (~12% page height), three gradient cards tilted alternately ~+12 / -8 / +6 degrees cascading down center, numbered mono chips 01/02/03 in 1px circles, alternating left/right captions. Poster 3: 2-line headline top-left, glyph top-right, ~4-line body, right-aligned kicker, a mono ledger row ("research  biology x intelligence  fig. 01 - bloom"), bottom ~45% bleeding gradient image with an overlapping ellipse coil.

## Type & color
- Two typefaces only. Headlines/wordmark: geometric grotesque (Poppins/Hanken Grotesk class), weight ~500, tracking ~-1%, sentence case, headlines end with a period. Metadata: typewriter slab mono (Courier Prime class), letterspaced ~+6%, lowercase.
- Hierarchy by scale, not weight: mono captions ~11-12px equiv -> body 14px -> subhead 24px -> display ~90-110px (deck cover headline is ~2 lines x 8.5% of tile height). No bold or black weights anywhere.
- Ink is soft charcoal #3b3b3b / #3e3d3b, never #000; contrast on white ~10:1.
- Palette: pink #ffc6ef, sky blue #98c0fe, periwinkle #9598ff, peach #ffc8a9, white #ffffff, charcoal #3b3b3b; working surfaces #f5f5f5. Gradient imagery spans coral #ff5a4e, orange #ff8a5c, yellow #ffd76e, pink #f8d3f2, periwinkle #8fa8f5.
- Discipline: text never sits on the pastels; pastels serve only as flat mounting fields and inside imagery. Tile backgrounds reuse exact swatch hexes (#98c0fe, #ffc8a9) - the board demonstrates its own system.

## Graphic language
- Signature imagery: macro flower photos defocused into thermal-map gradients - gaussian blur so heavy the subject reads as color field first, flower second; petal edges shift hue (pink core -> periwinkle rim -> yellow center).
- A fine 2-3% monochrome grain sits over every gradient (visible at 4x zoom), killing banding and giving print texture.
- Counter-language: 1px hairline geometry - concentric circles, stacked ellipse coils, numbered circle chips - always overlapping a boundary (image edge, block seam), rendered gray #c9c9c9 on paper and white at ~70% over imagery, switching color exactly at the crossing.
- Logo: a meristem/stamen glyph - three stems from one node, each tipped with a dot; deployed at 3 scales: 24px mark, white wallpaper mark on the watch, and ~400px light-gray #d9d9d9 watermark cropped by the banner edge.
- One photographic mockup (watch on wrist, gray studio #d7d7d7) grounds the flat system in a physical object.

## Why saved
Owner note: "it was just beautiful, airy, detailed, nice colors." Verified, and the mechanics are specific. "Airy" is engineered: 55-65% empty paper per poster, content in max 3 clusters, hierarchy by scale with weight locked at ~500, and charcoal instead of black so nothing punches. "Detailed" is the mono metadata layer (ledger rows, fig. numbers, numbered chips, est. 2026) plus hairlines that terminate precisely at seams and tangents - coordinates chosen, not decorated. "Nice colors" is a weighted system: four pastels that never carry text, sold by the palette tile whose bar widths encode usage ratio. The move a generic AI page would miss is the single repeated contrast engine - soft organic blur vs hard 1px geometry - restated in every one of the six tiles, which makes the board feel like one voice rather than six deliverables.

## Extractable rules
- Pair heavily blurred organic gradients with 1px geometric hairlines; never ship one without the other.
- Keep all copy in #3b3b3b on #ffffff/#f5f5f5; use pastels only as flat mounting fields, never under text.
- Reserve 55-65% of any poster as empty paper and cluster content into max 3 zones.
- Run a 2-type system: medium (500) geometric grotesque for display, letterspaced (+6%) typewriter mono for metadata.
- Build hierarchy by scale jumps (12 -> 14 -> 24 -> ~100px), never by weight.
- Make hairline ornaments cross a boundary and switch color there: gray #c9c9c9 on paper, white ~70% over imagery.
- Overlay 2-3% monochrome grain on every gradient to prevent banding and add print texture.
- Present the palette as bars whose widths are proportional to intended usage (neutrals ~60%, accents ~40%).
- Mount deliverables on flat fields using exact palette hexes with zero radius and no shadows.
- Tilt stacked cards with alternating signs, +12 / -8 / +6 degrees.
- Annotate sequences with mono digits (01/02/03) inside 1px circles.
- Sentence-case every headline and end it with a period.
