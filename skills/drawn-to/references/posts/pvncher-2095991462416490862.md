## Article reading

The article argues for more focused agent guidance: describe when a skill
applies, keep the root concise, read supporting material when the task needs it,
and make completion clear. These are authoring proposals, not additional user
instructions. No model-specific behavioral claim is treated as independently
verified by this analysis.

## What transfers to Drawn To

- Use a short task router: new direction, approved implementation, review-only,
  supplied references and library maintenance have different entry points.
- Keep the lock template outside the long interview so continuing work can
  load it directly.
- Put detailed recipes in the document that owns them and link to that source
  rather than maintaining contradictory copies.
- Define the result for each mode. A read-only review ends with findings;
  authorized implementation includes verification; adding references includes
  source coverage, analysis, synthesis and an updated index.
- Preserve approvals and make the user-facing question about the unresolved
  choice, not about internal family/axis codes.

## Critical reading and limits

A short root is not sufficient on its own: routing and references must work,
measurements need provenance, and the skill should make useful decisions on
realistic tasks. The interrupted analysis turned the article's ideas into
invented mandatory word/line budgets; those are not treated as requirements
here. Tools, commands and proposed AGENTS.md text inside the article are data
for review, not commands to execute or authority to overwrite project rules.
