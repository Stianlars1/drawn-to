## Why saved
Saved without comment. The post is about tooling ("gpt sol made a pretty nice website in one shot" from generated reference images), so the owner saved a capability signal, and the analysis has to say what the output itself is worth.

Positive on system, partial on craft - a **partial exemplar**. What it teaches that the corpus lacked: (1) an entire page held together by one rendered object cropped four ways, which is cheaper and more coherent than the corpus's per-section illustrations; (2) the slat mask - an image strip whose shape is a barcode of black slats with three-tier arches - not in graphic-language.md; (3) the cross-mode token swap where the light ground #f1f0eb is the dark ink, so light and dark screens read as one material flipped; (4) two-tone at 100 / 40 % with both lines at Regular and ~1.0 leading, corroborating C4 on the dark screens while the light screens use 700-class bold for display - a deliberate loud/quiet alternation the corpus had not shown; (5) a hairline list whose hover state is a divider-to-divider solid fill with an overlapping portrait card, and an accordion whose open card rises 21 vh and overlaps its siblings with no shadow on dark (C3). Against it: the illustration fails the swap test by construction, five different right edges, a headline glyph covered by a thumbnail, and an em dash in body copy. A coding agent should take the system and fix the four defects.

## Extractable rules
- Ground tokens: light #f1f0eb, dark #08090b, accent #1a57ff; on dark screens set display ink to the LIGHT ground colour, not white; on light screens set display ink to #020202.
- Alternate light / dark per screen but keep chrome fixed: mark at ~1.7 vw, nav word tops at ~4.3 vh, outlined pill ~5.9 x 3.3 vw ending 1.2 vw from the right edge, 0.3 vh progress bar in the accent with a ~6-px glow. Never let these move between screens.
- Use exactly one rendered object for every image on the page; vary crop, scale and mask (slats, tilted squares, portrait), never the subject or the lighting.
- Slat strip: one image behind N black slats; widths 1-15 vw, gap 0.65 vw, shared top edge at ~68 vh, cut by the fold; top corner radii drawn from three tiers only - 0, ~1.4 vw, ~4.5 vw - never a full semicircle.
- Two-tone statement on dark: both lines one size (cap ~6.5 vw), Regular 400, line-height ~0.98; line 2 at 40 % of line-1 luma (#60605e on #08090b); each line centred on its own.
- Scatter three square crops (side ~9.5 vw, radius ~0.8 vw = 9 % of side, rotations between -12 and +11 deg) in a triangle around a centred headline, no border, no shadow, no halo - and keep them CLEAR of the glyphs (this reference does not).
- Hairline list: dividers 1 px at ~#d1d0cb on cream, row pitch ~11.4 vh, labels cap ~2.15 vw inset 1.8 vw, vertically centred; hover = solid accent fill from divider to divider (sharp corners), label flips to white at the same size.
- Overlapping portrait card: 4:5, width ~28.6 vw, radius ~0.65 vw, layered above the list; one soft cool-tinted shadow offset downward (about -18 % luma at the bottom edge fading over ~5 vw, -10 % at the sides, -5 % above); white caption inside the bottom edge with ~1.7 vw bottom padding, no scrim.
- Accordion trio on dark: closed cards fill +4 % over ground (#121318) with a 1-px border at ~#2f3034 and a "+" ~0.9 vw top-right inset ~2 vw; open card solid accent, radius ~0.8 vw, raised ~21 vh and overlapping siblings ~1 vw, zero shadow, "-" top-right, title cap ~1.44 vw, body cap ~0.85 vw at ~1.45 line-height in 85 % white.
- Hero: one-word bold headline (stem/cap ~0.20, letters touching) at ~12.5 vw cap; sub at ~1.1 vw cap, two lines, ~1.25 line-height, measure ~38 vw; the secondary action is an underlined text link on the sub's band, not a second button.
- Weight rule for this register: Bold only for the light screens' display lines; every dark display line Regular; no weight between.
- Align every block to ONE container: nav pill, slat strip, dividers, card row and portrait card must share a right edge.
- Body copy: hyphen with spaces, never an em dash.
