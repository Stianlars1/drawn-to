# Cinematic dark dashboard animation reel

## What we see
A 14 s showreel ("Don't let a static UI hide your product's value") composited as a 2x2 quad grid - four simultaneous macro close-ups of dark SaaS dashboard UI, each quadrant cycling through 3-4 scenes at its own rhythm. Every shot is rendered in extreme 3D perspective (screens tilted 15-35deg, camera slowly dollying/drifting) with shallow depth of field, lens bloom on every luminous element, and film grain. Content covers a sales-pipeline product: KPI cards, forecast fan charts, kanban roadmaps, lead leaderboards, usage/billing panels, and neon segmented metric bars.

## Layout & composition
- Video canvas: 1920x1080 (delivered 1280x720), hard 2x2 quad split with ~0px gutter - the black backgrounds of the four scenes fuse into one dark field; quadrant borders are invisible except where UI slabs cross them.
- Each quadrant is a separate 3D scene: UI plane rotated ~15-35deg on X/Y, perspective distortion strong (near edge ~1.6x the far edge), constant slow camera drift ~5-15 px/s plus occasional dolly-in.
- Depth of field: focal plane on the hero element; foreground/background UI blurred ~4-12px gaussian equivalent.
- In-UI metrics (normalized to a 1440px design): card radius 12-16px, pill/chip radius 999px, inner control radius 8-10px; card padding ~16-20px; list row height ~56-64px; gaps 12-16px.
- Borders: 1px inside-stroke at ~8-12% white alpha on cards; panels separated by fill-step (#0A0A0C page → #131316 card → #1C1C21 nested chip) more than by lines; occasional 1px alpha divider between list rows.
- Screens themselves read as physical devices: outer bezel radius ~24-32px with a specular edge highlight (1px lighter rim catching light), sitting on pure #000 void.

## Card anatomy
- KPI card (TL "Opportunities"): big numeral (~32px semibold) + green delta chip ("↑ +31%") on the same baseline, 11-12px gray caption below ("Growth accelerated by local delivery and B2B requests"); an aurora-blue gradient sweep lives inside the card as ambient decoration.
- Deal card (TR pipeline): value first ("$250k", ~18px semibold), company name 12px gray above/below, footer row = 20px avatar + name + two icon-count chips ("12d", "84") right-aligned.
- Leaderboard row (TR): 32px round avatar, name 13px medium white, email 11px gray beneath, score in a rounded-rect chip right-aligned - green filled chip for 90+ (92, 91), neutral outline chip for lower (84, 64).
- Insight card (TL "Copilot Analysis"): status icon (red circle alert / green check) + 13px semibold title + 11px 3-line gray body; ghost "Generate full report" button top-right.
- Category row (BR storage list): 36px rounded-square icon with saturated glow color (blue/green/orange/pink), 14px bold white label, 11px gray description trailing; visual:text ratio ~1:4.

## Type & color
- Type: geometric sans (Inter-class). Hierarchy: hero numerals 32-48px semibold ("$4.2m", "96.4%", "/ 100 000" at ~56px), section titles 16-20px semibold ("Usage Insights"), card titles 13-14px medium, meta 10-11px at 55-65% white. Tracking normal; numerals tabular.
- Base palette: page #060608-#0B0B0E, cards #131316-#1A1A1E, strokes rgba(255,255,255,0.08-0.12), text #FFFFFF / #9CA3AF / #6B7280.
- Accent system is semantic neon on near-black: ice blue #4D9FFF / cyan #34D0E8, green #22C55E (positive deltas, "Good" status), hot pink/red #FF3B6B-#FF4D8D (alerts, churn), violet #A855F7, orange #F59E0B (Planned badges), magenta #E879F9. Fan-chart trio: green=Optimistic, blue=Base, orange=Conservative.
- Contrast strategy: keep 95% of the frame under ~15% luminance so every accent reads as emissive; each accent gets a matching outer bloom (~8-24px, same hue, ~35% opacity) so color = light source, not paint.

## Graphic language
- Charts as neon hardware: dashed polylines (dash ~6px/gap ~5px) with round caps and a bright "draw head" that blooms like an LED; bars are rounded-rect vessels (outlined, subtle diagonal hatch texture inside) that fill bottom-up with glowing liquid color; segmented meter bars read as equalizer LEDs (~4x10px segments, 2px gap) lighting sequentially.
- Frosted-glass blue bars (TL "Opportunity Landscape") carry inner glow + top-edge specular; a thin red 1.5px trend polyline with diamond vertex markers overlays them for contrast of language (soft glass vs sharp wire).
- Real product fragments, not abstractions: breadcrumbs, ⌘K keycap chips, bell icon with cyan unread dot, tab pills, tooltips with color-dot legends and right-aligned values - the UI itself is the illustration.
- Finishing layer: film grain over everything, anamorphic-ish lens flares at glow hotspots, chromatic softness at frame edges - motion-design post treatment, not raw screen capture.

Within scenes, camera moves are continuous pans/dollies (linear, very slow); UI events are ease-out with 400-800 ms durations.

## Why saved
Owner note: "elsket denne" (loved this). Verified - the reaction is earned by specific craft, not just dark-mode neon:
- Data animates the way the product creates value: a line forks into a forecast fan, a leaderboard re-sorts live with motion blur, a progress chip rides the bar head, counters ride tooltips. Every animation is semantic, none is decorative filler.
- The 2x2 grid is choreographed as a relay: cuts are staggered per quadrant (waves at ~3.5 s, ~6 s, ~8.5 s, ~12.5 s) so the composite frame always has something mid-motion - sustained energy without chaos.
- Glow is treated as physics, not a CSS box-shadow: bloom concentrates at draw heads and fill fronts, flares fire at inflection points, grain and DoF unify all four scenes. A generic AI page would apply uniform glow to whole elements.
- Hierarchy survives the spectacle: 95% of pixels stay near-black at 10-15% luminance; accents are rationed one hue per message (green=good, red=action, violet=retention), so the neon reads as signal.
- The ending resolves the tease: after 13 s of macro fragments, the one zoomed-out flat shot shows the whole product - reel structure with an actual payoff.

## Extractable rules
- Keep the base UI under ~15% luminance (#0A0A0C page, #131316 cards) so accent colors function as light sources.
- Pair every neon accent with a same-hue outer bloom, 8-24px at ~35% opacity, concentrated at the element's animation head, not spread evenly.
- Draw chart lines left→right in 800-1500 ms ease-in-out and pop dot markers only as the draw head passes them.
- Animate forecast uncertainty as a fork: draw one line, flare at the split point, then branch into 3 colored scenario lines drawn simultaneously.
- Ride the value with the motion: attach the % chip to the progress-bar head; count numerals up during the fill, ending together.
- Stagger sibling entrances 80-180 ms (bars, list rows, kanban cards); stagger sections 400-800 ms; never move two hierarchy levels at once.
- Re-sort leaderboards with visible vertical swaps (~400 ms) plus motion blur, and flash the changed score chip green.
- Use fill-steps (page→card→chip, ~+4% luminance each) plus 1px 8-12% white strokes instead of visible dividers.
- Give status its own chip grammar: filled green chip for good scores, outline neutral chip otherwise; radius 999px, 11px medium text.
- For reels, run parallel tracks and stagger scene cuts so at least one region is always mid-animation; end on the only full zoomed-out product shot.
- Shoot UI at 15-35deg perspective with shallow DoF (4-12px blur off the focal plane), slow constant camera drift (5-15 px/s), film grain over everything.
- Reserve one iridescent gradient sweep (blue→violet→amber, ~1 s) for the single hero CTA - nowhere else.
