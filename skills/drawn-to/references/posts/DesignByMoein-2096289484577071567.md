## What we see
A light footer for a fictional product "Tired AI" on a warm cream page. Left: the wordmark, a two-line tagline, and under them a rendered 3D scene - an orange cube-headed character in a brown fedora slumped against a beige beanbag with a laptop on its lap, a potted plant, a stack of three books whose spines read "Better Ideas / Less Work / Happier You", a cream mug with a crescent moon, and a sticky note taped to the wall reading "Good ideas take rest." Right of centre: two link columns (Product: Features, Pricing, Use Cases, Examples, Changelog; Company: About, Blog, Careers, Contact), a vertical hairline, then "Stay in the loop" with a 3-line body, an email field with a black Subscribe button set into its right end, and four gray social glyphs (X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Discord). A full-width hairline, then a copyright line left and Privacy / Terms / Cookies right. Nothing in the UI carries chroma; every coloured pixel belongs to the render. Post text says the footer was built with "Custom Skills + my video tool + Cursor + the client's design.md file" - a lock-file-plus-skill workflow, which is exactly what this skill is.

Over 5 s the character wakes: three alert strokes pop beside its head, it heaves itself upright with its eyes shut, then sits typing with two blinks. The UI never moves.

## Graphic language
- The mascot is the brand: cube head, two square eyes, fedora, human hands. It is rendered soft (matte materials, one warm key light from upper left, contact shadow under the beanbag #ebe4db) and placed ON the page floor, not in a card or a frame.
- Diegetic props carry the copy: the three value props are book spines, the brand voice is a hand-written sticky note, "tired" is a moon on a mug. No caption, no label under the scene.
- Separation ladder is C3 to the letter: 1-2 native px hairlines at 11-25 luma below the page, fill steps of 3-8 luma for floor and field, zero shadows on any UI element. The only shadows are inside the render.
- No icons apart from the four social glyphs; no eyebrow; no pill; a single radius token (~8-9) shared by field and button.

## Extractable rules
1. Footer row on one baseline: wordmark cap ~2.4x the column-header cap, baselines within 4 native px; columns at 176 / 145 pitch, a 1 px vertical rule 57 left of the newsletter column, spanning header cap top to 38 below the last element.
2. Brand column = wordmark + 2-line tagline (same size as links) + the asset under it, asset 4x wider than the text block, allowed to bleed 36 into the outer margin, top edge within ~10 of the tagline baseline.
3. Put the character on the page's floor: a floor band 3-4 luma below the page, its back edge 11 luma below the page fading over ~40 native px, tilted 1-2 deg; one soft mound 5-8 luma below the page behind the figure. No card, no frame, no drop shadow outside the render.
4. Quarantine the mascot's hue: UI ink #010103 / #606060 / #6b6b6b, CTA #202020, hairlines #e5e1de and #e7e6e0 on a #f7f4f0 page. Let the accent enter the UI at most once per site (sibling: one badge #db6643).
5. Subscribe group: field 335 x 42, fill page minus 5 luma, 1 px border page minus 25 luma, radius 8-9; button 126 x 40 in #202020 with an off-white #f5f6f1 label, inset 1 px inside the field's right end, same radius, 25 side padding; placeholder inset 20.
6. Social glyphs bare: ~20 tall, 56 pitch, #6b6a65, no rings; 39 under the field.
7. Bottom band: full-width 2 native px rule at #e5e1de, legal text cap 0.82x link cap and one gray lighter (#6b6b6b), 46 below the rule, 107 of air below the text.
8. Diegetic copy: print value props on props (book spines), the brand voice on a note, the product metaphor as an object; zero captions.
9. One-shot character entrance, three beats: alert pop scale-in ~370 ms (starts before anything else moves) -> pose change ~750 ms ease-in-out with a late peak, eyes shut across the fastest 230 ms -> idle with irregular hand bursts and 1-2 frame blinks ~1.3 s apart. The strokes exit ~350 ms before the pose lands.
10. Never loop a state change: play it once on scroll-into-view, then hold the idle. If a loop is required, author a palindrome instead (0xhammermann-2090 tense rule).
11. Keep the page composed at t = 0: text, links and the form never animate; only the asset does (C12).
12. Export renders at their native fps or render at the delivery fps; a 24 -> 30 pulldown leaves a duplicate every 5th frame and reads as judder on the fastest beat.
13. The form must end on the container edge: right edge of the button = right edge of the rule = right edge of the legal links.

## Limitations and transfer risks
- Container overhang: the subscribe button's right edge (native 1865) passes the container edge (1828) by 28 CSS px while the rule and the legal links stop at the edge. Alignment defect.
- Template link inventory: Product (Features / Pricing / Use Cases / Examples / Changelog) and Company (About / Blog / Careers / Contact) plus four default social platforms is the generic footer set; only the diegetic scene is specific.
- Scene-to-text contact: the sticky-note tape sits 14 native px under the tagline baseline, and after the sit-up the hat brim overlaps the note's lower-left corner; the text block and the render have no gutter.
- Frame pulldown: 24 fps render conformed to 30 fps (duplicate every 5th frame).
- No em dashes, no eyebrow, no triads, no icon-and-blurb cards, no lorem.
