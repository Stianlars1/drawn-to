# Dark ops-dashboard cards with bespoke chart forms

## What we see
Four standalone dark dashboard cards (no tweet text), each one a self-contained "data story" component: a feature-flag panel drawn as an 8-position DIP switch, a region/plan mekko chart, a per-model-tier beeswarm of prompt lengths against a 200K token cap, and an eval-run radar vs baseline. Every card follows the same anatomy - header, custom visualization, big KPI, plain-language insight, data table/checklist, mono status footer with two pill actions. The content is AI-infra themed (feature flags, token caps, opus/sonnet/haiku tiers, eval suites).

## Layout & composition
- Single centered card on a near-black canvas (#101012); card ~900×1086 @1x (measured 1799×2171 in a 2x export), ~46% of canvas width, radius ~21px.
- Card is built as stacked full-width bands, separated by hairline seams AND a stepped surface ramp that gets lighter downward: header/viz ~#1C1C1E → insight ~#28282A → checklist/table ~#303032 → footer ~#343436. Elevation replaces borders.
- Internal padding ~28-32px horizontal, band padding ~24px vertical; label/value rows on a ~44-48px rhythm with 1px #FFFFFF/6-8% row dividers.
- Viz zone occupies ~45% of card height; photo 1 lays a blueprint grid (~40px cells, 1px lines at ~4% white) with "+" registration crosses in the corners - technical-drawing framing.
- Right-aligned values everywhere; two-column label/value alignment is strict across checklist, table, and footer.
- Buttons: two pills bottom-right, ~40-44px tall, fully rounded; secondary #383838 with light text, primary white (#EDEDEF) with near-black text.

## Card anatomy
- Ratio: ~15% header / ~45% visualization / ~15% headline+insight / ~15% table / ~10% footer.
- Header: title 22-24px medium white + "/" + muted breadcrumb ("checkout · prod", "sampled runs"); right side mono-caps meta ("30 DAYS", "24 H", "6 SUITES") + 32px circular "…" button.
- KPI block: big numeral 44-48px white with muted unit word in the same size ("3.9 M eu-west", "18.4 K median", "84.7 /100") sitting directly above or inside the viz band; small mono-caps eyebrow above ("TOKENS PER PROMPT"), right-aligned counter with accent highlight ("38 OVER CAP", "EU-WEST 42%").
- Insight block: sentence-case headline ~19px + 2-line supporting copy ~15px at ~60% white, phrased as consequence, then a repeated accent stat ("19% OF ALL CALLS").
- Table/checklist: mono 14px rows; only meaningful values get accent color (over-cap counts 22/14 in amber; 2 and 0 stay muted), check icons green, pending item amber with dashed-circle spinner glyph.
- Footer: mono-caps provenance line left ("LAST FLIP 6 DAYS AGO", "FINISHED 12 MIN AGO · 4m 08s") + action pills right.

## Type & color
- Two-font system: neo-grotesk sans (Inter/Suisse-like) for titles, headlines, buttons; monospace (Geist/DM Mono-like) for every label, number, axis, and status line. Mono microcaps at ~12-13px with ~0.08em tracking are the card's connective tissue.
- Grays: canvas #101012, surfaces #1C1C1E→#343436, primary text #ECECEE, secondary ~#9A9A9E, tertiary/axes ~#5A5A5E.
- One accent per card, never two: amber #F5A11E (flags card + token-cap card), violet #9D8BF0 with #352C49 tinted fill (region card), lime #A9F04D (eval card), plus green #7ED332 reserved for passed checks. Everything not-the-point is desaturated gray - the accent literally is the finding.
- Contrast strategy: accents used at full saturation only on the critical datum, its label, and its table cell; big numbers stay white so hierarchy is size-then-color.

## Graphic language
Each card invents a chart form that embodies its concept instead of defaulting to bar/line:
- Feature flags = physical 8-slot DIP switch (slots ~22×55px @1x, radius ~4px, gray toggles with two grip lines, bit 6 amber with an up arrow showing the flip direction), annotated with an engineering-style dashed leader line and dot terminus ("---- checkout_v2"), numbered 1-8 beneath.
- Region mix = mekko chart: column width encodes region share, stacked blocks encode plan tier; only the focus block gets stroke+tint+labels, all others are unlabeled gray slabs.
- Prompt length = beeswarm strips per model tier on a deliberately non-linear axis (0 / 20K / 60K / 120K / 200K CAP), amber dashed threshold line, over-cap dots amber.
- Eval = hexagonal radar over a dot-grid, lime polygon with vertex dots vs dashed gray baseline polygon, axis labels with inline values ("Reasoning 88").
Every bespoke form ships its own reading key in mono caps at the panel base: "ONE DOT = ONE SAMPLED PROMPT", "WIDTH = REGION SHARE · STACK = PLAN TIER", "SCALE 50-100" (truncated axis disclosed honestly).

## Why saved
No owner note. Fits the owner's editorial-dark-UI (Linear-like) and design-engineer-detail lanes exactly. What earns the save, beyond generic dark-dashboard aesthetics: (1) chart form invented per data story - the DIP switch and mekko are the feature, not decoration; a generic AI page would render four bar charts. (2) One-accent discipline where color marks only the actionable datum, including inside tables (22 and 14 amber, 2 and 0 gray). (3) Insight copy written as consequence with stakes ("It cannot be reverted from this panel once traffic lands", "A regional outage there takes 19% of billed traffic with it") instead of describing the chart. (4) Self-teaching charts - every bespoke form carries a mono-caps legend explaining its own encoding, plus honesty notes like "SCALE 50-100". (5) Surface-ramp banding instead of borders, and a rigid mono microcap grammar (eyebrow left / counter right, provenance footer) repeated identically across all four cards, proving it's a system.

## Extractable rules
- Build data cards as stacked bands whose surfaces lighten downward (#1C1C1E → #343436); use elevation steps, not borders.
- Allow exactly one accent hue per card; gray out every non-critical datum, including table cells with unremarkable values.
- Pair a grotesk sans (titles/insights) with a monospace (all numbers, labels, axes) at 12-13px caps with 0.08em tracking.
- State the KPI as 44-48px white numeral + same-size muted unit word ("18.4 K median").
- Write the insight as a consequence with stakes, not a chart description; repeat its key number as an accented stat line.
- Invent the chart form from the domain metaphor (switch, mekko, beeswarm, radar) before reaching for bars/lines.
- Print the chart's encoding key in mono caps at the panel base ("ONE DOT = ONE SAMPLED PROMPT").
- Disclose truncated or non-linear axes explicitly ("SCALE 50-100", labeled 0/20K/60K/120K/200K stops).
- Mark thresholds as dashed accent lines and color only the points that cross them.
- Anchor the footer with a mono-caps provenance line ("FINISHED 12 MIN AGO · 4m 08s") opposite two pill buttons: dark secondary + white primary, fully rounded, ~44px.
- Use blueprint grid (~40px, 4% white) with corner registration crosses and dashed leader-line callouts to give diagrams an engineering-drawing register.
- Keep card radius ~20-24px, inner elements ~4-8px, buttons pill - three distinct radius tiers, never one.
