## What we see

## Layout and hierarchy

The circle is one consistent aperture for many heterogeneous images. Large white gaps make each material readable. Crops enter or leave the device edges, implying a collection beyond the current view. Scale and position vary so the chain does not become a uniform dotted border. The stable center text and bottom action provide reading anchors throughout the motion. The two phone frames are comparison/presentation furniture, not evidence that the website itself uses a two-phone layout.

## Type, color and material

Small dark sans copy and a dense black button are the UI. Photography carries almost all color: organic greens, stone, warm browns, bright metallic blue and iridescent edges. The neutral shell lets these distinct materials coexist without needing matching colored cards. Repeated circular masking provides cohesion; a shared hue is not necessary. Strong specular patches read as the content of the images, not a gradient to paint behind every section.

## Why it works

A rich portfolio is compressed into one recognizable geometric vocabulary. The eye gets variety from the pictures and changing scale, while the user always knows where to read and act. White space is active protection around the small text rather than unused canvas. Repetition establishes the collection; diversity lives inside the apertures. This is a clear case where an arbitrary ban on repeated graphic devices would destroy the concept.

## Transfer into a website

Use for portfolios, cultural work or a visual collection where the images themselves are evidence. Design a static composed arrangement first. Give text and controls a protected zone over the entire motion path, not just at the poster frame. Author position and scale tracks for the image layer separately from the DOM text. Use pause controls or an accessible static gallery when motion is continuous; reduced motion should reveal a deliberate subset, not an arbitrary halfway frame.

## Limitations and transfer risks

The images are not a product mechanism and should not replace useful product explanation on a technical landing page. Small text is hard to assess from the social export. Do not infer touch input from animation alone. The demo does not prove responsive layout, battery cost or a WebGL implementation.

## Extractable decisions

Lock the aperture, collection role, stationary anchors, motion envelope and static fallback. Keep circular repetition when it organizes a collection. Vary image subject, crop, scale and path. Reject any frame where moving imagery crosses essential text or the action. The result should still read as the same page with animation paused.
