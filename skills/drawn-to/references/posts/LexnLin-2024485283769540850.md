# Light bento grid of self-animating feature demos

## What we see
A 5-card light-mode bento section for a SaaS/meeting product where every card is a miniature live product mock running its own animation loop: a task list that shuffles itself, a typewriter meeting title, a pulsing scheduler tooltip, an auto-scrolling stats carousel with an AI prompt bar, and a document that highlights a sentence and pops a floating format toolbar. Captions (short bold title + one muted sentence) sit outside the cards on the page background. Author's tweet: a one-shot Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview generation ("a bit lazy still. But definitely crazy good at UI"). 10 s clip, 1212x898, 30 fps native; frames extracted at ~6 fps (166 ms/frame).

## Layout & composition
- Container ≈ 1076px wide, ~86px side margins on a ~1212px viewport.
- Grid: row 1 = 3 equal cards ~343px each; row 2 = one 2-col card ~709px + one 1-col card ~340px. Column gap ≈ 24-26px, row gap (card→next-row card incl. caption block) ≈ 90px.
- Cards: #fff, radius ≈ 24px, hairline border ~1px #eef0f3, soft wide shadow ≈ 0 8px 30px rgba(16,24,40,0.06). Card height ≈ 316px both rows.
- Card padding ≈ 24-32px; inner elements (task rows, stat tiles, buttons, chips) radius 12-16px - the 24px-outer / ~16px-inner pairing from the generating prompt checks out.
- Captions live OUTSIDE the card: title ~17px/700 ~20px below card edge, one 13px muted sentence under it. No text competes inside the mock UI.
- Page background: flat near-white #f9f9f9. The prompt's blue/green/purple radial mesh over #f5f7f9 is NOT visible in the clip - background reads flat (possibly crushed by compression, but effectively invisible).

## Why saved
Owner note: "Light-mode bento; short title + max 1 sentence per card; main focal point of every card is an animation sequence that conveys the feature pictorially. Loved: the animation sequences and motion use - clean, detailed, strong easing understanding, and each feature is understandable from the animation ALONE without reading the text."

Verified. The specific craft that earns it: (1) every animation IS the feature - reorder = organize, self-typing = effortless input, pulse = proactive automation, marquee = ongoing reporting, highlight+toolbar = AI editing - you could mute all copy and still pitch the product; (2) five loops on co-prime-ish periods (2.45/2.5/5/7.4 s/continuous) keep the section alive without ever synchronizing into a metronome; (3) easing is matched to meaning - linear only for the ambient marquee, spring overshoot for physical acts (row shuffle, toolbar pop), stepped monospace-rhythm for typing, and asymmetric type/delete speeds (105 vs 45 ms/char) that mimic real behavior; (4) motion is spatially anchored - the toolbar rises out of its selection with a tail, the tooltip points at Monday - no orphaned fades; (5) captions demoted outside the tiles keep every mock pixel product-true. The generic-AI tells it avoids: no gradient hero blobs, no in-card marketing copy, no single synchronized loop. Honest caveats vs the generating prompt: the mesh-gradient background and the mock cursor didn't materialize.

## Extractable rules
- Make each bento card a working UI fragment whose animation demonstrates the feature; copy is optional, one caption sentence max, placed outside the card.
- Card shell: #fff on #f9f9f9, radius 24px outer / 12-16px inner, 1px #eef0f3 border, shadow 0 8px 30px rgba(16,24,40,0.06).
- Give every card loop its own period (e.g. 2.45 s, 2.5 s, 5 s, 7.4 s, continuous) so the page never pulses in unison.
- Typewriter: type ~105 ms/char, delete ~45 ms/char (2-3x faster), hold ≥1.5 s before deleting, always show a caret.
- Rotate a 3-item list top→bottom every ~2.5 s; 400-450 ms move with spring cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1); let the stack compress mid-flight and overshoot ~4px on settle.
- Auto-scroll stat carousels at ~35 px/s strictly linear; fade both edges with a ~60px mask-image gradient so tiles dissolve, never clip.
- Pop floating toolbars from their selection: translateY ~32px rising + fade over ~350 ms with slight overshoot, tail pointing at the anchor text.
- Sweep text highlights left→right in ~400 ms before showing any toolbar; keep highlight alpha ≈ 0.18 blue.
- Pulse hint tooltips: in ~450 ms with 4px rise, hold ~1.2 s, out ~250 ms, period ~2.5 s; anchor with a speech-bubble tail.
- Ration chroma: one saturated CTA (orange #ed6917), ink-black #0c121e pills only for commitments, all other color as 8-12% alpha icon tints and tiny delta chips.
- Set captions as ~17px/700 title + 13px muted single sentence, 20px below the card, on the page background.
- Reserve linear easing exclusively for ambient/infinite motion; use springs only for discrete state changes.
