# Hunt.io hero - a lit threat map as the hero object

## What we see
Black-ground security hero: a hex-dot world map, cyan hex prisms as nodes
with teal concentric radar rings, red threat dots with tight glow rings,
1px rounded-corner connectors to mono label chips (Unique error page · JA4
fingerprint · Hash Match · Campaign), one flag badge. H1 "See What Other
Threat Feeds Miss." bottom-left; sub + white pill bottom-right.

## Layout & composition
Full-bleed map centered in the upper 70%; copy in the lower band, H1 left
(~64px/600 white), sub grey + pill right. Nav: wordmark · links · Log In ·
pill. The map is the hero object; type steps aside.

## Type & color
Geometric grotesque 600 white; body grey #8a8a8a-class. Palette: black
#050607, teal/cyan #35c9d6 nodes, red #ff3b30 threats (semantic), mono
chip text #9aa0a6 on #15181c chips.

## Graphic language
Structured light: glow has geometry - rings (1px, falling opacity) around
nodes, dot-map cells brighten near sources, connectors route orthogonally
with 8px rounded corners into chips. Prisms = extruded hexagons with lit
top face + 1px edge. One semantic red against one brand teal.

## Why saved
Owner: the gradients look expensive - not circle blobs but light with
lines/shapes. Verified: every glow is shaped (ring, dot-field, shaft) and
tied to an object; chroma rationed to teal + red-semantic.

## Extractable rules
1. Give glow geometry: rings/rays/dot-fields, never a lone radial blob.
2. Light lives on objects (prism faces, node rings), not on the page.
3. Label chips in mono, routed by 1px orthogonal connectors with rounded corners.
4. One brand hue + one semantic hue on black; no third color.
5. Hero object center-stage, copy demoted to the lower band.

## The six-hero plate (`local_HPbqQYUXAAAbrLQ.jpeg`) - per-tile audit

Media of this slug: a 1500 x 1400 sheet of six SaaS landing screens saved as a
light-field study. Plate shipped with the skill:
`assets/features/gradient-sheet.jpg`. Tier A - independently re-measured.

**Sheet geometry correction:** the seam is 2 px. Left tiles are 750 wide
(x 0-749), right tiles 748 (x 752-1499), and row 3 is 462 rows, not 463. Every
right-column and row-3 percentage from a first reading is off by 0.2-0.3 pp.

**Grain is not measurable from this file.** Content-free windows returned
0.00 / 0.27 / 0.33 · 0.26 / 0.31 · 0.23 · 0.21 · 0.00 / 0.37 · 0.03 % of 255.
Progressive 4:2:0 with luma AC quantisation steps of 10-15 quantises grain under
four levels to zero. Any grain claim about these six tiles is unwriteable
(`measuring.md` § 4).

### T1 Metricly - SLOP, with exactly one keeper

Fails four of the house tells: **eight floating glass icon squircles in four
exact mirror pairs about x = 370** (bboxes 38-43 x 44-46, the extra height being
drop shadow; implied rotation ~5-8 deg, not the claimed +/-15) · zero grain
surviving anywhere · a "109-degree hue path" that measures **34 degrees of
single-family drift** once read at chroma >= 12, i.e. an ordinary two-stop
periwinkle ramp · and the canonical generated hub glyph (white squircle plate,
nested inset ring at 7 px, blue circle, white 4-pane grid). The hub circle is a
monotonic diagonal ramp: `linear-gradient(135deg, #9FA3FE, #6567D6)`. The nav CTA
pill is **vertical** (#9EA3FF at y 23 -> #5C63F6 at y 39) and horizontally flat.

**Keep exactly one thing:** the light placement. Flat hold #F3F1F2 (hue 330,
chroma 2) exact to y 99, within one level to y 118, chroma >= 6 at y 150. The
per-column luminance trough fits `y = -0.000553x^2 + 0.4156x + 387.44` (rms
3.13 px over 92 samples) and the bottom edge reads 206.2 / 197.1 / 184.2 / 184.7
/ 183.2 / 195.3 / 206.2 at x = 5 / 125 / 245 / 365 / 485 / 605 / 725 - the field
is lit from the two bottom CORNERS. Corner ladder #B9CFFF / #B4C8FD / #9AB0FB
against a bottom-centre #A2BEFD; corner-to-centre delta +23 R / +17 G / +2 B, so
the corners are warmer as well as brighter. That is `gradient-fields.md` G1.
Its own failure is the vertex at **50.11 % of width** - dead centre, which means
nobody decided where the light was.

### T2 Signilo - PASS, the strongest construction on the sheet

A clipped light box: inset `59px 0 25px 374px` in a 750 x 468 tile (376 x 384).
The top edge is a genuine 1-2 row step (chroma 2 -> 61); the bottom edge is hard;
the LEFT edge is invisible because the fill's own horizontal dissolve reaches
white about 40 px before the clip. Two lobes on ONE vertical axis at 91.3 % x:
magenta #F96AD4 at 34.6 % y (chroma 143) and coral #FF8BAD at 72.9 % y (chroma
116). Vertical hue 315.6 -> 322.1 -> 336.1 -> 356.3; horizontal ramp over 300 px
to chroma 3 (#FEFDFB). Column rules 1 px #F7F7F7 at x_loc 31 and 203, full tile
height, running behind the logo and the CTA. The CTA pill IS horizontal
(#F68B85 -> #F26FBF) and re-uses the two lobe hues in field order - which is what
makes it a system rather than a picked palette. `gradient-fields.md` G3.

### T3 Flowpilot - field generic, PCB layer real

Flat hold #FAFAFA (saturation 0.0 %) from y 16 to 158 = 30 % of tile height;
chroma 2 at 160, 10 at 216. Hue path 35 degrees under the chroma gate and NOT
monotonic - the reversal is low-chroma noise. The circuit layer is the good part:
1 px #F3F3F3 traces on #FBFBFB, a delta of 8. Its hatch sits one pixel above
Nyquist - perpendicular pitch 2.20 px, horizontal period 2.67, vertical 3.90,
wave vector -34.3 degrees - so no angle claim is better than +/-10 degrees. Hatch
rms amplitude 1.65 L = 0.65 % (the "8/255" figure was peak-to-peak) and it is
achromatic, R-B sigma 0.11.

### T4 InboxWarden - PASS, with two defects

Hue sweep 148 degrees (not 287), peak chroma 27 at x_loc 125, mid band 18-23.
The asymmetry is violet-to-MINT at 5.4:1. Vertical chroma decay at x_loc 60 runs
22 / 20 / 19 / 16 / 14 / 11 / 9 / 5 / 2 / 0, reaching zero at y_loc 240 = 51 % of
height - and the bottom is NOT literal white (only 64.5 % exact). The sine
strokes live at y_loc 211-351 with widths varying 2-18 px along a single curve;
cores #8E84B9 violet, #F8C1E1 pink, #FFEED0 amber, #E2F9FF cyan.
**Defect 1:** headline line 2 is ONE gradient clipped to the line, not per-word
tinting (#CEBBF1, #CAB7E3 | #F3C1D6 | #F3CECC, #EEE1BA | #E0E3D0, #D8E4DA,
#CFE7E2, #C4EAEF). **Defect 2:** that headline measures 1.30-1.84:1 on white and
fails every threshold - the best idea on the sheet, executed until the operative
word is functionally invisible.

### T5 LeadBurst - PASS, the best pure ramp on either sheet

Base is literal #000000 (100 % exact in a 44 x 44 window at 560,960). Twelve-step
left-edge ladder, every step within 2 levels: #070C22 · #0D1945 · #16286F ·
#2A3F98 · #465EB8 · #7084D4 · #9FACE4 · #C7CFF0 · #E2E6F8 · #F3F4FA · #F8F9FD ·
#FCFCFC. **Hue locked 227.0-230.5** - 3.5 degrees over 218 luminance levels -
with the variety carried by a saturation arc instead: 65.9 % at L 8.0, 46.0 % at
L 51.4, 0 % at L 98.8. Iso-luminance-90 vertex at **44.8 % x / 50.9 % y**,
equivalent radius 944 px = 126 % of width (rms 4.0) - genuinely off-axis. The
chroma ridge sweeps diagonally: x = 0 for y 20-180, then x = 38 / 92 / 144 / 208
/ 253 at y = 200 / 220 / 240 / 260 / 280. The right edge is less saturated than
the left at the same row (C73 vs C90) - the source is on the left and the field
admits it. `gradient-fields.md` G4.

### T6 BugScout - REJECT on composition

The field caps at chroma 40 (p99.9) and has nothing below 50 % of tile height,
while the CTA pill reaches chroma 168 (#5760FF) - the loudest thing on the tile
is a button. It is vertical, #A9ADED -> #6268F4, with one #F1F6FF gloss row at
the top edge. It sits above three icon-plus-paragraph cards (C2), a glossy
periwinkle CTA and a sparkle-in-a-squircle. **Its banding verdict is not usable
evidence** - the "49 px staircase" is DCT-grid locked (edge-phase peak/mean 3.11
against 1.30 for a genuinely fine ramp), i.e. a JPEG artefact.

### What this plate is for

It ships in `assets/features/` partly as a counter-example. Two of six tiles fail
the tells, and the failures are more instructive than the passes: a symmetric
light vertex, mirror-paired ornament, a two-stop ramp dressed up as a hue
journey, and a CTA more saturated than the field it sits on.
