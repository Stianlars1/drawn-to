# Monochrome SaaS sections with one vibrant aurora asset

## What we see
Three sections of a fictional "AgentFlow" AI SaaS landing page: hero, pricing, and pre-footer CTA + footer. The entire UI chrome is strict grayscale (black, white, two grays); every drop of color comes from a single pixelated aurora-borealis artwork (cyan/teal over deep navy mountains) that is reused as the hero media panel, the Pro pricing card background, and the footer CTA banner. Each screen is presented as a white page card floating on a #FAFAFA canvas.

## Layout & composition
- Page container ~1200px, centered; white page surface on #FAFAFA outer canvas, page radius ~16px in the mockup frame.
- Nav: logo left, 4 links centered (gap ~36px, 15px/500), right cluster = "Login" light-gray pill + "Book a Demo" black pill (height ~44px, padding ~12x24, radius full).
- Hero stack is fully center-aligned: badge → H1 (2 lines) → subhead (max-width ~480px) → 2 pill CTAs (gap ~12px) → full-width media panel; vertical rhythm ~24-32px between blocks, ~64px before media.
- Hero media panel: full container width, aspect ~3:1, radius ~24px, no border; a white prompt bar (~660x64px, radius full, soft shadow ~0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.12)) floats dead-center over the artwork with a plus icon left and circled arrow-submit right.
- Logo row below media: 6 logos justified edge-to-edge (space-between), each = 40px dark circular icon + 18px/600 wordmark.
- Pricing: centered H2 + subhead, then a 2-segment Monthly/Annual toggle (~290x48px, active segment white with hairline border, radius ~10px), then two equal cards ~460px wide, gap ~24px, radius ~14px.
- Footer CTA banner: same artwork, full container width, aspect ~3.2:1, radius ~16px, centered white 2-line H2 + single white button.
- Footer: 4-column grid (~40% brand + 3 link columns), then a separate hairline-outlined bottom bar (1px #E5E7EB, radius ~12px, padding ~24px) holding copyright left / legal links right with a hairline divider between the two links.
- Dividers essentially absent elsewhere - separation is done with whitespace and surface changes, not lines.

## Card anatomy
- Starter card (light): white bg, 1px #E5E7EB border, radius ~14px, padding ~32px. Order: plan name (20px/600) → price ($19 at ~48px/700 + "/month" 16px/600) → 2-line description (16px/400, #374151) → full-width CTA (height ~56px, radius ~8px, #F5F5F7 bg, dark text) → 6 feature rows.
- Pro card (inverted): geometrically identical clone of Starter - same order, sizes, spacing - but the full-bleed aurora artwork is the card background, all text white, and the CTA is solid white with dark text. No "most popular" badge; the image itself is the emphasis device.
- Feature rows: 40px circular check chip + 16px label, row gap ~20px. Chips are #F4F4F5 with black check on light card; white at ~18% alpha with white check on the image card.
- Visual-area vs text: Starter is 100% text; Pro is text over ~100% imagery - emphasis by surface, not by size or extra ornament.

## Type & color
- Typeface: a neutral grotesk (Helvetica Now / Inter class), tight tracking (~-2%) on display sizes.
- Hierarchy: H1 ~72px/500, line-height ~1.08; section H2 ~48px/500; banner H2 ~44px/500 white; card titles 20px/600; body/subheads 18px/400 #6B7280; nav/links 15px/400-500; footer links 15px/400 with 16px/600 column headers.
- UI palette: ink #0A0A0A, white #FFFFFF, surface gray #F4F4F5-#F5F5F7, border #E5E7EB, secondary text #6B7280, outer canvas #FAFAFA.
- Artwork palette (sole color source): electric cyan #0AF0EE, teal #34C8E0, sea blue #0076AF, mountain indigo #09176F, deep navy #02143C-#00034B.
- Contrast strategy: zero-hue UI + one hyper-saturated cyan/navy asset repeated 3x; the artwork reads as the brand color system without a single colored token in the UI.

## Graphic language
- One hero illustration carries the whole page: an aurora borealis over mountains rendered through a visible pixel/mosaic grid (small square cells with hairline grid lines, ~8-10px cells), turning a photographic subject into a crafted, digital-native texture - reads as "AI-generated pixels" and doubles as product metaphor.
- Everything else is typographic or geometric: circular check chips, circular icon buttons, pill buttons, simple line icons (plus, arrow, social glyphs in 40px hairline-bordered squares, radius ~8px).
- Shape hierarchy: marketing CTAs (nav, hero) are full pills; in-component CTAs (cards, banner, subscribe) are ~8px-radius rectangles - two button languages, consistently split by context.
- Fictional client logos are unified: every mark forced into an identical 40px black circle so the trust row stays monochrome.

## Why saved
Owner note: "hero + pricing + footer sections. Liked: simple colors with cool vibrant contrasts that make it feel complete, whole, and beautiful."

Verified. The completeness feeling is engineered by single-asset color discipline: the UI itself is 100% grayscale, and one artwork supplies all color, appearing exactly three times at three scales (hero media, Pro card surface, footer banner). That repetition is what makes hero, pricing, and footer feel like one object rather than three sections. The craft moves a generic AI page would miss: (1) the artwork is texture-processed (pixel mosaic grid), not a raw gradient or stock photo, so it owns an identity; (2) the Pro card achieves emphasis by cloning Starter's exact geometry and only swapping the surface - no badge, no scale change, no accent border; (3) the check chips correctly re-derive as translucent white on imagery instead of keeping gray; (4) the floating prompt bar centered in the hero artwork demos the product inside the decoration; (5) two deliberate button radius languages (pill vs 8px) separate marketing chrome from component UI.

## Extractable rules
- Keep all UI chrome grayscale (#0A0A0A / #FFFFFF / #F5F5F7 / #E5E7EB / #6B7280); inject color only through one reusable artwork.
- Reuse the same signature artwork at least 3x across a page (hero media, one pricing card, footer CTA) to bind sections together.
- Process photographic/gradient artwork through a pixel-mosaic grid (~8-10px cells with hairline grid lines) to give it a crafted identity.
- Emphasize the featured pricing tier by surface inversion only: clone the base card's geometry exactly, swap bg to the artwork, flip text to white, CTA to solid white.
- On image surfaces, convert gray chips/controls to white at 15-20% alpha; never keep light-mode grays.
- Use pill radius (full) for marketing CTAs in nav/hero; use ~8px radius for CTAs inside cards, banners, and forms.
- Float the product's core input (prompt/search bar, ~64px tall, full radius, shadow 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.12)) centered over the hero artwork.
- Set display type in a tight grotesk: H1 ~72px/500 with ~-2% tracking and ~1.08 line-height; section H2 ~48px/500.
- Constrain subheads to ~480px max-width, 18px #6B7280, centered under display headings.
- Normalize partner/client logos into identical 40px black circles + 18px/600 wordmarks, justified space-between.
- Frame the legal footer row in its own 1px #E5E7EB, ~12px-radius container instead of a bare top border.
- Skip decorative dividers; separate content with whitespace (~64-96px section gaps) and surface changes only.
