# Dark editorial feature sections with ambient choreography

## What we see
A roundup ("Some of the best cards/feature section designs posted recently") of four dark-UI feature/card designs: (1) a Linear-like editorial bento for a docs product ("Docentra") with a live radar visual and a 2x2 capability grid separated by hairline dividers; (2) a three-card feature row for a forms product ("Nina") floating on a slow-drifting teal smoke background with a self-scrolling integrations grid; (3) a stats trio (1M+ / 25 / $1.5B+) whose visuals are slow-motion chrome-blue 3D renders; (4) a static 2x2 set of vivid gradient-mesh metric cards embedding tiny functional UI fragments. Everything sits between #0A0C10 and #111111; motion is continuous, slow, and linear — the layouts never move, only the materials inside them.

## Layout & composition
- Video 1 (bento): full page framed by two full-height 1px vertical rails at ~120px and ~1106px on a ~1226px viewport (content column ~985px). No cards, no radii, no shadows — every region is cut by 1px dividers. Section stack: top strip (~72px) / feature section (~520px) split ~46:54 into media panel (radar) and 2x2 grid / full-width divider / centered-left heading band (~130px) / divider / 3-col icon feature row (~175px, columns split by dashed 1px verticals) / divider / next section peeking.
- 2x2 grid cells ~265x225px, inner padding ~24-28px; outer/section dividers solid 1px ~#15181D on #0A0C10 (barely +5 luma — deliberately near-threshold); inner cell separators are even fainter and partially dashed (~4/4px dash), some only readable on hover.
- Videos 2 & 4 (card rows): 3 equal cards ~420x460px, gap ~24px, radius ~14px, near-black card fill #050608 with 1px inside-glow borders lit by the background gradient. The page behind is charcoal (#1A1A1A-#242424 under sheen) divided into large tiles by faint 1px grid lines — the bento language continues outside the cards.
- Photo: 4 cards ~square (each ~1200px at 2x), radius ~28px, gap ~64px, laid out as a staircase — row 1 occupies columns 1-2, row 2 occupies columns 2-3 — leaving two empty corners of pure #111111. No borders or shadows; the gradient fill itself is the card's edge.

## Card anatomy
- Bento cells (video 1): icon 20px stroke ~1.5px → 12px gap → 14-15px/500 title → 8px → 13px/400 two-to-three-line body. Text occupies <40% of cell height; the rest is deliberate dark air.
- Feature cards (videos 2/4): ~55-60% visual area (top or full-bleed), text block pinned to bottom: 16-17px/500 title, 8px, 13px/400 desc at ~55% white, max ~3 lines. Embedded UI props (chat bubble + Generate pill button, email input, settings row) are rendered at ~80% scale, cropped by the card edge, annotated with named multiplayer cursors (Steve = orange, Meghan = teal/blue).
- Stat cards (video 4): oversized metric (~56px gradient-silver numerals "$1.5B+", "1M+") top-left or center, 13px supporting copy, one glass pill CTA; 3D render owns the remaining ~65%.
- Photo cards: label top-left (20px, 2 lines) → giant center numeral (~150-170px, light weight, gradient-filled, superscript unit at ~40%) → one functional micro-widget on the numeral axis (slider, tick ruler, radial dial, crosshair) → 18px caption bottom-left.

## Type & color
- Grotesk sans throughout (Inter/Suisse-like). Video 1 hierarchy: H2 ~29-32px/400-500, near-white #E6E8EA; cell titles 14-15px/500 #D6DBD9; body 13-13.5px/400 #868D93; ~1.5 line-height; tracking neutral-to-slightly-tight. Photo numerals are light-weight (300) at display size.
- Three-step gray ramp does all hierarchy work: ~#E8EAEC titles / ~#9BA1A7 body / ~#5C636A captions on #0A0C10-#111.
- Accents: teal-cyan #2CB6D8→#8FE8F2 (videos 1-2, single accent), electric chrome blue #1E5BFF→#9EC4FF on black (video 4), and the photo's four saturated meshes: blue #0A6CF5/#33FAFF, crimson #E01F5A/#6D1926, orange-lime #F97316/#819C2B, violet #9C67FD/#50258E — each card monochrome-mesh, never mixed.
- Contrast strategy: keep structure at 3-8% luma deltas (dividers, cell fills) so the one saturated element per section carries 100% of the attention.

## Graphic language
- Features are depicted as instruments, not illustrations: a radar with orbiting named-cursor "contacts" for team knowledge; a self-scrolling logo grid for integrations; sliders/dials/tick-rulers for metrics. Every prop is a plausible UI fragment with real states (placeholder text, Enter-key hint inside the Generate button, dashed focus dividers).
- Materials do the branding: slow-mo 3D chrome ribs and liquid-metal ribbons (video 4), volumetric teal smoke/silk (video 2), conic radar gradient with layered concentric rings at +2% luma steps (video 1), heavy multi-stop gradient mesh with subtle grain (photo).
- Multiplayer cursors (small colored arrow + dark pill label, 11px text) recur across all three videos as a signature humanizing device.

## Motion timeline
- Video 1 (9.6s loop, 60fps): radar beam rotates counter-clockwise at exactly 60 deg/s — 6.0s per revolution, measured linear to within 3 deg, no easing, seamless loop. The two cursor contacts orbit CCW with the sweep at their own radii. t=1.9s: a simulated cursor begins touring the 2x2 grid; hover highlight = 1px teal border that is brightest on the bottom edge and fades up the sides, plus a bottom-up teal wash at ~6% opacity. Measured highlight envelope: fade-in ~230ms ease-in-out, hold ~570ms, fade-out ~200ms. Tour order BR→BL→TL→TR (mirroring the radar's CCW direction), first pass ~800ms per cell, second pass ~450ms per cell, ending t=6.7s; final 2.9s is pure radar idle. t=8.3s: as the cursor passes the bottom row, the "Quick to Launch" box-arrow icon fires a one-shot ~430ms launch-out-and-return and the sync icon rotates 180 deg — once, not looping.
- Video 2 (16.3s): background teal smoke gradient drifts diagonally across the whole viewport for the entire clip (~15px/s, linear), re-lighting card rims as it passes. Integration logos scroll leftward continuously at one icon pitch (~86px) per ~3.0s, linear, rows offset half a pitch, with occasional frosted placeholder tiles riding in the stream; icons fade under a mask at card edges. Cursors idle with sub-pixel jitter. No discrete events — a pure ambient loop.
- Video 4 (18.0s): left card's ribbed chrome stack translates along its own diagonal (~0.09 card-widths/s, constant velocity, like slow-motion footage); right card's liquid ribbon flows and self-shades continuously; middle card's blue-violet aurora breathes; a faint specular glint crosses near the headline around t=6s. Layout, type, and cursors never move.
- Global grammar: structure static, materials linear/constant-velocity and loop-safe; the only eased motion is interaction feedback (hover, icon one-shots) at 200-450ms.

## Why saved
Owner note: "This is the owner's absolute favorite dark-UI tone. Editorial bento feature section; thin sharp 1px dividers instead of typical rounded free-floating cards; Linear-like. The animation sequences are 'so professional, high detail-factor, flawless, unique' — owner cannot articulate exactly why."

Verified — the "why" is articulable and it is mostly discipline, not effects:
1. Two motion registers, never mixed: ambient material motion is strictly linear and constant-velocity (60 deg/s radar, 3s/pitch marquee, drifting smoke) so it reads as physics/footage, while interaction motion is short and eased (230/570/200ms hover envelope, 430ms icon one-shots). Generic AI pages ease everything, which makes ambience feel like UI and UI feel like soup.
2. The demo is directed like a screen recording: a simulated cursor tours the grid in the same rotational direction as the radar, decelerating pattern (slow pass, fast pass, rest), and micro-animations fire once as the cursor passes. There's narrative causality, not a loop of idle effects.
3. Near-threshold structure: dividers at +5 luma, cell fills at +2%, dashed inner separators — the grid is felt more than seen, which is exactly the Linear editorial tone. Cheap versions use #333 borders and kill it.
4. Hover is a gradient border (bottom-lit, fading up the sides) plus a directional wash — light behaves as if it has a source, consistent with the teal beam elsewhere on the page.
5. Detail density in props: Enter-key hint inside the button, named cursors with distinct colors, frosted placeholder tiles in the marquee, laurel glyphs around "25". Each card could ship.
6. One accent hue per section, everything else 3-step gray — so the choreography never competes with the palette.

## Extractable rules
- Build dark feature sections as divider-cut bento: 1px solid #15181D on #0A0C10, no card fills, no radii, no shadows.
- Frame the content column with two full-height 1px rails (~985px column on 1226px viewport) and run section dividers full-bleed between them.
- Keep dividers within +4-8 luma of the background; use dashed 1px for inner sub-splits.
- Type ramp on dark: title #E6E8EA 14-15px/500, body #868D93 13px/400/1.5, one H2 at ~30px/400; nothing bolder than 500.
- One accent hue per section (teal #2CB6D8 or chrome blue #1E5BFF); all other color from a 3-step gray ramp.
- Run ambient/material motion at constant velocity with zero easing: rotations ~60 deg/s, marquees ~1 item per 3s, drifts 10-30px/s, loop-safe.
- Reserve easing for interaction only: hover in ~230ms ease-in-out, hold ~550ms, out ~200ms; icon one-shots 400-450ms fired once on cursor pass.
- Render hover as a gradient 1px border brightest on one edge fading up the sides, plus a <=8% directional color wash — never a uniform outline.
- Direct demo loops with a simulated cursor whose path has narrative pacing (slow tour, fast tour, rest) and triggers micro-events en route.
- Depict features as working instruments (radar, dial, slider, marquee) at ~80% scale, cropped by the container, with real UI states.
- Add named multiplayer cursors (11px pill label + colored arrow, one warm one cool) to imply liveness.
- For stat cards, pair one oversized light-weight gradient numeral (~150px) with a single monochrome gradient mesh and a subtle grain overlay, radius ~28px, no border.
