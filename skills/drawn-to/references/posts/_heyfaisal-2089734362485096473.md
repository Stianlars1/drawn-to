# FLOWAI - airy light AI-automation landing page kit

## What we see
Three static shots of "FLOWAI", a premium AI workflow-automation UI kit (ui8 product, made in Figma): a hero closeup, and two zoomed-out full-page composites showing every section - feature grid, chip marquee, FAQ, industry cards, ROI stats, solution rows, and a running-workflow glass card. Aesthetic is airy premium light UI: near-monochrome grays on white, one royal blue accent (~#3770E9), unified by a blue marbled-ink texture used as backdrop, hero media, banner fills, and card imagery.

## Layout & composition
- Presentation: page mock floats as a 1440px-wide white card (radius ~24px, soft ambient shadow ~0 24px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)) on a full-bleed blue-ink marble texture backdrop (#EFF4F6 → #5B97BB → #1B4B73).
- Nav: ~72px tall, 3-zone - logo left (pixel-dither flower mark + bold wordmark), 4 center links 15px #6E6E6E with ~48px gaps, black pill CTA right (~44px tall, radius 10, #1A1A1A).
- Hero: centered column, H1 max-width ~800px, sub max-width ~640px, CTA pair gap ~16px; hero media is a 1280px-wide texture image (radius ~16px) with a centered glass card.
- Features: row of 3 equal cards (~400px, gap ~24px) above a row of 2 wide cards (~55/45 split); visual panels are #F1F1F1 rounded-16 canvases, titles/descriptions sit below on white.
- Marquee: two horizontally offset rows of white task-chip pills (radius ~12, 1px #EAEAEA border, ~40px tall) on a #F7F7F7 band - implied infinite scroll.
- Industry: eyebrow + centered H2, then 2×2 gray cards (#EFEFEF, radius ~16, padding ~28px) flanked by texture-filled image tiles; ROI section is split stat cards on #F5F5F5 with hairline separation by gap, not borders.
- Solution sections: alternating text-left/media-right rows; media is texture imagery with floating glass UI fragments.
- Dividers essentially absent - separation is done with background-tone steps (white / #F7F7F7 / #EFEFEF) and whitespace (~120px section padding).

## Card anatomy
- Feature card: gray visual panel ~55–60% of card height on top (containing a UI fragment), then 18–20px medium-weight title, then 13–14px #8A8A8A description, all left-aligned on white. No border, no shadow - the gray panel IS the card.
- Industry card: 48px white icon tile (radius 12, 1.5px stroke line icon) top-left, ~24px gap, 20px title, 13px 3-line description; whole card #EFEFEF radius 16.
- Glass chat card (hero): all-caps 15px tracked label, white inner input card (radius ~16) with 18px gray placeholder, footer row: 40px "+" attach button (1px #EAEAEA border, radius 12) left, mic icon + 40px black send square (radius 12, #1C1C1C) right; below, 2×2 suggestion chips (white pills, sparkle icon + 14px label).
- Workflow glass card: header "WORKFLOW – RUNNING" + status dot label right, hairline divider, 5-step vertical checklist (24px circles: done = blue fill + white check, pending = white ring + blue dot, joined by 1px connector line), footer: PROGRESS / 60% + 6px full-radius bar filled to 60% in blue.
- ROI stat card: area chart top (2px #3B6FE8 line, gradient fill #C6DAF5→transparent, faint vertical gridlines), below a pair of 40px stats ("10x", "85%") with 14px gray captions.

## Type & color
- Typeface: geometric rounded sans with single-story "a" (Poppins/Century Gothic family). H1 ~64px medium (500), section H2 ~36–40px medium, card titles 18–20px medium, body 13–16px regular, all-caps labels 14–15px with ~8% tracking.
- Text: headings #3A3A3A (soft charcoal, never pure black), body #8A8A8A–#6E6E6E. Contrast is deliberately gentle.
- Accent: royal blue #3770E9 (primary CTA, AI Router node, hub tile, checkmarks, progress, eyebrow labels, chart line). It is the ONLY saturated hue in the UI layer.
- Neutrals: white surfaces, #F7F7F7 / #F1F1F1 / #EFEFEF panel grays, #1A1A1A black for nav CTA and send button.
- Texture palette: blue ink-marble family #7FBBE6 / #50A0DC / #1B4B73 - all color intensity lives in imagery, not components.
- Primary CTA detail: blue button (radius ~12, height ~46px) with an inset white 32px icon tile (radius 8) holding a blue chevron glyph, plus a blue-tinted drop shadow (~0 10px 24px rgba(55,112,233,0.35)).

## Graphic language
- Features are shown as miniature UI fragments, not illustrations: Multi Model AI = an actual routing diagram (white "Task Input" pill → 1px connector → blue "AI Router" pill → row of 5 white model pills: Claude, GPT-5, ElevenLabs, DALL-E, Gemini); Integration = a row of 9 white rounded-square brand tiles with the FLOWAI hub tile in blue, larger and glowing, at center - hub-and-spokes with no lines; Agent Builder = a running workflow checklist with real step copy ("Enrich lead data via API", "Log activity to CRM").
- Glassmorphism is the framing device: frosted white ~35–50% cards with backdrop blur and 1px white/60 borders float over the marble texture.
- The blue ink-marble texture is the single art-direction asset, recurring at every scale (backdrop, hero, banners, tile imagery) - it replaces both illustration and photography.
- Logo marquee uses plausible fictional brands (IntelliSpark, NeuroLink, DataStack, Graphizo, Overlay) rendered ~#9C9C9C grayscale.
- Brand mark is a pixel-dithered flower/starburst, echoed in the hub tile - mark doubles as product iconography.

## Why saved
No owner note. This is squarely the owner's "airy premium light UI" lane with feature-fragment storytelling. What earns the save, beyond generic AI-page output: (1) one saturated hue total - every blue in the UI layer is the same #3770E9, and all other color is quarantined inside texture imagery, giving a disciplined monochrome+1 system; (2) features demonstrated as literal working UI fragments (router diagram with real model names, workflow checklist with believable CRM steps, 60% progress) instead of icon+blurb cards; (3) the single marble texture reused at every scale creates brand cohesion cheaper than custom illustration; (4) micro-details a template would miss - the inset white icon tile inside the primary CTA, blue-tinted button shadow, done/pending checklist states, mixed done-check vs pending-dot geometry, texture tiles slotted into the industry card grid; (5) contrast restraint (charcoal on white, gray body) that reads expensive rather than washed out.

## Extractable rules
- Float full-page mocks as a 1440px white card, radius 24px, on a full-bleed texture backdrop.
- Allow exactly one saturated accent (#3770E9); confine all other color to imagery/texture layers.
- Use one signature texture (blue ink marble) for backdrop, hero media, banners, and card image tiles alike.
- Show features as miniature working UI fragments (diagrams, checklists, integration rows), never icon+text alone.
- Build feature cards as a #F1F1F1 rounded-16 visual panel (~55–60% height) with title/description below on white.
- Separate sections with background-tone steps (white → #F7F7F7 → #EFEFEF) and ~120px padding instead of dividers.
- Set headings in #3A3A3A at weight 500, body in #8A8A8A - never pure black.
- Give the primary CTA an inset 32px white icon tile (radius 8) and a blue-tinted shadow rgba(55,112,233,0.35).
- Float glass cards (white 35–50% + backdrop-blur + 1px white/60 border) over texture imagery for product UI.
- Encode process state visually: blue-filled check circles for done, white ring + blue dot for pending, 1px connector line.
- Anchor an integration section on a centered, larger, glowing brand tile amid uniform 44px white app tiles.
- Keep component radii in a tight family: 8 (nested tiles), 12 (buttons/chips), 16 (cards/panels), 24 (page frame).
