## What we see

The gallery is ten live React components from the Wealthsimple web app, laid out as a masonry of light-gray squircle tiles on a near-white page, each a working fragment with real data: a currency switcher (CA/US flag chips), an account-details drawer with a black card carrying a gold W that flips to its back and frosts over when "Lock your virtual card" is toggled, two appearance sliders (blue gradient track, red stepped track), a monthly-millionaire counter with a gold tick ruler, a credit-card payment flow (Pay Today 0.00 / Total balance 9,254.95 in gold / payment-window bar strip / black Pay pill / Back / Payment amount / three radio rows / Review), a $100.01 amount stepper with a stacked +/- key, a fluid-selectors row (checkbox, two radios, green switch), a spinning checkmark that resolves to "Order sent", a privacy-mode balance ($2,854,256.78 with a red -$0.96 (-0.37%) line) and a 1D/1W/1M/3M/YTD/1Y/ALL timeframe segmented control. ## Layout & composition
- Three container widths, all Tier A: prose `--content-width: 720px`; media breakout `--breakout-width: 893px`; the three widget galleries `--demo-w: min(1280px, 100vw)` with `padding: 0 20px`, so the mosaic is 1240 wide, `margin: 64px auto`. Prose stays narrow while figures widen in two steps.
- Mosaic: `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr)`, `grid-auto-rows: 1px`, `grid-auto-flow: row dense`, `gap: 0 20px` (row packing done by measuring each tile and spanning rows, +20 px per tile). Columns 400 px each at the widest breakpoint; 2 columns under 1100 px, 1 column under 640 px. Measured on f_001: tiles 740/741/741 native (400 CSS), gaps 36-37 native (20 CSS).
- Column stagger `[56, 0, 56]` px of padding-top on the first tile of columns 1 and 3, so the top edge is not a flat line; measured 104 native = 56.2 CSS between column 2's top (y 54) and columns 1/3 (y 158).
- Tile aspect ratios come from each widget's meta: 1.78 (switcher, stepper, fluid, timeframe, privacy), 1.5 (slider, checkmark), 0.66 (payment, pinned to column 2), 0.58 (drawer, pinned to column 2). Column pins keep the two tall phone-shaped widgets stacked in the middle column.
- Every tile centres its widget with flex; the widget itself is capped (slider `maxWidth: 296`, drawer 240x153 closed) so it floats in gray air, never touches the tile edge.
- Reserved height per gallery (`min-height: calc(var(--demo-w) * 1.0846 - 51px)` for ws-gallery) so the page does not jump when the shadow-root demo mounts.

## Why saved
Saved without comment; the post's own text calls out "the article's live interactive UI widgets - you can feel the design quality and delight of these components directly", and the quoted essay by @brotzky is the source. Positive exemplar. What it adds that the corpus lacked: (1) the article-as-demo pattern at essay scale - GrahamPaterson-2089 and ImranUxi-2089 are docs and a curriculum; this is long-form prose whose figures are ten shipped components in a shadow root, with a three-width layout (720 / 893 / 1280) that lets figures outgrow the column without breaking the reading measure; (2) a masonry gallery grammar for components: 1 px auto-rows with measured spans, dense flow, column pins for tall tiles, a 56-0-56 stagger, hover-only frosted labels, 38 px superellipse tiles - the corpus had no measured mosaic for demo tiles; (3) a complete, named spring vocabulary (bounce/snap/roll plus the per-widget springs) from a production fintech design system, which the corpus has only from craft demos (flornkm-2089, cabralorenzo-2090); (4) "state as material": lock renders as frost, privacy as dots that keep tabular width, success as a ring that draws, pops in 3D and glows - every state change is a material change, not a colour swap.

## Extractable rules
1. Three widths, not one: prose 720, figure breakout 893, component gallery 1280 with 20 px side padding; figures may widen, the measure may not.
2. Component mosaic: `grid: repeat(3, 1fr)`, `grid-auto-rows: 1px`, `grid-auto-flow: row dense`, column gap 20, each tile spans `ceil(height + 20)` rows; pin tall tiles (`grid-column: 2`); stagger the first tile of the outer columns by 56 px; collapse to 2 columns at 1100 and 1 at 640.
3. Tile skin: `#f2f2f2` on `#fcfcfc`, radius 38 with `corner-shape: superellipse(1.6)` (16 / superellipse(1.8) on narrow), border `color-mix(ink 4%)`, no shadow; reserve the gallery's height with `min-height: calc(var(--demo-w) * ratio - offset)` so late-mounting demos never shift the prose.
4. Labels only on hover: 30 px pill, 13px/500/-0.01em, frosted gradient fill plus 1 px masked gradient border, `blur(10px) saturate(1.3)` only while visible, 150 ms opacity, `visibility` gated.
5. Every widget carries its own aspect ratio (1.78 / 1.5 / 0.66 / 0.58) and is capped in width so it floats centred in gray air.
6. Numbers: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` everywhere, 700 weight with -0.04em at 32 px, -0.02em at 26, -0.005em at 18; money-in gold `#CFA65F`, loss `#C02416`, done `#409652`, one hue per meaning, none shared across widgets except green.
7. Spring set to reuse: `snap {440, 32, 1}` for segmented indicators and thumbs, `bounce {325, 20, 1}` for pops, `roll {160, 8.9, .37}` for rolling digits, `{600, 35, .4}` for an 18 px digit roll with 120 ms opacity and 150 ms blur, `{260, 24}` to flip a card and `{260, 30}` to flip it back.
8. Squash on direct manipulation: thumbs stretch toward their destination (`scaleX 1.5` at the midpoint, `0.9` on arrival, `elastic.out(1, .5)` 450 ms settle) with `transform-origin` on the leading edge; checkboxes compress `.85/.9` on press and release with `elastic.out(1, .25)` over 800 ms.
9. Success = draw, pop, glow: ring draw 450 ms, 3D pop `1 -> 1.15 -> .95 -> 1.03 -> 1` over 900 ms, check draw 400 ms starting at 380 ms, glow disc `blur(24px)` scaling `.5 -> 1.6` to opacity 0.12; status text leaves with `blur(8px) translateY(6px) scale(.96)` in 400 ms.
10. State as material: lock = frost (frozen texture image plus `blur(.5px)` through five growing radial masks, 1.8 s in `[.33,1,.68,1]`, 0.9 s out `[.16,1,.3,1]`); privacy = digits crossfade to 15 px dots over 420 ms with 22 ms stagger, keeping tabular width.
11. Text entry animates per character: `opacity 0, y 8, scale .85, blur(8px)` to rest over 950 ms `[.16,1,.3,1]`, delay per index; a 2x26 px caret bar sits in the flow.
12. Inner white cards inside gray tiles: radius 16-20, `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.04), 0 10px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.04)`; `1px rgba(0,0,0,.08)` row dividers; black pill CTAs; keys 34 px at radius 9 with a 1 px white gradient rim.
13. Ship every widget with a light and dark object (`#f2f2f2` / `#141414` tile, `#fff` / `#1c1c1e` card) even when the essay renders only light.
14. One ambient loop per page at most (the ruler's 14 s stroke rotation); everything else waits for the pointer.
