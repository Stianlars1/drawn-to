# Tactile WebGL slider with gooey glass physics

## What we see
A single interactive slider component rendered in WebGL (credited to @jaenam97), demoed in an 18.7s loop on a 1578x1578 near-black canvas. Bottom: a smoky glass capsule track whose gel body physically dents, ripples, and wrinkles around a triangular thumb as it is dragged. Top: a tumbling glass cube displaying the live value as amber 7-segment digits on every face, sitting inside a semicircular protractor gauge whose ticks and arcs light up in sync. Everything reads as one HUD instrument: slider, dial, and counter are three redundant views of the same value.

## Layout & composition
- Square stage ~1578px (display ~800 CSS px); vertical stack, both instruments center-aligned on the x-axis.
- Background #040607 with a 1px square grid, ~40px cell, grid line ~#0e1418 (6-8% white); subtle vignette toward corners.
- Gauge cluster occupies the top ~60%: outer tick ring ~840px diameter, semicircle 180deg; minor ticks every ~5deg (1px, ice-grey ~#5c6866), longer major ticks every ~15deg.
- Cube ~370px per face, corner radius ~60px (rounded glass cube), centered on the gauge's origin.
- Slider band in bottom ~30%: corner-only bracket frame ~1380x300px, 1px ice-grey strokes, arms ~40px; one 6px chartreuse status dot centered outside each side (lit #dde47c on interaction, dim grey idle).
- Track capsule ~1180x160px, fully rounded (r ~80px), smoky glass body ~#16181c with white specular micro-streaks.
- Value line inset ~90px from each capsule end, 1px, with I-beam end stops; minor ruler ticks hang below the line and deform with the gel.

## Type & color
- Only glyphs are 7-segment digital numerals (DSEG-style), always zero-padded to 3 digits (005, 084, 100) - padding keeps the display width stable while counting.
- Digit color amber #ffb43c with hot bloom core ~#ffe9a0 and glow falloff to ~#7a3d10; repeated on 3 visible cube faces, each oriented to its face plane.
- Palette: bg #040607; grid #0e1418; structural ice-grey ~#aebdba at ~50-60% opacity for idle ticks, brackets, unfilled line, idle thumb outline; single hot accent amber-orange #ff9c28-#ffb43c for everything value-related; tertiary chartreuse #dde47c side dots; tiny red/green ghost circuitry (#ff3b30 / #34c759-ish) trapped inside the cube glass as chromatic-aberration accents.
- Contrast strategy: idle = grey hairlines on near-black; interaction = amber ignition. State is communicated purely by the grey-to-amber swap plus bloom, never by size or copy.

## Graphic language
- Shader-driven glass/gel materials, no illustration and no words. The track is a soft-body: the thumb presses a dent into it, drag velocity spawns a trailing ripple wake, and wrinkled refraction (plastic-wrap normals) distorts everything seen through it.
- The craft flex: the orange fill line is rendered *inside* the gel and visibly refracts/sags through the dent under the thumb - the data layer obeys the material's optics.
- The value cube is a rounded glass die: digits glow on each face, internal red/green wireframe doodles and a white specular dot give it depth; it tumbles in the drag direction like it is rolled by the slider, with spring overshoot.
- Gauge = protractor ring + thin orange progress arc + translucent filled sector sweeping 0-180deg proportional to value; ticks inside the swept range ignite amber, the rest stay grey.
- HUD grammar everywhere: corner brackets, I-beam end stops, status dots - instrument-panel semantics rather than web-UI chrome.

## Motion timeline
Source 120fps; frames sampled at 6fps (1 frame ~167ms).
- 0.0-1.0s - idle: cube at rest showing 000, gauge grey, gel smooth, thumb is a 1px outline triangle at left; cursor enters ~0.9s.
- 1.0-1.8s - press + fast drag right: thumb ignites (fill + glow, <100ms), value counts through real intermediates (005, 012, 027, 043), gauge arc/sector sweeps with ~100ms lag, cube starts tumbling, gel dents under the thumb with a ripple wake trailing ~150-250ms behind.
- 1.8-3.3s - continue to 084-100: leading-edge gel bulge piles up near the right cap; passed ticks light amber.
- 3.3-4.0s - settle at 100: thumb reaches end stop, arc full, ripples damp out over ~600-800ms; thumb outline returns to idle grey ~300ms after release.
- 4.0-6.0s - drag back to 000: mirrored wake; heavy squash-and-stack wrinkles near the left cap (~5.0s); full decay to idle by 6.0s.
- 6.3-11.0s - second, slower pass to 100 (009, 037, 065, 088, 097, 100): deformation amplitude is visibly smaller - gel displacement scales with drag velocity, not position.
- 11.7-15.9s - rapid scrubbing (100→055→075→035→085→071): violent full-track ripples; cube flips rotation direction with each reversal and overshoots ~1-2 oscillations (spring feel, ~500-700ms settle, est. stiffness ~120 / damping ~14); gauge chases with slight lag.
- 15.9-17.6s - decelerating sweep to 0 (013, 002); ripples damp progressively.
- 17.7-18.7s - rest at 000; gel relaxes ~800ms; amber fades to grey; frame matches t=0 for a clean loop.
Easing character: input mapping is direct/linear; all reactive layers are damped springs (cube rotation, gel waves) or short ease-out lags (gauge, tick fades ~150ms).

## Why saved
No owner note; tweet text: "Tactile WebGL Slider by @jaenam97". This is squarely the owner's "motion that SHOWS the feature" taste applied to a primitive: the demo never explains the slider, it makes you feel drag velocity, resistance, and settle through material response. The decisions a generic AI page would miss: (1) deformation amplitude driven by velocity, so slow drags feel calm and flicks feel violent; (2) the fill line refracting through the gel dent - the data respects the physics; (3) triple redundancy of one value (counter, dial, track) choreographed with distinct lags so the system feels mechanical, not synced-by-code; (4) a strict grey-idle/amber-active state language with zero-padded digits; (5) an 18.7s demo scripted as four acts (fast pass, slow pass, scrub, decay) that doubles as a perfect loop.

## Extractable rules
- Keep the canvas near-black #040607 with a 6-8% white 1px grid at ~40px cells for HUD scenes.
- Use one hot accent (amber #ff9c28-#ffb43c) for value state; everything idle stays ~50% ice-grey hairlines.
- Communicate active/idle purely by grey-to-amber color swap plus bloom, never by resizing.
- Scale deformation/feedback amplitude with input velocity, not position.
- Let overlay data (fill lines, ticks) refract and sag through the deforming material instead of floating above it.
- Trail secondary motion 150-250ms behind the pointer; damp it fully within 600-800ms of rest.
- Give rotating reactive objects spring overshoot: ~1-2 oscillations, 500-700ms settle.
- Count numbers through real intermediate values and zero-pad to fixed width (005, 084, 100).
- Echo one value across 2-3 instruments (counter, dial, track) with slightly different lags per instrument.
- Frame interactive zones with 1px corner-only brackets (~40px arms) and small status dots that ignite on interaction.
- Ignite the pressed control in <100ms; relax it back to outline ~300ms after release.
- Script component demos as acts - fast pass, slow pass, scrub, decay to rest - and end on the exact idle frame for a seamless loop.
