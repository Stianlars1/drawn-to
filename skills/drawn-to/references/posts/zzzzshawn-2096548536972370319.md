## Transferable construction

Build an original orb with explicit idle/engaged/resolving or product-specific states. Separate silhouette, surface/volume field, lighting and state driver; state meaning should change density, speed or excitation coherently. Use one shared active renderer or strictly scoped rendering rather than dozens of uncontrolled loops. Preserve a composed poster and text label for each meaningful state.

## Limits and risks

The linked repository confirms React/TypeScript, WebGL and a shadcn registry, with named state inputs. Its runtime and some examples are MIT; 19 ported XorDev shaders use non-commercial attribution terms. Do not treat the whole collection as unrestricted reusable source. Shader equations are not inferred from the recording. Exact frame cost and broad browser support were not measured here.
