# Dark 3-step row: hairline diagrams, hover light

## What we see
A dark "How it works" section for a recruiting/lead-gen automation product ("Here's how it works – fully automated, end-to-end" / "Three modules that win new mandates for you around the clock"). Three equal columns — 01 Lead Sourcing, 02 Enrichment & Scoring, 03 Outreach — each headed by a bespoke vector illustration built from the same vocabulary: dark rounded app tiles, 1px hairline geometry, faint graph-paper patches, and magenta-violet light. The 16.7s video is a ScreenStudio-style recording: camera zooms in, a cursor visits each column left→right, each illustration assembles on approach, reacts to hover with colored light, then the camera pulls back to the full composed row. Tweet: "Minimal hover interactions, built w/ Claude Code."

## Layout & composition
- Values estimated for a ~1600px page (video is 800×450, zoom factor ~1.9–2.2x during close-ups).
- Section header is split: serif display headline left (2 lines, ~32px), 2-line gray sans kicker right-aligned at the far edge (~13px) — editorial tension across the full width.
- Below: 3 columns on a ~64px gutter grid. Each column = illustration zone (~360×260px, illustration vertically centered) → hairline divider is absent; instead a ~40px gap → "01" index (12px, #7d8288) → 15px medium title (#e8e6e1) → 3-line 13px body (#8b9096, line-height 1.55).
- Every illustration is hub-centric: the product's own logo tile sits at the exact center of all three diagrams; partners/satellites orbit or flank it. The brand is the constant, the context changes.
- Backgrounds: near-black #0e0f10; each illustration sits on its own confined graph-paper patch that fades radially — texture exists only where the story happens.

## Card anatomy
Not boxed cards — open columns. Anatomy of the three illustrations:
1. **Lead Sourcing — quincunx tile grid.** Middle row of 3 logo tiles (ClickUp, product hub, Airtable) connected by a 1px horizontal line, plus 4 EMPTY dark tiles offset above/below at the corners (implying more integrations/slots). Hub tile is ~1.15x larger and one lightness step brighter than satellites.
2. **Enrichment & Scoring — orbit.** Product hub tile at center; two concentric 1px rings (~diameters 150px and 230px); three circular satellite nodes (~36px, dark fill, 1px rim) riding the rings, each containing a four-diamond-dot "sparkle" glyph; a magenta→violet gradient arc segment travels the rings like a comet.
3. **Outreach — arch merge.** Row of 3 tiles (Salesforce, product hub, HubSpot); two 1px S-curves rise from the side tiles' tops, bend inward, and converge at a pointed apex above the hub — a gothic-arch/wishbone profile with a faint stem dropping to the hub.

## Type & color
- Headline: warm off-white serif (#e9e7e2), old-style/editorial cut, ~32px/1.3, sentence case with an en dash — the only serif on the page; everything else a neutral grotesque (Inter class).
- Text grays: title #e8e6e1, body #8b9096, index #7d8288, kicker #9aa0a6.
- Surfaces: tiles #1e2124 top → #17191c bottom (subtle vertical gradient, lit from above), 1px top rim highlight rgba(255,255,255,0.07), radius ~22% of tile size (~12px on ~52px tiles). Empty corner tiles one step darker (#16181a). Hub tiles one step lighter (#24272b).
- Hairlines: grid rgba(255,255,255,0.04–0.05) fine / ~0.08 major; orbit rings and connector curves #3c4044 at 1px.
- Accent = light, not surface: magenta-violet underglow (#d13ce0 → #8b5cf6 family, ~30–40% alpha, ~16–24px blur). Hover glyph hues: magenta #f261d4, blue #5aa2ff.
- The ONLY saturated fills are real brand logos at full color: ClickUp, Airtable, Salesforce blue cloud, HubSpot orange sprocket — chroma is delegated to third-party marks; the product itself stays white-on-charcoal (a two-shard "double flash" mark).

## Graphic language
- **One hairline drawing system.** Everything structural is a 1px stroke: graph-paper patches, orbit rings, connector line, arch curves. No filled illustration shapes exist except the tiles themselves. Depth comes from lightness steps + the top rim highlight — zero borders, zero drop shadows on shapes.
- **Graph-paper patches, two densities.** Fine ~8px cells with a stronger line every 4th (~32px), clipped to a rectangle (~360×260px) behind each illustration and faded radially to nothing before reaching the text. Near the glow the grid lines pick up the accent tint (light bleeds onto the paper).
- **Tiles as the atomic unit.** All three diagrams are built from the same rounded-square tile: logo tile (full-color third-party mark on dark), hub tile (white product mark, brighter, larger), empty tile (no mark, darker — a vacant slot implying extensibility). Consistent radius (~22%), consistent top-light gradient.
- **Orbit satellites.** Circular nodes (~36px) with a 4-diamond-dot sparkle glyph (3px dots in a plus arrangement) — idle #83888e, i.e. data points drawn as neutral "particles" until hover charges them with color.
- **Comet arc.** On the rings, a gradient arc spanning ~90–120° (#f542c8 → #8a5cf6) whose both ends fade to transparent — a moving highlight ON the hairline, exactly one accent element per diagram (same "one lit line among gray siblings" logic as the corpus's connector fans).
- **Arch connector.** The two outreach curves are not arcs of a circle but S-profiles: vertical out of the tile top, easing inward, meeting at a pointed apex — reads as two streams merging into one send channel above the hub.
- **Underglow, not overglow.** Light attaches to the BOTTOM edge of tiles as an elliptical skirt (like a device sitting on a lit surface) — the illumination metaphor is "powered on", and it is the sole hover affordance.
- **Logos as chroma quarantine.** Page chrome is fully grayscale; all hue lives in 4 third-party logos + one accent light family. The product mark is white — it owns brightness, partners own color.

## Motion timeline
t = frame/6. Camera moves are recording-tool (zoom ~1.9–2.2x, ease-in-out ~1.0–1.2s, pans ~800ms); page motion below.

**Build (one-time per column, triggered as the pointer first approaches / column enters focus):**
- 1.6s — col 1 hub tile fades+scales in (opacity 0→1, scale ~0.9→1, ~250–300ms ease-out); the logo mark leads the tile surface by ~1 frame.
- 1.8s — side logo tiles (ClickUp, Airtable) pop in ~150ms later; the 1px connector appears with a brief horizontal light-sweep flash along the row (a warm streak visible for ~2 frames).
- 2.1s — the 4 empty corner tiles fade in last (+300–450ms after hub). Total build ≈ 650ms, order = hub → satellites → frame.
- 5.8–6.4s — col 2 builds the same way as the cursor arrives: ghost of the hub mark (f_035) → tile + both rings fade/scale in (~300ms) → 3 orbit nodes pop with ~120ms stagger, gradient arc already traveling.
- 10.3–10.8s — col 3: hub tile first (already carrying a faint idle underglow), Salesforce + left curve +167ms, HubSpot + right curve +333ms; the HubSpot logo pops slightly oversized and settles (~10% overshoot, one oscillation).

**Ambient (persistent, strictly linear):**
- Orbit rotation: nodes ride the rings at ~15°/s (~24s/rev, clockwise), measurable across the entire clip — it keeps running through hovers, camera pans, and the final zoom-out; never pauses.
- The comet arc travels along the ring continuously at a similar angular rate; at idle it is the only lit element in col 2.
- Cols 1 and 3 are dead still at idle (hold state static) except a faint residual underglow beneath hub tiles.

**Hover (light only — no scale, no lift, no border):**
- 3.3s — cursor enters col 1 hub: elliptical magenta-violet underglow blooms at the tile's bottom edge, ~250ms ease-out in; adjacent tile glows follow at lower intensity. Glow FOLLOWS the hovered tile (moves to Airtable when the cursor slides right at 4.8s). Decay on leave ~500–600ms — ignites fast, cools slow, phosphor-style.
- 7.5–9.3s — col 2 hub hover: the three sparkle glyphs colorize (gray → 2× magenta #f261d4 + 1× blue #5aa2ff) in ~300ms while the arc brightens and the hub gains its underglow; on exit (~9.4s) glyphs desaturate back to gray in ~300ms, symmetric. Rotation speed unchanged — hover recolors, never accelerates.
- 11.3–13.5s — col 3 hub hover: hue-matched underglows per tile — violet under the hub, blue-tinted under Salesforce, orange-tinted under HubSpot; connector curves brighten one step. Each tile answers with its OWN brand color as light.
- 13.8s — cursor exits; glows decay over ~600ms; camera pulls back to the fully-assembled idle row where the orbit still turns.

**Easing character:** every entrance/hover is 250–350ms ease-out; exits/decays ~1.5–2x slower; ambient rotation and arc travel are pure linear; exactly one overshoot in the whole piece (HubSpot tile pop).

## Why saved
Owner note: loved the illustrations/vectors/paths — this kind of product showcase. What earns it: (1) three different feature metaphors (grid of sources, orbit of enrichment, merge of channels) are drawn from ONE atomic vocabulary — tile + 1px hairline + graph patch + light — so the row reads as a single system; (2) hover is reduced to a single device, brand-hued underglow, and it is enough — no scale, no borders, the restraint IS the premium; (3) chroma discipline: real partner logos are the only saturated fills, the accent exists purely as light (glow, comet arc, glyph charge); (4) register separation executed cleanly — the orbit never stops, never eases, while everything pointer-driven is short and eased; (5) the illustrations assemble on first approach in causal order (hub → satellites → frame) and then persist, so the section ends composed.

## Extractable rules
- Build all feature diagrams in a set from one atomic kit: rounded logo tile (radius ~22%, #1e2124→#17191c top-lit gradient, 1px top rim rgba(255,255,255,0.07)), 1px hairline geometry, confined graph patch, one accent light.
- Put the product's hub tile at the center of every diagram — one lightness step brighter and ~1.15x larger than satellites; give partners full-color logos and keep the product mark white.
- Make hover pure light: an elliptical underglow (~30–40% alpha, 16–24px blur) attached to the tile's bottom edge — no scale, no lift, no border change; ignite ~250ms ease-out, decay ~500–600ms.
- Hue-match the hover light to each tile's brand (violet = product, blue = Salesforce, orange = HubSpot) so the glow reads as the tile powering on, not a generic highlight.
- Lay graph-paper patches at two densities (fine ~8px cells, major line every 4th) at 4–8% white, clipped to a rectangle behind the illustration and faded radially before any text.
- Include 2–4 EMPTY dark tiles (one step darker, no mark) at the grid corners as vacant slots — extensibility implied, never labeled.
- Animate orbits at constant ~15°/s (≈24s/rev) and never pause, ease, or accelerate them — not for hover, not during camera moves.
- Put exactly one traveling gradient "comet" arc (~90–120° span, magenta→violet, both ends fading to transparent) on a hairline ring as the diagram's single ambient accent.
- Charge satellite glyphs with color on hover only (gray #83888e → magenta/blue, ~300ms, symmetric revert) — hover changes hue, ambient motion carries position.
- Assemble each diagram on first pointer approach in causal order — hub (250–300ms, logo leading surface by 1 frame) → satellite tiles (+150ms stagger) → frame/empty slots (+300–450ms) — then persist assembled.
- Flash a brief light-sweep along a connector line the moment it first draws, then drop it to a static 1px #3c4044 hairline.
- Draw merge/convergence connectors as two S-curves rising from satellite tops to a pointed apex above the hub (arch profile), not as radial spokes.
- Allow exactly one overshoot per scene, on a discrete pop (a tile landing, ~10%, one oscillation); everything else pure ease-out.
- Pair a serif editorial headline (left) with a small right-aligned sans kicker across the full section width; keep column text at 12px index / 15px title / 13px body in three grays.
