## What we see
A pricing section for a fictional product "Tired AI" on a warm cream page. Centre-stacked header: orange eyebrow PRICING, a two-line bold H1 with a designed break ("Simple plans / for a less tiring you."), a two-line grey sub, then a Monthly | Yearly segmented pill with a solid-orange "Save 20%" chip inside the Yearly half. Three equal cards: Basic $12, Pro $24 (peach MOST POPULAR chip, black filled CTA), Team $49. Each card: title, one-line tagline, price + "/ month", a hairline divider, 4-5 check rows, one full-width CTA. Below the cards a three-item trust row (line icon + bold label + grey sub) separated by two faint verticals. Two handwritten grey notes sit outside the grid - "Same tired AI. / More done." top-right above the mascot, "Less busywork. / More you." bottom-left under card 1 - each with a short slanted underline; three short strokes next to the mascot's hat act as a comic "attention" mark. The mascot is a rendered 3D character (orange box head with two black rectangular eyes, brown felt hat, shirt, backpack) standing BEHIND card 3 with its orange block hands draped over the card's top edge. Nothing on the page moves except the mascot, which lifts its head, ducks to look at the card, then rises to face the viewer, and the clip is played back in reverse to close the loop. The sibling DesignByMoein-2096289 is the footer of the same site and shares the cream, the orange and the mascot.

- Handwriting layer: grey (#6F6B68, ~45% ink, never black), stroke 2-3 px native, letters on a -5 to -7 deg baseline, underlines steeper at -15 to -18 deg, 2 lines each, placed only in the two vacant corners. ## Motion timeline
Only the mascot moves; rest-of-frame diff between consecutive extracted frames is 0.01-0.29 luma (JPEG floor). Mascot-region diff series is mirror-symmetric: f3/f34, f4/f33, f9/f28, f13/f24, f17/f20, f18/f19 pair at MAD 0.6-0.9 (noise), while the off-by-one pairing f9/f29 gives 14.3. So the export is a 89-frame forward clip (2.97 s) plus its reverse - a boomerang. Loop closes trivially (f1 vs f36 MAD 0.88).
Forward half (extracted frame = 5 native = 167 ms):

- f3-f7 (333-1000 ms): head lifts and straightens, hat top 199 -> 187, head-box top 238 -> 214; hands still.
- f7-f8 (1000-1167 ms): fast duck - head bows toward the card, orange x-min jumps 1447 -> 1469, hat top drops to 193 then 204, hands slide down the card face (orange bottom 379 -> 391).
- f8-f14 (1167-2333 ms): bowed hold with sub-pixel drift (diffs 2.6-5.3), hat top 204-207, hands at 397-399 - the pose where the arm shadow paints onto the card.
- f15-f17 (2333-2833 ms): rise to upright, hat top 202 -> 186, hands back to 381.

Reverse half plays the same beats backwards, so the character "un-ducks" and returns to the tilted rest. Timing is ease-in/out on every beat (2-3 frames of 12-20 diff bracketed by 5-9 diff frames); no linear register present.

## Extractable rules
1. Cream system: page #F6F2EF, card #F9F6F2 (+3 luma), 1 px rim ~#EEEDE8, shadow biased downward (~-12 luma at +3 px, gone by +12 px). Never a visible grey border.
2. Three equal cards, gap = 4.3% of card width, card aspect ~0.88, row centred; corner radius ~3% of card width; CTA radius ~0.8x the card radius; chips and toggle full pills.
3. Emphasise the recommended tier by exactly three moves: tinted chip (#F7DAD0 / #CB5436), dark CTA (#222222 / #FFF), one extra feature row. Same size, same fill, same border.
4. One commit ink: the selected toggle segment and the primary CTA share #222222. Secondary CTAs are a -16 luma tone step (#E9E8E4), no border, black label.
5. One accent hue at three tones - solid #DE6B48, text #C54913-#CB5436, tint #F7DAD0 - and let the mascot carry the hottest version (#F36D38) so the accent reads as the character's colour.
6. Card stack ratios: price digits = 1.75x title cap; title cap = 1.5x body cap; check rows at 2.3x body cap pitch; check ink darker than row ink (#181713 vs #4F4E4A), tick 17x13 at 3-4 px stroke (native).
7. Two insets per card: text at ~9% of card width, CTA and divider at ~7%; CTA spans 87% of the card.
8. Header rhythm: eyebrow -> H1 -> sub -> toggle at one 20-25 px gap (native), then a 3x jump to the cards' underside row. H1 two lines, short-over-long, tight leading (pitch 1.2x cap).
9. Ledge composite: place the character's body behind the card (clip at the card's top edge), draw its hands over the edge, and paint a soft shadow on the card under the arms (~5-14 luma, ~40 px tall) that moves with them.
10. Handwriting layer: grey ~#6F6B68 (never black), 2-3 px stroke, baseline -5 to -7 deg, underline -15 to -18 deg, two short lines, placed only in vacant corners outside the content column; one comic emphasis mark next to the character, static.
11. Loop a rendered character by boomerang: author 2.5-3 s of eased, held poses (rest 0.33 s -> lift 0.67 s -> duck 0.17 s -> hold 1.17 s -> rise 0.5 s -> hold), then append the reverse. Amplitude ~4% of card height; everything else on the page stays at zero motion.
12. Trust row: three items max, 32 px line icons at 3 px stroke, bold label + grey sub, 1-2 px verticals at -8 luma; 72 px (native) clear above it.

## Limitations and transfer risks
- The icon-and-label trust strip is supporting information, not a feature illustration. Check that any trust claim is accurate.
- Eyebrow above the H1: acceptable once per page; if the sibling footer or other sections repeat it, it becomes an eyebrow spray.
- The visually bolder heading, price and title voice is a valid choice; exact font weight is not recoverable from raster appearance alone.
