# SaaS hero with invoice-pipeline illustration (Veyro)

## What we see
A light-mode landing hero for "Veyro", an AI invoice-processing product ("Process invoice faster with AI"). Centered stack: pill badge → 2-line display heading → 2-line subhead → dark pill CTA → a white invoice table card → and below it a composed illustration where paper invoices flow through the Veyro logo chip via curved connector lines into that table. Tweet text is just "New website in the making 🚧" — the value is the composition itself.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: single flat ice-blue wash `#dbf3ff` edge to edge — effectively NO gradient on the body (sampled uniform across all corners; nav band is ~1% lighter `#ddf3ff`). All perceived depth comes from element lighting: white cards, soft shadows, dot-grid fade, paper fade-out.
- Values below assume a ~1440px design canvas (screenshot 2160px = 1.5x).
- Navbar: ~65px tall, transparent on the blue wash; logo left, 4 center links (16px medium, dark slate, dropdown chevrons), white pill "Get a Demo" right with hairline border + soft shadow.
- Vertical rhythm (centered column): badge (~24px pill) → 24px → heading (~72px/1.08, 2 lines) → ~28px → subhead (20px/1.6, max-width ~500px, 2 lines) → ~48px → CTA (187×44px pill) → ~80px → table card (~717×249px) → connector-fan zone (~120px) → chip (~85px squircle) overlapping the paper stack → papers fading out at viewport bottom.
- Table card: radius ~24px, borderless, large soft blue-tinted shadow (est. 0 24px 60px rgba(30,90,120,0.10)). Header row ~48px + 3 data rows ~69px each, full-bleed 1px dividers `#eef0f3`, ~22px side padding. 4 columns: invoice id / category chip / sender / status pill.
- Z-order storytelling: table card on top → connector lines behind card but over dot grid → chip over paper stack → papers over dot grid → dot grid over flat bg. Bottom-to-top read = physical invoice → through product → structured data.

## Card anatomy
Single UI card (the table), 100% data, no title — it IS the product shot:
- Column headers: 12px ALL-CAPS, tracking ~0.08em, `#9aa6ad`.
- Invoice id: 17px medium `#1d2b36`.
- Category chips: light-gray fill `#f4f3f1`, 1px border `#e4e6e8`, radius ~10px, 15px `#4a5560` text.
- Sender: ~34px circular avatar (`#e7e7eb` circle, monochrome navy `#33404f` glyph) + 17px medium name.
- Status pills: fully rounded, leading 6px solid dot, three semantic hues — orange `#fff3dc` bg / `#92400e` text (Need Approval), blue `#dbeaff` bg / `#1d4ed8` text (Auto-Categorized), green `#cef8e2` bg / `#15803d` text (Payment Ready). Dot is a saturated step of the text color.

## Type & color
- Face: neo-grotesque (Inter/Aeonik class), double-story a, tight display cut.
- H1: ~72px, weight 700, letter-spacing ~-0.02em, line-height ~1.08, near-black petrol `#0e2a38`.
- Subhead: 20px regular, desaturated blue-gray `#6e8895` — hue-matched to the bg, not neutral gray.
- Badge: white pill, tiny doc icon + 11px caps "INVOICE INTELLIGENCE", tracking ~0.1em, deep teal `#114769`, soft shadow.
- CTA: vertical gradient `#223c49` → `#082535` (lighter top = lit from above), white 18px semibold + chevron, wrapped in a ~3px pale outer ring (rgba(255,255,255,~0.55)) plus drop shadow — reads as a physical pressed-glass pill.
- Palette: bg `#dbf3ff`, ink `#0e2a38`, muted `#6e8895`, surfaces `#ffffff`, accent stream blue `#34a2d5`, semantic orange/blue/green pills. Contrast strategy: one dark petrol ink family against one pale blue field; pure white reserved for surfaces so cards pop without borders.

## Graphic language
The product idea (documents in → intelligence → structured data out) is drawn, not stated:
- Paper invoices: 3-4 white sheets, radius ~16-20px, each rotated 2-6°, edges separated by 1px light strokes and soft shadows. Surfaces carry a subtle monochrome film grain (~2-3% noise) so they read as physical paper against the perfectly clean UI card. Typography on the sheet is real mock content ("INVOICE", "Invoice #001", From/contact/date in gray) partially cropped by the fold.
- Page curl: the right sheet has a large diagonal fold; the folded triangle is a lighter tint `#f0f7ff` with its own rounded corner and cast shadow — a "document mid-turn / being ingested" cue.
- Fade-out mask: the whole paper stack dissolves into the bg toward the viewport bottom (linear alpha mask over the last ~40%) — no hard crop line.
- Logo chip: ~85px white squircle (radius ~26px) with drop shadow, pinned at the junction of papers and flow lines like a clip. Inside, the hexagon mark is rendered as brushed metal — vertical silver gradient (#a8a8a8 → #6e6e6e) with a white chevron cutout — while the same mark in the navbar is flat dark petrol. One logo, two material treatments.
- Connector fan: ~9 curved 1.5-2px bezier lines converging into the chip top and fanning out under the table card — cool gray `#ccd6e2`, with exactly ONE saturated sky-blue line `#34a2d5` as the "active stream" accent.
- Dot grid: ~2px square dots on a ~19px pitch, `#cde3f0` (≈7% darker than bg), confined to a band behind the illustration and radially faded — texture only where the story happens.
- Avatars: three different identity types drawn consistently (abstract company arch, real brand mark in rounded square, flat human bust with face as negative space) — all monochrome navy on the same gray circle.

## Why saved
Owner note: "hero section with title + description + CTA + pretty background gradient + graphic details/illustrations/vectors representing the product (Veyro). Extremely well composed by a design engineer. YOUR JOB: describe HOW the graphic elements are constructed, represented, and how they look."
Verified, with one correction: the background is a flat `#dbf3ff` wash, not a gradient — the "gradient feel" is manufactured by lighting (CTA vertical gradient, metallic logo, paper fades, dot-grid falloff). What earns the save: (1) the hero illustration is an argument, not decoration — z-order and vertical position literally diagram the pipeline (paper → chip → table); (2) material contrast as meaning: grainy, rotated, curled paper = the messy input, crisp borderless white card = the product's output; (3) one accent line among nine gray ones directs the eye and implies liveness without motion; (4) micro-consistency a generic page misses — subhead gray is hue-shifted toward the bg blue, pill dots are saturated steps of their text colors, the logo swaps material (flat nav / metal chip) instead of color, and every fade is masked rather than cropped.

## Extractable rules
- Set the hero canvas to one flat tinted wash (e.g. #dbf3ff) and create depth with element shadows, not bg gradients.
- Diagram the product pipeline vertically: input artifact at bottom, logo chip as the transformer, output UI card on top.
- Render input documents with 2-3% grain, 2-6° rotations, and a page curl; keep the output UI card perfectly clean — material contrast = before/after.
- Fade illustration stacks into the background with an alpha mask over the last 40%; never hard-crop.
- Fan 8-10 curved 1.5-2px connector lines in cool gray (#ccd6e2) and color exactly one accent blue (#34a2d5).
- Confine dot grids (2px dots, ~19px pitch, bg -7% luminance) to a radially faded band behind the illustration.
- Tint every neutral toward the bg hue: body text #6e8895 on blue bg, not #6b7280.
- Build status pills as bg-tint + dark text + leading 6px dot in a saturated step of the text color (orange/blue/green for pending/auto/done).
- Give the primary CTA a top-lit vertical gradient (#223c49→#082535), a ~3px 55%-white outer ring, and a soft drop shadow.
- Set the H1 at ~72px/1.08 with -0.02em tracking in near-black petrol (#0e2a38) matching the bg hue family.
- Label the section with a compact ~24px white pill: icon + 11px caps at 0.1em tracking in the deep brand hue.
- Reuse the logo mark in two materials — flat ink in the navbar, brushed-metal gradient on the illustration chip — instead of two colors.
- Keep borderless white cards at radius ~24px with a large low-opacity hue-tinted shadow (0 24px 60px rgba(30,90,120,0.10)).
