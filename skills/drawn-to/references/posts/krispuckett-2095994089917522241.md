## What we see
Two screen recordings from one post. The author's text: the agent (Astra) took his particle animation "to an incredible level" and, unasked, also produced a landing page he calls "the sloppiest slop to ever slop on front end design". Both claims test out.

**Video 1 - the studio.** A dark three-region tool: 56 px top bar ("NEUMA / Astra / Particle studio", Import, Export JSON, Save image), a 1207 x 640 px WebGL canvas, a 300 px inspector on the right (Study / Starting point / Form / Transition to / Wind direction / Formation sliders) and a 106 px transport bar (reset, play, Speed 2x, a phase-labelled progress track, Loop, "Space play / pause"). On the canvas a MacBook Pro built from 500k cyan-white particles sits on near-black with a sparse static star field. The user orbits it, presses Transition: the laptop releases into a bright turbulent ribbon, is blown right by the wind, gathers and settles as an iPhone; then back to a MacBook seen from inside the storm; then the preset switches to "Warm dust" and the whole system re-forms in amber. A live "500k particles · 47-60 fps" readout sits bottom-right of the canvas.

**Video 2 - the landing page.** Dark navy-black page, transparent nav, left-set hero: blush dot + mono eyebrow, a two-line 118 px H1 "It starts / with a word." with "word." in a pale steel-blue serif italic, a three-line sub, an off-white primary button and a text link, a "Created by" byline with a monogram avatar. Right half: a hollow grayscale particle shell that dissolves into a 25-degree wind streak and re-forms as an iPhone, then a MacBook, driven by a "01 MacBook / 02 iPhone / 03 Breath" switcher with "Pause motion". Below: "01 / THE IDEA", a LIGHT section "02 / YOUR FIRST CREATION" with a numbered list and an inverted dark example card, "03 / THE COURSE" with three stats, a creator portrait card, a $129 pricing card, an FAQ accordion and a viewport-wide "pneuma" footer wordmark.

## Graphic language
- The device is drawn as a particle SURFACE, not a mesh: a settled MacBook covers ~122k px above L50 in a 1130 x 580 canvas region; edges and creases read brighter because particle density stacks there, so there is no outline, no fill, no lighting model to fake. "Settle distance 0.055 / Front softness 0.3 / Edge irregularity 0.55" are the authored looseness.
- Background is a sparse static speck field (~0.13 % of canvas pixels above L40), never a gradient. Nothing on the canvas is a shape drawn by CSS.
- Transport track carries phase words under it ("Wind · Gather · Settle" while forming, "Release · Wind · Form" while transitioning) and a live percentage - the timeline is diegetic UI, not a decorative progress bar.
- During a transition the whole inspector dims (labels drop from #e8eced-class to ~#6b7074-class; "Transition ->" becomes "Transitioning..." in a dim well) - disabled state by opacity, nothing hidden (C11).
- Landing figure: the same particle system at render tier, grayscale, with three authored formations and a wind-streak dissolve at 25.4 degrees rising to the right (PCA on the streak pixels, aspect 2.45:1). The figure crosses the hero's bottom hairline and the right edge, so it reads as a field, not a boxed illustration.
- Section 02's example chat card is the only product fragment on the page; the rest of the sections are typography.

## Why saved
Saved without comment. The post is the owner's cleanest example of the two halves of agent output living in one artefact. The studio is a positive exemplar: a real render tier (500k GPU particles at an on-screen 47-60 fps), one hue per preset and zero chroma in the chrome, 4 px radii on every control, a phase-labelled transport, a panel that dims while the sim runs, and a transition whose timing you can read off the UI and verify on the clock (5.6 s, 2.75 s). The corpus had particle fields only as filmed or probed heroes (local-production-heroes); this is the first one with its parameters exposed, so the numbers are buildable.

The landing page is the counter-example, and the author's verdict survives measurement. What is good: the particle shell as a hero field with the type set directly on the page (no box), the 118 / 80 / 20 / 12 scale in one grotesque with one italic serif, the dark -> light -> dark section alternation with an inverted card inside the light band, hairlines at 0.10-0.13 white and a light-section list rule at 0.16 black. What sinks it: fourteen mono-caps labels across seven screens; one content model rendered four times (label left, label right, 80 px H2, one italic word, right-column body); the italic accent word in every headline; seven interactive elements in the first screen; a self-byline with monogram in the hero; a nav button border at 0.22 white. Status: partial - build the studio, do not build the page.

## Extractable rules
1. Particle device as render tier: draw the object as a density surface, ~100-120k lit pixels on a 1130 x 580 stage for a laptop, edges brighter only because density stacks; no outline, no fill, no lighting pass. Expose Settle distance 0.055, Front softness 0.3, Edge irregularity 0.55 as the looseness controls.
2. One hue per preset, carried only by the particles: cold #8ed7e1 -> #214454 -> #81a1b5 settled (hue 187-203), warm #91653a -> #452c1b -> #735c47 (hue 25-30). Chrome stays at chroma 0.
3. Transition grammar: Release (brightness burst, ~0.8 s) -> Wind (constant drift ~700 px/s in the panel's arrow direction, ~2.4 s) -> Form (settle, ~2.3 s). Total 5.6 s; label the phases under the progress track and show the live percentage.
4. Formation duration = authored Duration / transport Speed (5.5 s / 2x = 2.75 s, measured 2.7 s). Never hide the arithmetic from the UI.
5. Studio shell: canvas #050a0e, bars #121617, panel #262a2d, hairlines +9 to +14 luma, wells #1b1f22, no shadows; type 14 / 13 / 12 / 10 px, ink #e8eced / #a1a5a8 / #787c7d / #64686b; radius 4 on every control, 2 on selects; primary fills #c3ccd1-#ccd5da, never #fff.
6. While the sim runs, dim the whole inspector to ~45 % and swap the trigger label to its progressive form ("Transitioning..."); nothing disappears.
7. Field hero: the figure crosses at least two edges (right edge and the hero's bottom hairline); no container, no gradient behind it; a sparse speck field (<0.2 % coverage) is the only background texture.
8. Dissolve-through-zero morph: dissolve ~1.4 s into a 25-degree wind streak, hold ~0.2 s near-empty, re-form ~1.2 s (2.6-2.8 s total). Do not interpolate device A into device B.
9. Hero scale on dark: H1 ~118 px / 1.0 line-height (cap 82.5), sub 20 / 28, eyebrow 12 px mono caps at 8.5 px per character advance, primary 265 x 50 with radius 5 and fill #cbccc6, hairlines at 0.10-0.13 white.
10. One serif-italic accent word per PAGE, not per headline; here it is used six times and becomes the template.
11. Section labels: pick one ledger device ("02 / YOUR FIRST CREATION") and drop every free-floating mono eyebrow ("PERSONAL BY DESIGN", "THE INVISIBLE, MADE REAL", "YOUR NEXT CHAPTER" ...). Budget: one eyebrow in the hero, numbers elsewhere.
12. Light band inside a dark page: #e6ebe5 shell, ink #060f0c, list rules rgba(0,0,0,0.16), and exactly one inverted dark card (#1a2127, radius 10) carrying the product fragment.
13. First-screen action budget: one primary, one secondary. Remove the scroll cue, the byline, "Pause motion" and the device switcher from the fold, or fold the switcher into the figure.
14. Outline buttons on dark stay at <= 0.12 white; 0.22 (the nav CTA) is the mid-contrast border the ladder forbids (C3).
