# Dual shader-poster loop, dark editorial stage

## What we see
A 10.5s, 3840x2160@60fps loop of two animated poster panels sitting side by side on a flat #1f1f1f canvas. Left poster "+Liquid+ influence": a monochrome graphite liquid/satin shader with thin specular crests, continuously melting. Right poster "Llanberis drop": a slowly revolving blurred-gradient field cycling black -> steel blue -> cream white -> caramel, with a telemetry-style glyph line "++++ ---- +++" and a "Shader.labs EST 2X26.1." credit. Tweet text is just "+Llanberis+Drop*" - pure shader/poster exploration, no product.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: 3840x2160, solid #1f1f1f, no texture.
- Two identical poster panels: ~1070x1601px each - exact 2:3 poster ratio.
- Gap between panels: ~107px = 10% of panel width.
- Pair optically centered: side margins ~795-800px, top 276px, bottom 284px (near-equal, slight optical lift).
- Corner radius: ~16px at 4K (~1.5% of panel width - reads as 8px at 1x). Same on both panels.
- No borders, no shadows, no dividers - panels separate from canvas purely by luminance contrast; the left (dark) poster is held by its own internal speculars, not an outline.
- Text is inset ~75px (7% of panel width) from panel edges on all anchored corners.

## Card anatomy
- Both posters are ~92% visual field, ~8% typography; type never occupies a reserved band - it floats over the shader.
- Left poster: title block top-left ("+Liquid+" bold white over "influence" regular grey, zero extra line gap); circular wireframe compass/globe mark (~150px dia, ~2px strokes, white ~80%) bottom-left. Nothing right-aligned.
- Right poster: title top-left ("Llanberis drop") with a plus/hyphen glyph line directly beneath as a sub-label; two-line credit ("Shader.labs / EST 2X26.1.") bottom-right. Diagonal corner-to-corner text tension: top-left vs bottom-right.

## Type & color
- Grotesk throughout (Helvetica Now / Inter character). Hierarchy by weight and tone at a single size per block, not by size jumps.
- "+Liquid+": ~94px cap height at 4K (5.9% of panel height), weight ~700, #ffffff. "influence": same size, weight ~400, ~#9a9a9a - the wordmark/subtitle contrast is weight+value only.
- "Llanberis drop": ~47px caps (2.9% panel height), ~600, #ffffff; glyph line "++++ ---- +++" same size, wide tracking, reads as Morse/telemetry ornament echoing the tweet's "+Llanberis+Drop*".
- Credit: ~40px caps, two lines, right-anchored.
- Palette: canvas #1f1f1f; left poster graphite #17191a -> #56575d with white specular threads; right poster deep black #010304, steel blue boundary ~#5580a0 -> #9db4c6, cream core #eef0f2 -> #fcfcf3, caramel/bronze #6d5b4d -> #a08060 at light-dark boundaries.
- Contrast strategy: deliberately unstable - white type is allowed to sink into the passing light band (at t~5s "Llanberis drop" is half-erased). The bottom-right credit flips polarity against the gradient (measured dark #4c565b on light bg at t=0.1, light #fef5eb on dark bg at t=7.6) - a difference/exclusion-style blend softened by bloom, so it self-adapts instead of fading out.

## Graphic language
- Left: 3D-feeling viscous displacement shader - folded satin/tar with narrow (2-6px at 4K) specular highlights riding fold crests, soft SSS-like mid-grey slopes, near-black valleys. Entirely monochrome; reads as sculpted relief, not gradient.
- Right: heavily blurred rotating color field (classic "mesh/fluid gradient" shader) - no geometry, only mass and temperature; the blue appears only as a thin boundary phenomenon between black and cream, and caramel only where light decays into dark, mimicking chromatic bloom of real optics.
- The two panels are a thesis pair: same palette temperature, opposite rendering strategies (specular relief vs defocused field), unified by identical frame, type system, and canvas.
- No grain overlay detectable; softness comes from the shader blur + slight bloom, not noise.

## Motion timeline
Frames extracted at ~6fps (63/10.54s); t = frame/6. No cuts - one continuous take, both shaders run on linear shader-time (no easing curves, no stagger; ambient constant velocity).
- Right poster = one full revolution of the light mass in ~10.5s (~34 deg/s), seamless loop:
  - t0.0: dark mass fills upper-left ~60%; cream core bottom-right; blue S-boundary from top-right to bottom-left.
  - t0.7-2.0: boundary steepens; blue band saturates to azure ~#5580a0; light climbs the right edge.
  - t2.5: light engulfs bottom-right corner - credit text washes to near-invisible (measured contrast ~15% at t=2.5).
  - t3.3-4.2: bright band lies diagonal across the top; warm cream enters at top edge; header begins sinking into it.
  - t5.0: light column dominates center/right; dark reduced to bottom-left with blue fringe; "Llanberis drop" half-legible.
  - t5.8-6.7: field inverts - light mass now left/center, champagne tint; new dark seed grows top-right into a center-right blob.
  - t7.5: dark holds center-right; caramel underglow blooms across the bottom; credit text has flipped to light-on-dark and is fully legible again.
  - t8.3-9.2: dark takes the right half; bright diagonal re-enters from top-left; caramel at bottom-left boundary.
  - t10.0-10.5: bright band sweeps bottom-left -> top-right, returning to the t0 configuration.
- Left poster: continuous viscous melt - fold crests slide down-left at ~2-3% of panel height per second (~40-60px/s at 4K); topology self-similar noise, speculars migrate along crests; no detectable loop seam, no keyframes.
- Net effect: legibility itself is choreographed - each text block passes through a ~2s illegible phase and recovers, on opposite phases for header vs credit.

## Why saved
No owner note. This maps to the owner's shader-driven poster and editorial-dark lanes: it is a presentation template for shader art that a generic AI page would never produce. The craft moves that earn the save: (1) exact 2:3 poster ratio with a 10% gap and optically centered stage on flat #1f1f1f - gallery presentation, zero chrome; (2) hierarchy built from weight and grey-value at one size ("+Liquid+" 700 white over "influence" 400 grey, no size jump); (3) the credit text's polarity-flipping blend so it survives a rotating gradient without ever being animated itself; (4) the confidence to let the headline drown in the light band for ~2s and resurface - treating legibility as a phase of the loop, not a constraint; (5) blue and caramel existing only as boundary phenomena between black and cream, which is what makes the gradient read as light instead of colored paint; (6) two opposite shader idioms (specular relief vs defocused field) disciplined into one system by frame, type, and palette.

## Extractable rules
- Stage poster/shader work as 2:3 panels on flat #1f1f1f; gap = 10% of panel width; side/top/bottom margins near-equal (~74% of panel width per side).
- Use a small corner radius (~1.5% of panel width, ~8-12px at 1x) and no borders or shadows - let luminance separate panel from canvas.
- Build title hierarchy at one size: weight 700 #ffffff line over weight 400 #9a9a9a line, zero added line gap.
- Keep typography under 10% of the composition; anchor blocks to opposite corners (top-left title, bottom-right credit), inset 7% of panel width.
- Add a telemetry glyph line (e.g. "++++ ---- +++") under the title as a sub-label instead of a descriptive subtitle.
- Render gradient hues only at boundaries: steel blue ~#5580a0 where light meets dark, caramel ~#a08060 where light decays - never as flat fills.
- Drive ambient shader loops on linear time, one full field revolution per ~10s, seamless loop; no easing, no stagger.
- Give fixed text over moving gradients a difference/exclusion blend so it flips dark-on-light / light-on-dark instead of washing out.
- Allow transient illegibility: let a text block sink into the light for ~2s per loop and recover - motion reads as real light passing over print.
- Keep specular highlights on dark liquid shaders thin (2-6px at 4K) and white; valleys near-black #17191a, slopes #4a5055.
- Pair contrasting render idioms (relief shader + blurred field) under identical frame, palette, and type to read as a series.
