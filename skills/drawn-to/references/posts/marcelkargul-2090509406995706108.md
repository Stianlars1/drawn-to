# Kargul Studio CTA - wireframe dome on isometric graph paper

## What we see
Light closing CTA: "Let's build something amazing" over a geodesic wireframe
dome with meridians and a stipple cut-away wedge, iso primitives around it
(stairs, cylinders, lego brick, pie wedge, arc ring), dashed bezier orbit
lines with dot nodes, selection handles on bounding boxes, graph-paper grid;
two pills (dark + white), footer row.

## Layout & composition
Centered H1 ~72px/700; the illustration spans the full width below it;
pills centered under; footer meta line. Paper #EDEDED, lines #c9c9c9-class.

## Graphic language
Iso line-art (2:1), faces with soft white→#e6e6e6 gradients (the "soft
gradients" that make it feel like a product), stipple interiors, dashed
guides overshooting to the edges, Figma-style handles. One material system
continued from the dark Services bento, inverted to paper.

## Why saved
Owner: "the CTA illustration is even better" - the iso vocabulary survives
the light inversion with soft gradient faces adding volume.

## Extractable rules
1. Invert the blueprint system to paper: outlines #c9c9c9, faces white→#e6e6e6.
2. Soft per-face gradients 1-2% give volume without shadows.
3. Dashed orbit curves + dot nodes tie primitives into one scene.
4. Keep Figma chrome (handles, bounding boxes) as the ornament vocabulary.
