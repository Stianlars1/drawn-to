## What we see

A pale gray gridded page with small nav, a left-centered headline and compact actions. A broad reflective ribbon sweeps diagonally through the hero. Below it, three dark feature cards carry different dot-based objects. The hero material is colored; the explanatory objects below are monochrome.

## Layout and material

The ribbon is a continuous form with narrow necks, wide bends, dark folds and sharp specular regions. Magenta, violet, blue, cyan, green and near-white appear as reflections across that one material rather than as multiple UI accents. A repeated screen/dot texture gives the form a different reading from a soft mesh blob. The dark lower cards provide a strong compositional stop after the luminous hero.

## Motion evidence

The 2340x2160 source is about 15.15 s at 60 fps. Uniform samples show the ribbon deforming from a rising diagonal into arch-like shapes; the lower dot objects also change. The text does not animate with the ribbon. Shader geometry, live versus prerendered delivery, exact loop period and easing are unknown from this export.

## Why it works

One large material provides spectacle, while restrained UI and a monochrome lower row preserve hierarchy. The three dot silhouettes share a construction vocabulary but differ in form, giving the row cohesion without using the same object three times. The ribbon's dark folds make the highlights appear brighter; uniformly bright color would flatten it.

## Transfer into a website

Design the ribbon as an original material with a silhouette, folds and an explicit path through the layout. Judge readable text against several extreme frames, including the bright region passing behind the heading. Lock a still fallback and choose a render tier after deciding whether the camera or the shape actually needs live interaction. Lower feature objects must argue the product's real claims; attractive dot geometry alone does not establish meaning.

## Limitations and transfer risks

The moving ribbon crosses the headline in some samples, so legibility needs improvement in a new implementation. Do not infer a grain percentage from a compressed frame. The screenshot does not prove that lower abstract objects meaningfully demonstrate the adjacent features.
