# Gooey confirm-to-delete button morph

## What we see
A single circular delete button (white disc, red trash icon) on a flat light-gray canvas. On click it liquid-morphs - through a metaball "dumbbell" neck - into two fully-rounded pill buttons: red-text "Confirm" on the left, black-text "Cancel" on the right. Clicking Cancel absorbs both pills back into the original circle through the same gooey merge. The 14s clip loops the cycle 5 times with an oversized black macOS cursor demonstrating hover and click states.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: 960×960 (frames analyzed at 800×800; values below in 800-space, ×1.2 for native). Background flat #F7F7F7, zero chrome - component isolated dead-center.
- Idle circle: ~128px diameter, centered at (~398, 400).
- Expanded state: two pills, each ~278×130px, fully rounded (radius = 65px = height/2), gap ~22px between them; the pair (~578px total) is centered on the circle's original center, so both pills travel outward symmetrically.
- Borders: none - separation is done entirely with a soft drop shadow (~0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06), edge tone #EBEBEB) plus a faint top-light gradient inside the fill (#FAFAFA top → #F6F6F6 bottom), giving a soft extruded "keycap" read.
- Cursor rendered as an oversized (~56px) black arrow with white outline and drop shadow - part of the composition, not an afterthought.

## Card anatomy
Single component, three states:
- Idle: white circle, centered 40px-ish red trash glyph. Icon-only, no label.
- Armed (expanded): text-only pills. Confirm = red text on near-white; Cancel = black text on near-white. Destructive option carries color in the TEXT, not a filled red button - restraint move.
- Hover states: circle fill blooms to pink #FACDD2 (icon stays red); Cancel fill darkens to #EDEDED. Confirm shows no sampled hover in the clip.

## Type & color
- Labels ~34px (800-space) semibold/bold sans (SF-Pro-like), sentence case, generous side padding (~70px) inside pills.
- Palette: bg #F7F7F7; button fill #F9FAFA (gradient #FAFAFA→#F6F6F6); brand red #E0113A (sampled #CE143B–#C8052B range); pink hover #FACDD2; Cancel hover #EDEDED; text black #0A0A0A; shadow #EBEBEB.
- Contrast strategy: near-monochrome grays 2–4% apart carry all structure; the single red hue does all the signaling (icon, Confirm label, hover pink is red at ~20% saturation on white).

## Graphic language
No illustration - the component IS the graphic. The mechanism is a metaball/goo morph: during split and merge the two shapes stay connected by a liquid neck (classic SVG goo: feGaussianBlur + feColorMatrix contrast, or a canvas/shader equivalent). Content gets a velocity-scaled blur: labels smear directionally (motion blur) while moving, the trash glyph gaussian-blurs during the morph and snaps crisp ~100ms after the shape settles. Blur amount visibly tracks speed - heaviest mid-transition, zero at rest.

## Motion timeline
Extraction: 85 frames / 14.12s ≈ 6.02 fps (≈166ms/frame). One representative cycle:
- t=0.00–0.33s - idle circle; cursor approaches; on hover the fill crossfades white→pink #FACDD2 in ≤150ms.
- t=0.50s - click. Fill snaps back toward white in the same frame; "Confirm" ghost already sliding rightward out from behind the icon (content leads the shape).
- t=0.50–0.85s - split (~350–450ms, fast ease-out / softly-damped spring, no overshoot): circle stretches into a dumbbell; Cancel pill exits right through a metaball neck with its label almost immediately crisp; Confirm lobe stays heavily blurred longer - asymmetric focus that pulls the eye to the safe option first.
- t=1.0s - both pills settled; residual text ghost fades over ~150ms.
- t=1.0–1.66s - cursor travels to Cancel; hover darkens fill to #EDEDED (~100ms).
- t≈1.75s - click Cancel → merge (~330–450ms, ease-out): right pill is absorbed leftward through the goo neck; labels smear into a pink streak; trash icon reassembles from blur.
- t≈2.2s - settled idle circle, crisp icon.
The clip repeats this cycle 5× (splits at ~0.5s, ~5.1s, ~7.6s, ~9.6s, ~12.2s; merges at ~1.8s, ~4.1s, ~6.5s, ~11.0s, ~13.9s) and ends on the idle circle for a seamless loop. Merge is consistently ~20% faster than split.

## Why saved
No owner note; catalogued as "confirm-to-delete interaction." Tweet text: "confirm to delete interaction ⎯⟡° by @nitishkmrk" (posted by @_heyfaisal, original by @nitishkmrk). The save fits the owner's taste for motion that SHOWS a feature: destructive-action confirmation expressed as physics instead of a modal. The craft moves a generic build would miss: (1) the metaball neck - buttons don't just slide apart, they tear apart like liquid, selling one-object continuity; (2) velocity-coupled blur on content, with the Cancel label sharpening before Confirm so the safe path reads first; (3) content leading shape - the label starts escaping the circle a frame before the shape deforms; (4) danger encoded as red TEXT on white, saving the filled-pink state for hover feedback; (5) a shadow-only elevation system on a 2%-delta gray palette; (6) confirm UI that appears in-place at the click point, no overlay, no repositioning cost.

## Extractable rules
- Morph destructive icon-buttons into inline Confirm/Cancel pills in place; never open a modal for a single-item delete.
- Keep one continuous shape: split/merge via goo filter (feGaussianBlur stdDeviation ~8 + feColorMatrix contrast) so a liquid neck connects the states.
- Split in 350–450ms ease-out; merge back ~20% faster (300–400ms).
- Scale blur with velocity: 0 at rest, peak (~8–12px) mid-transition, crisp within ~100ms of settling.
- Let the safe option (Cancel) resolve to sharpness before the destructive option.
- Start content motion 1 frame before container deformation - text slides out from behind the icon.
- Fully round pills: radius = height/2 (65px at 130px height); expand symmetrically from the trigger's center.
- Signal danger with red text (#E0113A) on near-white fill; reserve filled pink (#FACDD2) for the hover state only.
- Elevation without borders: fill 1–2% lighter than bg (#F9FAFA on #F7F7F7) + soft drop shadow (0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)).
- Neutral hover = 4% darker fill (#F9F9F9 → #EDEDED), 100–150ms crossfade.
- Center the expanded button pair on the original trigger center so the cursor stays within reach of both options.
- Keep hover feedback ≤150ms and separate from the morph so states never queue.
