# Per-feature instrument illustrations, one identity product

## What we see

Four sections of the same identity-verification product: a light two-card
feature pair, a dark document-catalog section, a dark 3-step "how it works",
and a dark 6-card bento. Every feature gets a bespoke illustration whose
composition literally performs the feature's claim. Owner note: layout/style
is secondary here — the point is HOW WELL each illustration fits its feature,
and that the skill should be able to propose and build graphics at this level.

## The fit-method (why each graphic matches its card)

- **Instant Verification** → phone skeleton + floating dark app tile with a
  glowing green ring aperture + "Verifying.." spinner pill + green halo. The
  claim "in seconds, no friction" is shown as the app mid-verify.
- **Built-In Security** → same phone skeleton, blue lock pill, two stacked
  alert chips ("Fraud Protection — Risk signals monitored in real time",
  "Encrypted Data — 256-bit protection active"). Claim + orbiting proof.
- **Verify more than just your face** → depth-stacked document cards (Travel,
  Financial Credentials, Government IDs) receding in opacity/blur, each with
  title + mono sub ("190+ Regions Supported") + icon chip + skeleton rows.
  Breadth expressed as a physical stack.
- **Face Scan** → squircle scan tile w/ silhouette + "Scanning.." caption +
  masked mono ID string with a caret scan-line (Kh12k9|8Sa1K45L).
- **AI Verification** → screwed hardware plate w/ blue spark glyph; three
  check chips above (Identity Matching / Fraud Checking / Confidence Score);
  circuit traces fanning out.
- **Instant Confirmation** → large circular button/coin w/ green check,
  circuit traces with node dots entering both sides — the signal terminates.
- **Biometric Precision** → glowing cyan face silhouette in a screwed tile,
  bracket plates left/right.
- **No documents, no problem** → vertical mini-flow: avatar tile → browser
  card → result pill, joined by a thin spine.
- **Instant Verification (bento)** → speedometer dial, segmented ring, seven-
  segment LCD "00:10 Max Speed", a cable entering and leaving the gauge.
- **Encrypted Infrastructure** → shield inside a knob, cyan tick-arc
  progress, seven-segment band "SECURED 15%".
- **Global Verification** → dotted-pixel hemisphere globe with avatar
  location pins (London, UK / Sydney, AU) under an arc.
- **Peace of Mind** → radar/sonar arcs around a central avatar puck + mono
  key-value chip (name Jhon / location Sydney, AU) — "know where your data
  is" drawn as a radar you own.

## Construction system (what makes it cohere)

- Material rhetoric matches the brand promise: a SECURITY product drawn as
  trustworthy HARDWARE — screwed plates, brackets, cables, dials, LCD
  readouts, coins/buttons. The metaphor system is one decision, reused.
- Surfaces are 2-5% lightness steps on near-black (#101318-class), inset top
  highlights, zero hard borders; light mode uses the same skeleton language
  on #F5F7F9 with soft shadows.
- One accent (cyan/#38BDF8-class) + semantic green/blue/orange; the accent
  marks the LIVING datum (face glow, tick arc, pins), never decoration.
- Data voice: mono + seven-segment/LCD type for all readouts; skeleton bars
  for every non-essential row; product-true fiction (9:41, 190+ regions,
  masked ID strings, real city names).
- Composition per card: ONE hero object, evidence chips orbiting it, faint
  circuit/frame traces tying it to the card edges; generous void.

## Why saved

The owner loved how each illustration is inseparable from its feature — you
could delete every caption and still assign each graphic to its claim. This
is C2 (show the feature) executed at Figma-illustration grade rather than
UI-fragment grade: the fragments are ABSTRACTED into instruments while
keeping product truth in the data.

## Extractable rules

1. Derive the metaphor from the feature's VERB (verify→aperture mid-scan,
   speed→speedometer, encrypt→shield knob, global→pinned globe,
   control→radar), never from its noun or an icon.
2. One hero object per card; proof orbits as 1-3 evidence chips with real
   microcopy.
3. Choose ONE material system for the whole set and restate it every card
   (here: engineered hardware — screws, plates, cables, LCD).
4. Reserve the accent for the living datum; grayscale everything else.
5. Readouts in mono/seven-segment; filler as skeleton bars; data stays
   plausible and product-true.
6. Depth by lightness steps + inset highlights + soft glow — no hard borders
   inside illustrations.
7. Connect hero objects to the card with faint traces/spines so they sit IN
   the card, not pasted on it.
