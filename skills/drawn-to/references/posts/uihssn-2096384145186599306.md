## Layout and material

These differ structurally, not just by color. The image-first version gives artwork the top of the screen; the split version separates reading from product evidence; the centered version uses quiet repeated icons as context; the photographic version makes the background the field. Serif/italic words act as a second typographic voice in several studies. Photography, pixel processing and real product fragments provide three distinct graphic sources.
