## Graphic language
Glass/light metaphor, no literal iconography. The ring is not a flat conic gradient: it reads as light refracting through a glass meniscus. Construction details a generic recreation would miss:
- 3–4 hue arcs of unequal length that stretch and compress as they travel (the magenta arc spans ~90–120° and breathes), so the ring never looks like a rigid rotating cone-gradient.
- 1–2px hairline filament streaks inside the band (visible at top in f_035/f_045, bottom in f_110) - glass-caustic lines sheared at a slightly different rate than the main band, adding fluid-dynamics texture.
- Heavy gaussian softening (~5–7% of diameter, est. 14–20px at 288px) on the band, but the inner edge of the ring stays crisper than the outer bloom - an asymmetric blur that produces the "liquid inside a shell" read.
- Interior is not flat white: a very low-amplitude radial gradient plus directional hue bleed from the rim keeps the sphere volumetric.

## Extractable rules
- Center a hero orb at ~36% of canvas width on pure #FFFFFF; let a soft bloom (not a shadow) anchor it.
- Confine all saturation to a rim annulus at 0.78R–0.98R; keep the interior within 2–3% of white (#FCF9FF).
- Build the ring from 3–4 unequal hue arcs (violet #8E0EF7, magenta #E921B8, raspberry #F3447B) that stretch/compress ±25% as they travel - never fixed gradient stops.
- Rotate ambient loops at constant velocity (linear easing); reserve easing for enter/exit, never for infinite loops.
- Target ~18 deg/s (one revolution per 20s) for "alive but not spinning" ambient motion.
- Make loops seamless: last frame must equal first frame exactly; design the hue layout as a closed cycle.
- Blur the band at 5–7% of orb diameter, but keep the inner rim edge crisper than the outer bloom falloff.
- Add 1–2px lighter filament streaks inside the band, animated at a slightly offset rate to the main rotation, for fluid texture.
- Let the near-white interior inherit a directional tint (#F1EDFE cool / #FEF2FC warm) from whichever hue arc is nearest.
- Modulate band thickness (15–22% of R) with the passing hot arc so the ring breathes instead of staying geometric.
- Fade the outer bloom to background over ~2–7% beyond R (#F5A3B9 → #FFFFFF) for the backlit-glass read.
- When a motion asset will be needed in variants, build a parameterized generator (hues, speed, arc count) instead of hand-animating one-offs.
