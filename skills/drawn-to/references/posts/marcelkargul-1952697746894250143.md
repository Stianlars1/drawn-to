# Chatsheet hero - soft-shaded isometric conveyor

## What we see
Light SaaS hero: serif H1 "Turn Manual Tasks into Automations", two pills,
and an isometric scene - a blue concentric-ring core disc on an iso plane,
integration app tiles (Slack, Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Notion…) scattered on
dotted paths converging into the core, and a conveyor rail of iso cards
("Automation Action", "Strategy Contract", "Personalized Outreach") sliding
up-right from the core.

## Layout & composition
Centered copy column; illustration occupies the right/lower 60%, bleeding
off the right edge; soft blue radial under the core is the only strong
chroma.

## Graphic language
Soft-shaded iso: white→#eef1f5 faces, blue-tinted contact shadows, 1px
edges at ~8% navy, app tiles as small iso slabs; one chroma hero (blue
core + blue pills), everything else near-white.

## Motion timeline
Conveyor cards advance along the iso axis at constant velocity (linear
ambient, period = one card pitch); tiles bob ±4px slowly; dotted paths
static; loop closes on the card pitch. Frames 1→60 show steady advance,
no easing, no entrance choreography (C12).

## Why saved
Owner loved the isometric details; verified: the iso scene IS the flow
(inputs → core → outputs) and motion enacts it (C2/C6).

## Extractable rules
1. Soft-shaded iso: faces white→light-gray, shadows tinted to the accent.
2. One chroma object; integrations as neutral iso tiles on dotted paths.
3. Conveyor motion is linear and loops on item pitch.
4. Serif display over a modern iso scene = editorial × product contrast.
