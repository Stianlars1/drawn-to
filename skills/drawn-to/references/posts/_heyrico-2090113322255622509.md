# Micro illustrations for product-design feature cards

## What we see
Five uniform rounded cards on a white canvas (3 top + 2 bottom, bottom row centered), each a self-contained micro illustration for an AI customer-support product ("conduit.ai"). Every card pairs a heavily blurred macro flower photo (orange bloom on cyan/periwinkle sky) with one oversized, cropped white UI fragment: an app-icon carousel, stacked knowledge-source pills, an AI chat window, a notification stack, and AI tag chips. Zero captions — each illustration communicates one feature purely through composition.

## Layout & composition
- Canvas: white (#ffffff), 4000x2664 export; cards ~1030x660 px each (≈515x330 @1x), aspect ≈14:9, all five identical size.
- Grid: 3-up top row, 2-up bottom row optically centered under it; horizontal gap ≈165 px (@2x) ≈ 16% of card width; vertical row gap ≈170 px.
- Card radius ≈72 px @2x (≈36 px @1x, ~7% of card width); no border, no visible card shadow — cards sit flat, texture does the separation.
- Inner UI elements deliberately bleed off card edges (icons cropped both sides in card 1, pills cropped right in card 2, chat window cropped right+bottom in card 3) — implies continuation beyond the frame.
- Repeating compositional grammar: 3-of-5 cards use a 3-item diagonal stagger (each row offset ~90-110 px further right, or overlapped iOS-stack style).

## Card anatomy
Visual-to-text ratio is 100:0 at the card level — no titles or descriptions outside the artwork; all text lives inside the fake UI.
1. Channels: 5 white squircle tiles (~200x200 @2x, radius ≈28%) with real brand logos (Messenger, Slack, WhatsApp, two cropped) in a horizontal rail bleeding off both edges — reads as infinite integrations carousel.
2. Knowledge sources: 3 full-round white rows (~140 px tall @2x), leading icon (Google Drive, Notion, globe) + 600-weight label, each row indented ~100 px further right and cropped off the right edge.
3. AI chat: layered sheets — top bar "AI Agent" on a near-white sheet, inner white sheet with ✳ asterisk avatar + gray reply, and one dark charcoal (#333333) user bubble (radius ≈48 px @2x) overlapping bottom-right; whole window bleeds off two edges.
4. Live inbox: iOS-style notification stack — front row fully visible (avatar 44 px, name 600-weight, message 400-weight, colored channel dot right-aligned), two rows behind peek out ~70 px each, progressively narrower.
5. AI tags: 3 pill chips (~104 px tall @2x, full radius) with four-point sparkle ✦ + label, placed on a descending left→right diagonal staircase.

## Type & color
- Typeface: SF Pro / Inter-class grotesque; labels ~48 px @2x (≈24 px @1x) weight 500-600 in #333–#424242; secondary text weight 400 in ~#555; no other hierarchy needed.
- Backgrounds, one dominant hue field per card: sky cyan #77dbff (1), lavender→blue gradient #e2bde9→#add9f6 (2), teal #7dd4dd (3), periwinkle #86abd5 (4), cyan-teal #6ac7d8 (5).
- Shared warm accent across all cards: blurred flower oranges #eea733 / #fdb772 with pink #d8a8c8 and deep red #ad3b3a shadows — complementary warm-vs-cool tension.
- White (#ffffff) UI at maximum contrast on top; one single dark anchor in the whole set (charcoal chat bubble #333).
- Soft diffuse shadow under white fragments only: ≈0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.10).

## Graphic language
Feature ideas are depicted as oversized cropped UI fragments floating over photographic blur fields — not icons, not full screenshots. The blur base is a macro flower photo pushed past recognition (gaussian ≈40-80 px), then re-textured so it doesn't read as a sterile mesh gradient: card 1 gets a stochastic dissolve-speckle grain at the flower edges, cards 4 and 5 get a woven halftone/CRT mesh grid (visible ~4 px pixel lattice), cards 2-3 get fine film grain. Real brand logos (Messenger, Slack, WhatsApp, Drive, Notion) are used at full color inside white containers for instant recognition. Fake-UI copy is short and diegetic ("agent is replying...", "Angry tone") so the fragment itself narrates the feature.

## Motion timeline
Static image — no motion. (The stagger/stacking compositions are pre-choreographed for an obvious entrance animation: 3-item cascade, ~80-120 ms stagger, ease-out, if ever animated.)

## Why saved
Owner note: "micro illustrations for product-design showcase. Liked: simple presentation and good compositions."
Verified. The craft that earns it: (1) strict series discipline — five cards share identical geometry, one flower motif, one white-UI system, differing only in hue field and texture, so the set reads as one brand; (2) one idea per card with zero caption dependency; (3) edge-bleed cropping that keeps fragments oversized and legible instead of shrinking whole screenshots; (4) grain/dissolve/halftone texturing on top of the blur — the exact step a generic AI-generated gradient card skips, and what makes these feel printed rather than rendered; (5) a repeated diagonal 3-item stagger acting as a compositional signature; (6) exactly one dark element in the whole set as a contrast anchor.

## Extractable rules
- Keep every card in a set the same size (~14:9) and radius (~7% of width, ≈32-36 px at 500 px wide).
- Build each illustration as one oversized UI fragment over a blurred photo field; never a full shrunken screenshot.
- Crop fragments off 1-2 card edges to imply continuation and force scale.
- Limit each card to exactly one idea and max 3 repeated elements.
- Assign each card one dominant hue field; share one warm accent motif (same photo) across the whole set.
- Layer texture over the blur — film grain, dissolve speckle, or ~4 px halftone mesh — so gradients read as material, not render.
- Use real brand logos inside white squircle tiles (radius ≈28%) for integration stories.
- Repeat a 3-item diagonal stagger (~50 px x-offset per step at 1x) as the set's compositional signature.
- Keep UI text at one size (~24 px @1x), weights 400/600 only, colors #333 and #555.
- Allow exactly one dark (#333) element per set as contrast anchor; everything else white on pastel.
- Shadow only the white fragments: 0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.10); leave card containers flat and borderless.
- Write diegetic microcopy inside fake UI ("agent is replying...") instead of captions under the card.
