## Why saved
Saved without comment (the post is the author's "Front View." of the SCI-FI HERO DESIGN scene).

It earns a place for three measurable things the corpus lacked. First, the accent is the headline: the corpus keeps chroma in chrome (C1) and hierarchy in gray (C4); here the single amber #F9AF2F is spent on a 56 px uppercase H1 while every control stays gray or white, and it still reads as one accent because the tile and the active tab are the only other carriers. Second, the depth echo - a 0.90-scaled, blurred, grayscale, 4-8% copy of the entire UI layer behind itself - is a way to give a flat dark page depth that uses no shadow (C3) and no bloom (F8); no existing reference has it. Third, the diagram half is a product-truthful mechanism (Cluster / Graph / Field / Token / Recall are the memory primitives the H1 promises) drawn with F4 vocabulary at measured values: pin pitch 16.5, two-tier leaders where only the last 40-50 CSS px go white, T-bar terminals, node dots, a selection frame around the logo.

Partial exemplar. The right panel ships two identical primary buttons plus an install command plus "Try it" in the nav - four calls to action in one view against a budget of two - and the flat front view leaves 15% of the canvas empty at the right, a 3D-scene framing decision that a page would not make. The mechanisms are exemplary; the CTA budget and the framing are not.

## Extractable rules
- Ground #070707; every structural line is one hairline token at +16 luma (#171717, 1 px, rgba(255,255,255,0.08)); no shadow anywhere on the dark surface.
- Draw the hero panel as rails, not a card: two full-height verticals ~716 CSS apart and three horizontals at 120 / 580 / 1143; let the verticals overshoot the bottom rule by 40 and put a 2-3 px white node dot on every junction; keep the panel fill identical to the page.
- Inset all content 36 CSS from the rails on both sides; stack eyebrow -> H1 -> sub -> buttons on 48 / 48 / 52 CSS gaps.
- One accent, #F9AF2F, carried by exactly three things: the logo tile (36 px square), the active tab label, and the H1 itself. Every control label is white or the secondary gray.
- Three text grays only: #FFFFFF primary, one secondary at ~70% white (#ADADAD +/-6) for tabs, eyebrow, sub and secondary buttons, and #202020 for diagram labels that should be found, not read.
- H1: uppercase, 56 px, weight ~600, line-height 1.31, no added tracking, two designed lines of <= 18 characters, set in the accent. Eyebrow at the same size as the UI type (21-22 px caps), one per page, 48 above the H1.
- Sub 22/33, two lines of <= 60 characters, secondary gray.
- Primary button: 56 tall hairline box, 25 px horizontal padding, no fill, plus the registration kit - four 1.5 px white corner dots, a 9 px dash inside top-left, a 6x6 L inside top-right, a 6 px tick inside bottom-right, a 12 px underline 4 px below the bottom-left corner outside the box. Secondary button: plain hairline box 60 tall, gray label, chevron, 24 px gap - hierarchy by ornament, not fill.
- Install bar: 56 tall hairline box, mono, the package manager word in secondary gray and the command in white; 19 px below it a 1.5 px white rule 108 long from the left inset with a 6 px tick at the far right.
- Dot grid as a zone: 2 px #6E6E6E dots at 26 px pitch, confined to one band between two rules, never behind type.
- Chip map: bodies at #111111-#191919 fill steps, pins 6x12 at 16.5 pitch in #181818 on all four sides, labels in mono; leaders orthogonal with one bend, gray hairline for the run and #FFFFFF only for the final 40-50 px before the target, starting at a node dot and ending in a T-bar. Three white segments per diagram, no more.
- Depth echo: duplicate the whole UI layer behind itself, `transform-origin` at the panel's left rail and vertical middle, `scale(0.9)`, `filter: blur(2.5px)`, grayscale, opacity so white text peaks at 4-8% of white. No shadow, no bloom; the echo is the depth cue.
- Wrap the logo tile in a 73 px hairline square with 8 white handle dots (corners + midpoints) - the selection frame is the ornament.
- CTA budget still applies: one primary, one secondary. Do not repeat "Get Started" twice in one panel and do not add a third action in the nav.

## Limitations and transfer risks
- Four calls to action in one view: "Try it" (nav), "Get Started" (top), the install command, "Get Started" again plus "Documentation" (bottom). Quality bar budget is one primary + one secondary; the primary label is literally duplicated.
- Both primary buttons carry the identical ornament kit, so the device is used twice on one screen; keep the kit for the one true primary.
- Front-view framing leaves 611 native px (15%) of dead black at the right; the panel is not centred in the canvas. A scene decision, not a page decision - do not copy it.
