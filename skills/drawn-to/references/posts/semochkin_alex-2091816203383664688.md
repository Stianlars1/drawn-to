## Composition, material and visual weight

The useful method is a controlled relationship between an authored path, dense sampled points and secondary connecting lines. Line continuity, particle distribution and the absence of spills at boundaries matter more than raw particle count. The transition must resolve cleanly into an intentional static composition.
