## What we see

## Layout and material

The paired composition connects the expressive hero object to its practical destination. Large objects use thickness, rounded edges, transmission and soft grounding shadows. Small icons retain the outline/negative-space identity with much simpler flat fills. Color groups the functions; shared corner geometry and cuts keep the family recognizable.

## Motion evidence

The six-second 1600x1200 clip has 180 frames at 30 fps. Objects rotate and travel through the left slot while right-hand panels change. Rotation helps reveal depth; it is not evidence that the in-product icons spin.

## Why it works and transfer

The system is tested at two scales. Create a small readable glyph first, then derive a richer brand rendition. Keep meaningful cutouts, direction and silhouette through both versions. Decide which shape parameters are shared and which vary per function; do not try to shrink a refractive render into an operational icon.

## Limitations and transfer risks

The artist's rendering stack, materials and geometric construction are not exposed by the video. A logo-like sparkle can be intentional identity; judging it solely by its shape misses the system. Do not copy the Responsive glyph geometry or assets.
