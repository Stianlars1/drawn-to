# Breathing glow rosette as AI thinking state

## What we see
A single 15s clip (2724x1724 @ 30fps) of a three-petal light rosette - like a rose curve r = cos(3θ) drawn in light - floating on a near-black field. The rosette continuously breathes (petals open into hollow outlined loops, then collapse into solid glowing teardrops) while a magenta→violet→blue hue gradient slowly orbits the shape. It is a "thinking/loading" state for an AI product, built with a Claude-made tool per the tweet, and reads as shader/canvas output, not keyframed vector animation.

## Layout & composition
- Single centered element: rosette center sits at ~50% x, ~48% y - optically centered, a hair above true center.
- Scale at full expansion: rosette spans ~16% of frame width (~440px of 2724px); at full contraction ~7% (~190px). Enormous negative space - the element occupies <3% of frame area.
- Background: near-black, ~#050408, with a barely-visible warm-magenta ambient wash upper-right and cool-blue wash lower-left (bleed from the hue field, ~2-3% opacity, radius ~60% of frame).
- Soft bloom halo behind the shape at all times, ~3-4x the rosette diameter, intensifying when the shape contracts (light compresses, halo brightens) - classic energy-conservation read.
- No container, no border, no chrome. The composition is pure figure-on-void.

## Type & color
No typography. Palette (additive/screen blending over black):
- Core: pure white #FFFFFF along petal centerlines, never clipping into a blob - the exact center of the rosette keeps a dark pinch point even at peak brightness.
- Magenta lobe: ~#E060D0 → #C94FB8
- Violet mid: ~#A78BFA → #8B5CF6
- Blue lobe: ~#4A7DFF → #5B8CFF
- Background: ~#050408
Contrast strategy: extreme - white-hot cores at ~21:1 against black, with 3-4 falloff stops (white → saturated hue → dim hue haze → black) doing all the depth work. Hue is mapped angularly around the shape (each petal picks up the color of its compass position), so rotation of the hue field re-colors the petals over time.

## Graphic language
Generative light-painting, not illustration: the rosette is a polar SDF / rose-curve stroked with a Gaussian glow falloff and additive blending. Construction details:
- At expansion the petals are hollow - bright outline, dark interior - reading as ribbons of light; at contraction the loops close into solid filled teardrops. The morph between stroke-state and fill-state is the signature move.
- Petal tips stay sharpest; brightness tapers toward the center junction.
- Center detail: petal bases converge into a crisp dark 3-point star of negative space that survives every state - the shape never blows out to a formless blob.
- Halo layers: tight bloom (~1.2x shape) + wide ambient (~3-4x) at much lower alpha; both hue-tinted, not white.
- Zero grain/noise; the falloff is perfectly smooth, which is what makes it read as premium shader work.

## Motion timeline
Extraction: 90 frames / 15.0s = 6 fps sampling, ~167ms per frame. Two superimposed periodic systems, no discrete events:

1. Breathing (scale + stroke-to-fill morph). Minima at t ≈ 1.3s (f_008), 3.7s (f_022), 6.0s (f_036), 8.3s (f_050), 10.8s (f_065), 13.0s (f_078) → period ~2.33s (14 frames). Contraction ~1.1s, expansion ~1.2s - near-symmetric sinusoidal ease-in-out (sin² LFO character), no overshoot, no hold at either extreme (minimum lasts <200ms). At each minimum: petals fill solid, bloom halo brightens ~2x, apparent brightness compresses into the core.
2. Hue-field orbit. The magenta lobe travels clockwise around the rosette: NW at t=0 → N at ~5s → NE/E at ~7-9s → SE at ~13s → S at ~15s. ~225° in 15s → ~15°/s, linear, implying a full hue revolution every ~24s. Blue lobe stays diametrically opposite; violet blends between. Because hue period (~24s) and breath period (~2.33s) are non-commensurate, no two breaths look identical - the loop never visibly repeats.
3. Rotation of geometry itself is minimal-to-none; the perceived rotation comes from the hue field sweeping across a mostly stable 3-fold symmetric form.

Easing summary: breathing = ease-in-out sinusoidal, ~2330ms period; hue orbit = linear, ~24000ms period; halo intensity = inverse-coupled to scale, same 2330ms period.

## Extractable rules
- Build indefinite loading states from two superimposed periods that don't divide evenly (e.g. 2330ms breath + 24000ms hue orbit) so the loop never visibly repeats.
- Use sinusoidal ease-in-out with zero hold and zero overshoot for ambient "breathing" motion; period 2000-2500ms.
- Couple glow-halo intensity inversely to element scale: contract → brighten ~2x, expand → dim; light feels conserved.
- Morph strokes to fills during contraction (hollow outline at max, solid teardrop at min) instead of only scaling.
- Keep a dark negative-space anchor at the center of any glow composition; never let the hotspot clip to a formless white blob.
- Map hue angularly around the shape and rotate the hue field linearly (~15°/s) rather than rotating the geometry.
- Use additive blending over near-black (#050408, not #000) with 3-4 falloff stops: white core → saturated hue → dim haze → black.
- Palette for AI/thinking states: magenta #E060D0, violet #8B5CF6, blue #4A7DFF, white cores.
- Size an ambient state indicator at 7-16% of frame width; give it >95% negative space.
- Let background inherit a 2-3% opacity tinted wash from the element's hue field so the void isn't dead flat.
- Reserve full-choreography glow shaders for states users stare at (thinking/loading); the calm sinusoidal cadence is the point, not spectacle.
