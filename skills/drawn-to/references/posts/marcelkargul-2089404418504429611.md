# Hunt.io footer - one prism emitting light shafts over the dot map

## What we see
The closing section reuses the hero's asset: a single cyan hex prism dead
center on the hex-dot world map, radiating light SHAFTS (anisotropic
wedges) across the dots; two-line H1 "Find the Threat Before It Finds You."
top-left, sub + pill top-right, wordmark + legal links at the bottom.

## Layout & composition
Full-viewport dark close; H1 64px/600 left, support right, footer row at
the base. The map is reused at the same scale - F3 single-asset discipline.

## Type & color
Same system as the hero; cyan #35c9d6 only; no red here (no threats in the
closing promise).

## Graphic language
Rays: 8-10 uneven wedges from the prism, linear falloff to transparent,
blurred; dot cells lit by proximity; tight radial base under the prism.
Light direction = outward from one source; nothing else glows.

## Why saved
Owner: "the footer is even better". Verified: the close is a second hero
built from the SAME asset with a different light behaviour (rings → rays).

## Extractable rules
1. Close with the hero asset re-lit, not a new illustration.
2. Shafts = uneven wedges with linear falloff + blur, masked radially.
3. One source, one hue, copy in the corners, object in the center.
