# Animated three-card feature section, camera-tour style

## What we see
A light, airy SaaS feature section ("HUB — All-in-one place. For everyone. Start now.") for an affiliate/partner product (PartnerStack-style), with a grayscale logo bar and three feature cards: Roles, Use cases, Company sizes. A 13.5 s choreographed video (958x720 @ 60fps native, sampled at 6 fps): the section blur-reveals word by word, then a virtual camera zooms in and dollies card-to-card while each card's vignette plays a live micro-demo — avatar drag-and-drop, a radial dial loader, and a company-size slider with a synced employee count-up — before zooming back out and fading to the loop point.

## Layout & composition
- Section container: rounded panel (~radius 16-20px) spanning ~84% of frame width, centered, on a paler page background (#F0F5FE). Panel fill is a soft mesh gradient: near-white top-left with lavender bloom (#E6E4F6), sky blue right edge (#C9DDF6), whitening toward the card row.
- Vertical rhythm (est. at 1440px design width): eyebrow → 16px → H1 (2 lines) → 24px → 2-line subcopy → 48px → logo bar → 72px → card grid → 48px bottom padding.
- Logo bar: 6 grayscale logos (iHeartMedia, ADP, Oracle, Intercom, Logitech, MongoDB), single row, even gaps ~56-64px, all tinted one gray (#7E8AA0), optically size-matched.
- Card grid: 3 equal columns, column width ~380px, gap ~32px. Each column = white visual card on top + text block below sitting directly on the section background (no card chrome around text).
- Visual cards: white #FFFFFF, radius ~16px, no border, shadow ~0 8px 24px rgba(24,36,80,0.08); inner vignettes get their own second-level shadows (~0 12px 28px rgba(24,36,80,0.12)).
- No dividers anywhere; hierarchy is carried by whitespace and elevation only.

## Card anatomy
Ratio per column ≈ 55% visual / 45% text. Text block: title (20px/600) → 8px → 2-3 line description (15px/400, ~1.5 line-height) → 12px → "Explore X →" link (14px/500, blue, arrow with 4px hover-gap feel).
- Card 1 "Roles": nested mini-card ("Building Blocks" row with red-orange isometric-cubes icon, divider, "MARKETING TEAM" caption with megaphone icon, row of 5 circular photo avatars ~36px) sitting on a stacked deck — two offset card layers peek out below, implying a pile of teams.
- Card 2 "Use cases": row of 5 neumorphic circular dial knobs (~56px, extruded look: top-left white highlight, bottom-right #D9E0EC shade) hung from a thin bracket line; center knob active with a dashed blue progress arc; a connector line drops to a profile row "Frank Allen — Cloud Architect at Roodle" (avatar 44px, "Roodle" as blue link).
- Card 3 "Company sizes": left half is a rounded panel with a vertical white→sky→cobalt gradient (#FFFFFF→#9CC4F2→#4E8BE8) beside a vertical ruler of tick marks; caps labels STARTUP/SMALL/MID/ENTERPRISE bottom-to-top; active label darkens and gets a long dash; floating white chip "Chipoodle" (red dotted-flower logo, 40px) with "N employees" line, elevated on its own shadow.

## Type & color
- Grotesque sans throughout (Neue Haas/Helvetica Now character). H1 ~56px/1.15, weight 500-600, slightly tight tracking (-1%), color deep navy #1B2A50. Sentence punctuation ("place.", "now.") used as rhythm device across two lines.
- Eyebrow "HUB": ~11px all-caps, +8% tracking, link blue.
- Body/subcopy: ~15-16px, slate #5C6B8A. Card titles 20px/600 navy; ruler labels 11px caps +6% tracking #7C879C (active #303B55).
- Palette: page #F0F5FE; panel gradient #FDFDFF/#E6E4F6/#C9DDF6; navy text #1B2A50; slate #5C6B8A; action blue #2F5BE7; logo gray #7E8AA0; single warm accent red-orange #E8442E reserved for the two fictional brand logos (Building Blocks, Chipoodle). Contrast strategy: navy-on-pastel for headers, everything interactive in one blue, one warm accent at ~2% surface area as focal spice.

## Graphic language
Features are depicted as UI fragments — credible mini product components, not abstract illustration: an avatar-team card deck, a bank of tactile dial knobs, a size slider with a live chip. Construction details: photographic circular avatars (diverse, business-dress, consistent crop); neumorphic extrusion used only inside the knob vignette as tactile contrast against the otherwise flat design; org-chart connector lines (1px, #D9E0EC) literally wire the knob to a person; a custom cursor drawn as a rounded blue translucent triangle. Fictional brands (Roodle, Chipoodle) keep it demo-honest while the real logo bar carries credibility.

## Motion timeline
Extraction: 81 frames / 13.48 s ≈ 6 fps sampled (native 60fps); t = frame/6.
- 0.0-0.2 s: empty pale-blue canvas.
- 0.2-1.6 s intro reveal: headline resolves in word groups from gaussian blur + opacity ("All-in-one" → "place." → "For everyone." → "Start now."), reading order, each word-group ~400-500 ms blur→sharp, stagger ~100-150 ms, ease-out. Eyebrow and gradient panel fade in first; logos fade up center-out; card vignettes enter bottom-up with blur — center card first (f_003), side cards ~150 ms later; card titles resolve before descriptions; links last. Whole page is readable by t≈1.6 s.
- 1.6-1.9 s: hold on full section.
- 1.9-2.6 s: camera zooms into card 1 (scale ~1.0→2.2 with left pan, ~700 ms, ease-in-out); custom cursor enters already carrying a 6th avatar.
- 2.6-5.4 s: drag choreography — cursor arcs the avatar from bottom-right, up past the row, hovers with slight bob and tilt (avatar rotates ~±8° following drag direction), then drops; the avatar row reshuffles to accept it (f_033-f_034), stacked deck below implies more teams. Deliberate ~2.8 s presentational pace, spring-smoothed cursor easing.
- 5.4-6.3 s: lateral dolly one column right to card 2 (~800 ms, ease-in-out); the center knob's dashed blue arc is already animating mid-pan.
- 6.3-8.7 s: hold on card 2; dashed arc sweeps around the active knob like radial progress (~2 s/cycle, linear, ambient loop); connector line anchors it to the Frank Allen row.
- 8.7-9.3 s: dolly right to card 3 (~700 ms).
- 9.3-11.3 s: slider tick slides SMALL→MID→ENTERPRISE while the chip counter counts up 14→22→43→90→111→122→129→132 employees — counter increments are synced to tick position and eased together (big steps mid-travel, 122→129→132 settle = ease-out tail ~2 s total); active caps label darkens as the tick passes it.

- 12.5-13.5 s: whole section fades to the pale-blue blank (~800 ms) — clean loop point back to frame 1.

## Why saved
No owner note. This is squarely the owner's taste: airy premium light UI plus motion that SHOWS the feature instead of describing it. The craft moves a generic AI page would miss: (1) headline revealed in word groups synced to reading order, not a single fade; (2) a single continuous virtual camera (zoom → dolly → dolly → pull-back) that turns a static marketing section into a guided product tour; (3) every card vignette is a functioning micro-demo with real state change — an avatar row that actually accepts a drop, a counter that actually counts, not looping decoration; (4) the count-up is eased in lockstep with the slider tick, so number and control read as one mechanism; (5) neumorphic knobs used as deliberate tactile contrast inside one vignette only; (6) one warm accent hex shared by both fictional logos across cards; (7) ambient loops persist through the zoom-out, so the section feels live rather than keyframed; (8) fade-out returns exactly to the intro's blank state for a seamless loop.

## Extractable rules
- Frame a light feature section in a rounded mesh-gradient panel (lavender→sky pastels, ~radius 16-20px) on an even paler page, cards in pure white on top.
- Reveal headlines in word groups from blur+fade (400-500 ms each, 100-150 ms stagger, ease-out), in reading order; body and links resolve last.
- Depict features as UI fragments — credible mini components with real state — instead of abstract illustration.
- Give every feature card one micro-interaction that demonstrates the promise (drag-drop, progress dial, slider+counter).
- Sync count-up numbers to the control driving them; ease both together with an ease-out settle (~2 s, final steps small).
- Tour a static section with one continuous camera: zoom in ~700 ms ease-in-out, dolly card-to-card ~700-800 ms, pull back ~900 ms.
- Keep ambient loops (spinners, dials) running during camera moves so the scene never freezes.
- Put card chrome only around visuals; let titles, descriptions, and links sit directly on the section background — hierarchy by whitespace and elevation, zero dividers.
- Reserve one warm accent (#E8442E-ish) at ~2% surface area in an otherwise blue/navy palette; everything interactive gets a single blue (#2F5BE7).
- Tint all partner logos one gray (#7E8AA0) and optically size-match them in a single row.
- Use soft double-tier shadows: outer card 0 8px 24px rgba(24,36,80,0.08), inner vignette slightly stronger 0 12px 28px at 0.12.
- Imply depth of content with a stacked-deck effect: 2 offset card layers peeking 6-10px below the top mini-card.
- End choreographies by fading back to the intro's blank state for a seamless loop (~800 ms).
