## Graphic language
- The band is a processed field, not a gradient: a mint ground, a square lattice of cells with randomised luminance, a central white bloom, and glow on the lit cells - a light-mode cousin of the pixel-mosaic aurora in kevserctk-2090 and of the halftone slab in basit_designs-2089995, but built as a lit matrix rather than a screened image.
- One object, rendered as material: the sparkle glyph gets a radial dark-centre gradient, heavy stipple grain, 6 px rounded tips, feathered edges and two satellite plus marks at two sizes - the treatment that keeps a stock glyph from reading as an icon.
- Three surface physics in one card: frosted (close disc), gel (pill: top rim, bottom glow, tinted ambient shadow), machined (CTA: outer rim, inner top highlight, vertical falloff, tight drop). The card and tiles stay flat. Chrome that is pressable is the only chrome that is modelled.
- Radius ladder 30 / 11 / 8 / pill, with 30 - 18 padding = 12 ~ the CTA's 11: the CTA is concentric with the card within 1 px.
- No borders anywhere: the whole separation system is a 4% tile tint, one large card shadow and two element shadows.

## Extractable rules
- Modal: white card 492 wide, radius 30, padding 18, no border, shadow offset downward only (edge 5% dark, tail past 130 px; 0 30px 80px rgba(0,0,0,.08) class) on a neutral #EBEBEB-class page.
- Give the header 36% of the card height (226 of 628) and clip it with the card's own radius; never draw a seam under it - let the field's bloom reach 253+ at the seam so the join disappears.
- Build the band as a lit-cell matrix: ground #C1DFD9-#D1E8E5, cells ~4 px on a ~6.8 px pitch (gap ~2.8), per-cell luminance randomised so roughly a quarter are lit near-white and the rest sit 1-4% above ground; add a 2-3 px glow on lit cells and a radial white bloom (+20 luminance over the edge value) behind the object; bias lit cells into vertical runs of 2-4.
- Render the single object as material, never as an icon: radial gradient dark centre #208367 to tips #68AD9A, stipple grain over the whole fill, 6 px tip radius, ~2 px feathered edge, two satellite plus marks at ~20 and ~16 px with 6 px round-ended bars in the tip colour; centre the GROUP in the band, not the object.
- Put a frosted control over art: 32 px disc, white at ~50%, no border, no blur, inset 14 px from the band's top and right; 10.5 px X at 1.4 px stroke, round caps, #1F2324.
- Type ladder: 24/600 #050505 title, 16/500 #000 row titles, 16/400-500 #5C5C5C body at 1.4 line pitch; demote with gray first, weight second; nothing above 600.
- Checklist rows on a 60 px pitch: 32x46 tile, radius 8, #F5F5F5 (a 4% step, no border), check 16 px #0D0D0D, 13.5 px gap to text, title over one gray line at 23 px pitch, text block vertically centred on the tile.
- Primary CTA, light mode: 456x40, radius 11 (= card radius - padding, within 1 px), label 16 white centred; fill #333333 to #2D2D2D top to bottom, 1 px outer rim #343434, 1 px inner highlight #696969 at the top fading over 12 px and weakening to #4D4D4D on the sides and #3D3D3D at the foot, shadow 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.15).
- Discount pill: 70x28 full pill on the title's baseline, right-aligned to the padding; vertical gradient #4E927D rim, #329075 body, #73AC9B bottom glow; ambient shadow tinted to the pill hue (~rgba(50,144,117,.2) at the edge, 20 px below, 10 px around).
- Quarantine the hue: exactly one green-teal family, spent on the offer (pill) and the object (star, plus marks); the commitment button stays ink-black; every other surface is gray.
- Model only what is pressable: frosted, gel and machined finishes go on the three controls; the card, the tiles and the text stay flat.
- Secondary action is text only: 16 px #626262, centred, 19.5 px under the CTA, no underline, no box.

## Limitations and transfer risks
- The object is the stock four-point AI sparkle with plus marks - the glyph quality-bar names as a tell. The material treatment (grain, radial gradient, rounded tips, feathered edge) is what saves it; a flat vector of the same shape would fail the swap test.
- Three check-tile + bold title + gray blurb rows are the icon + paragraph shape (C2). They pass only because each check is a semantic entitlement in a plan comparison, not a decorative icon.
- "AI-powered budget insights" is category copy, not product copy; the other two rows are specific.
