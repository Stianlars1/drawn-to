# Feature-card reference plates - third-party material

Sixteen plates covering the RANGE of ways a feature can be presented. They exist
so an agent proposing or building a feature section can SEE the level, instead of
inferring it from prose. Deliberately varied: dark and light and paper, product
fragments and line-art and photography and isometry, large bento cells and
three-up rows and near-empty micro cards.

All are downscaled to 1440 px wide (or their native width if smaller) and
re-encoded at quality 82-84. `dark-bento-scenes.jpg` and the two `iso-*` bento
plates are composed from video frames.

**Rights.** Every page and artwork belongs to its author. Nothing here is
licensed by this repository and the repository's own licence does not extend to
these files. They are included as study material, the way a design review pins
printouts to a wall.

**Rules of use, enforced by the skill.**

- References are vocabulary, never templates. A lock cites the PATTERN ("one
  soft-shaded isometric scene per automation, dashed leader paths between
  cells"), never "make it look like this image".
- Never trace, copy or re-use artwork, copy, or marks from a plate in a build.
- Never ship a plate, or a crop of one, as an asset of a delivered site.
- Being in this folder is not an endorsement. `gradient-sheet.jpg` is here
  partly as a counter-example; three of its six tiles fail the tells in
  `quality-bar.md` § 1, and the audit says which.

Own, freely reusable schematics live in `../plates/` (CC0, geometry only).

If you own one of these pages and would rather the plate were not here, open an
issue on the repository and it will be removed.
