# Showcase style export and original-page review

Version 1.2.0 adds a distinct visual brief to all 55 scenes and refines the
original 30. The newer 25 keep their established artwork and rendering code.
The optical glass, woven strips and original cinematic art remain the visual
calibration targets, not just examples of a palette.

## What the prompt contains

Each prompt identifies one stable scene and describes its composition,
material/light/type, meaningful behavior, required assets, reference vocabulary,
actual implementation and scene-specific acceptance checks. It carries enough
context to use in a new conversation. The original studies that synthesize
several ideas do not receive invented single-post provenance.

The canonical 55 profiles live in the portable skill. A generator validates
scene coverage, unique IDs, existing source paths and generated-file drift.
The page fetches only the selected prompt. Copy and preview are available from
every header; denied clipboard access provides selectable full text. Install
commands and workflow prompts have separate labels.

## Skill review

The earlier 1.1 review already scoped the twelve defaults and expanded the
reference library. This pass found remaining conflicts deeper in the older
blend, layout, interview, poster and animation guidance. It updates those active
instructions rather than appending contradictory exceptions. It adds the exact
showcase contracts and explicit checks for hidden mobile evidence, truthful
archive data and realistic renderer/asset requirements.

The 85-reference metadata, media and link validation is retained. This is not a
new frame-by-frame review or live remeasurement of every historical source.
The scene prompts were reviewed against their implementation and visual targets;
they are not a guarantee of identical output from every model. This pass does
not claim to have regenerated 55 separate downstream websites from the prompts.

## Verification

Browser evidence covers the original 30 at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844,
with before/after captures in reduced motion; dynamic behavior is checked separately. Prompt verification covers all 55 real clipboard
exports and all 165 header/viewport combinations, preview/focus/manual-copy and
loading-failure states, plus the repaired original controls and preserved mobile
content. The existing full showcase verification covers all 55 routes and the
newer interaction, GPU, reduced-motion, disposal and poster behaviors.

The visual judgment is deliberately scoped: the older pages remain quieter
technical/editorial studies. They are clearer and more complete, with stronger
material and illustrative detail where it serves their purpose. They do not
all attempt the cinematic density of Night Garden or the physical optics of
Optical Type; forcing that treatment onto every page would erase useful range.
