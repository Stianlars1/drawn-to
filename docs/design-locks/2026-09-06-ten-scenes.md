# Ten scenes, a stronger first impression

The owner requests ten additional pages, greater material/detail quality and
more difference between them. They explicitly selected both varied media and
advanced 3D/light, and authorized publication after review and testing. The
existing Drawn To product, reference library and shared catalog remain the brief.
This is continuation of the approved showcase direction, not a new product.

## Composition and craft

| Stable ID | Concept and material promise | Route and actual interaction |
|---|---|---|
| optical-type | Huge black typography seen through a thick clear optical lens on white | Three.js transmission through a real text texture; pointer and keyboard-controlled lens position |
| woven-spectrum | A dense folded structure with bright spectral edges and dark reflective intervals | Original Three.js ribbon geometry; change fold/tension |
| aperture-control | Machined metal, radial engraving, dark blade layers and a controlled aperture | Original Three.js instrument; an accessible range changes the physical blade opening |
| night-garden | A cinematic glass conservatory at dusk, with water, transmitted light and atmospheric depth | Original imagegen artwork; inspect composed detail areas |
| paper-theatre | A tactile paper world, folded edges, fiber, creases and grounded shadows | Original imagegen artwork in a dimensional editorial layout; switch reading chapters |
| specimen-cabinet | A curated set of distinct physical material specimens | Original imagegen specimen atlas; selected item and inspectable detail remain coherent |
| living-terrarium | A close botanical miniature with glass, condensation, moss and visible contact | Original imagegen artwork; a controlled detail lens reveals the delivered texture |
| chromatic-relief | Repeated physical fins become one varied sculptural surface | Original Three.js relief; change the wave/shape parameter |
| contact-sheet | A photographer's editing surface with film edges and intentional crops | DOM layout using the new original art; inspect and reorder a selection with real controls |
| red-chamber | Large chrome folds against deep red textile, strong grazing light and shadow | Original imagegen artwork; inspect full composition and a close material detail |

The routes are chosen for delivered quality. A prerender or generated image is
identified as such in provenance and is not described as a live simulated scene.
Three.js is not a quality claim by itself. Judge all large faces, joins, reflections
and crops at delivery size. There are no invented customers, metrics or services.

Study sources: the already inspected September archive, especially material and
spatial work by Basit and Dmitry Lepisov; image/crop composition by LexnLin;
photographic and varied hero architecture by uihssn; physical detail from Nils
Eller; repeated identity geometry by Adrian Kuleszo; and varied operational
evidence from Oguz. These are property-level influences, not claims that a source
contains our new subject or uses our rendering stack.

## Implementation boundaries

- Keep the descriptor API `render({command})` and
  `mount(root, {signal, reducedMotion, still}) -> cleanup`.
- `site/js/catalog-order.js` owns the curated stable-ID sequence and validates
  it against the actual registered pages, independent of chronology numbers.
- New descriptors/styles are split into studio (real-time 3D), editorial
  (image compositions) and collection (cabinet/contact sheet) units. Existing
  45 page implementations are preserved apart from home/order integration.
- A new studio renderer owns lifecycle, camera, environment and resource cleanup;
  each scene supplies its own geometry, materials and interaction state. Reuse
  the locally pinned Three.js 0.185.1, without another framework or package.
- Stop motion under reduced motion, hidden tab, offscreen state, Save-Data and
  explicit pause. A departed scene must not leave a canvas or late draw behind.
- Every real-time scene has composed desktop/mobile posters and a tested forced
  poster route. Poster interactions must have meaningful matching states.
- Original asset prompts, outputs and source roles are recorded beside the art.
  Reference media stays in the skill library, not the shipped page artwork.
