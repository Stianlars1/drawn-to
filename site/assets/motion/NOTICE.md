# Original motion-expansion assets

The five raster scenes (cloud-chamber, ember-portrait, terrain-interface,
sunlit-field, thermal-type) were generated specifically for Drawn To on
2026-09-07 with the built-in OpenAI image tool. They are original artwork, not
extracted reference frames. The generation briefs are retained in the source
repository documentation and the owner's local evidence archive.

The posters are renders of the original scene implementations under
`site/js/motion/`. The Silver Tide films are deterministic exports of its
original wave shader, with a 16-second analytic loop and no duplicate final
frame. Their frame count and rendering provenance are in `films/provenance.json`.

The runtime uses the already-vendored Three.js under its MIT license. No
Orbkit or XorDev shader source is included in these effects. The external
reference material is separate study evidence under the skill's media notice.
