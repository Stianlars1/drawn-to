Top left: amber resin block with deep carved curved aperture, suspended tiny bubbles, thick honey-colored transmission and brilliant edges. Top middle: cobalt-blue fluted glazed porcelain vessel with beautiful uneven glaze, thin lip, finely described radial ridges. Bottom right: a thick pale green cast-glass spiral with small internal bubbles, subtle frosted outer edge and visible caustic/refraction on the neutral ground. Each specimen centered inside its own cell and occupies roughly 65 percent of that cell, fully in frame, medium close product photography. The six compositions should have distinct silhouettes and honest material behavior. No repeats, no generic rounded cubes, no logos, no writing, no watermarks, no hands. Treat this as an art-directed museum material collection, not UI.
