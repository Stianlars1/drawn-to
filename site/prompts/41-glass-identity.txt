COMPOSITION
A large translucent sculptural mark occupies the right of a pale green editorial page. A simple left headline and progressively sized flat versions of the same mark make the relationship between identity and material explicit.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Sage-white ground and deep green type. Use a clear polished volume with a restrained cool cast, tighter bevels, dark/bright edge intervals and softly grounded depth. A typographic plane genuinely behind the mark makes refraction visible, while crisp flat vector marks retain the same silhouette.

BEHAVIOR
A very quiet rotation or pointer reaction can reveal thickness. Use an actual modeled mark for a changing viewpoint and a matching composed poster fallback. Keep small flat marks in crisp vector form.

ASSET AND IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
The folded mark is original procedural geometry. Its optical backing plane and typography are composed inside the renderer and are actually sampled through the glass. This is a known scene, not arbitrary DOM refraction.

ACCEPTANCE
Inspect an actual landmark through the volume and its unchanged portion outside it, at rest and under a changing view. Look for coherent thickness and reflections, with a readable clear center. A mint fill or a transmission setting alone is not proof of the intended optical finish.
Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant. A neat page with matching colors alone is not enough. Test all controls and motion extremes, keyboard/touch, reduced motion and relevant fallback states. Preserve content and evidence on mobile; recompose or allow controlled reading space instead of hiding them. Stop timers/rendering when hidden or unmounted, avoid needless per-frame CPU work and measure performance before making claims. Report what still differs and refine the substantive gaps before calling it finished.
