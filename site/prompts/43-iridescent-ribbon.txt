COMPOSITION
An oversized folded ribbon sweeps through a warm neutral stage beside or above a compact editorial title. Let the silhouette and its negative space dominate; secondary captions and a pause control stay small.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Pearl paper ground and thin-film metallic color. Model a continuous ribbon with smooth curvature, thickness and distinct front/back lighting. Iridescent hue follows angle and reflection, with bright edges and darker folds, not a static rainbow painted over a flat blob.

BEHAVIOR
Slow rotation reveals the material. Pause is immediate. Use view-dependent physical shading for interactive motion and a visually matched poster when GPU is unavailable.

Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant.
