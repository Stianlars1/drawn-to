COMPOSITION
Place large readable type in a protected central zone while a curated collection of image cards occupies the surrounding field with varied size, rotation, depth order and generous intervals. Give every card distinct material or imagery and establish an authored sequence of subject, crop, scale and overlap rather than scattering arbitrary rectangles.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use warm near-white and graphite for the interface while richly rendered objects and illustrations carry the collection's color. Keep the central type stronger than every individual image card and protect the heading, action and header throughout the composition. Use crisp card edges, subtle thickness, small shadows and controlled overlaps to establish front-to-back order.

BEHAVIOR
Animate image transforms slowly inside a tested envelope while type and actions remain fixed; reduced motion holds an intentional authored arrangement. Provide a pause action and recompose the collection for portrait screens without losing the central reading zone.

ACCEPTANCE
Sample multiple timestamps and every travel extreme for collisions with the heading, actions and header, and compare the actual art quality rather than only the number of cards.
Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant. A neat page with matching colors alone is not enough. Test all controls and motion extremes, keyboard/touch, reduced motion and relevant fallback states.
