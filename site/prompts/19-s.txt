COMPOSITION
Three small paper documents rest on a warm desk-like ground. Slightly different rotations and offset crop corners make the collection feel handled. Each contains a revision code, narrow serif title and a short note.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm cream #f2efe8, ivory sheets, muted gold registration marks and charcoal ink. Add a subtle lifted paper corner, a fine outer edge and soft directional contact shadow. Texture remains secondary to the type.
