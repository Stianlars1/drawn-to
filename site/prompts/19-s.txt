COMPOSITION
Rest three small paper documents on a warm desk-like ground, using slight rotation differences and offset crop corners; each contains a revision code, narrow title and short note.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use warm cream #f2efe8, ivory sheets, muted gold registration marks and charcoal ink. Set each slip's title in a narrow serif and keep its revision code and note clearly readable. Model a subtle lifted corner, fine outer edge and soft directional contact shadow on the paper, keeping texture secondary to type.

BEHAVIOR
Keep the arrangement still and retain all three slips at narrow widths rather than removing one to fit a poster height.
