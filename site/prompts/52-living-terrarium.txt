COMPOSITION
A massive thick-glass vessel containing a miniature moss garden dominates the right. A calm left editorial column introduces the study, followed by three numbered inspection points. A circular loupe lets a small detail carry as much attention as the whole silhouette.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Sage-gray ground, dark green type, thick clear glass, condensation beads, layered ferns, wet stone, soft moss and a small dark pool. Glass distorts the edge of the miniature scene and carries large soft reflections. The light penetrates the canopy and leaves contact shadows on the rocks.

BEHAVIOR
