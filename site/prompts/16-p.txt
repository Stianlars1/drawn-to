COMPOSITION
Place a narrow numbered chapter rail beside one spacious detail panel. Give the active item a tiny registration line and keep panel title, prose and a short evidence list in one stable layout.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use an almost-white ground, white panel, graphite typography and one muted blue registration accent. Use muted monospace indices in the rail and graphite reading typography in the detail panel. Give the focal panel a restrained radius, broad faint elevation shadow and crisp surface edge without turning the whole page into a panel.

BEHAVIOR
Support click, keyboard focus and activation, and touch as equivalent ways to select a chapter; rapid selections resolve to the latest choice. Swap the panel in place with a brief dissolve. Any auto-demo stops after user input and under reduced motion.
