COMPOSITION
Frame a sheet containing twelve aligned rows, with a narrow title, small document metadata, row numbers, dotted leaders, a right-aligned value column and a thin print band closing the sheet.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use warm gray board, white paper, charcoal text and faded red row numbers. Use a narrow serif title, small metadata, faded row indices and tabular monospace values, with exact baseline and final-digit alignment. Keep dotted-leader density exact and confine minute halftone detail to the print treatment. Let subtle paper edges provide the physical depth.
