COMPOSITION
A large white exhibition mat on warm paper. A tall, narrow serif title occupies the upper left; small specimen metadata occupies the opposite corner. A saturated printed slab anchors the lower quarter, with a large clean paper interval between.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm off-white #f3f2ee, charcoal ink, Instrument Serif or a similarly narrow editorial serif. Use physical mat edges, fine crop marks, pink-to-violet ink, a bounded halftone region and subtle print texture. Halftone and registration details must be crisp enough to inspect, not a noisy full-screen filter.

BEHAVIOR
Keep the print still. The only functional movement is the short action confirmation. A complete mat stays inside its available stage.

ACCEPTANCE
Do not crop off the mat corners or cover the ink slab with navigation. At narrow widths move metadata into the reading flow.
Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant. A neat page with matching colors alone is not enough. Test all controls and motion extremes, keyboard/touch, reduced motion and relevant fallback states. Preserve content and evidence on mobile; recompose or allow controlled reading space instead of hiding them. Stop timers/rendering when hidden or unmounted, avoid needless per-frame CPU work and measure performance before making claims.
