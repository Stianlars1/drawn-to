COMPOSITION
Anchor a warm closing page with one oversized friendly character, balanced by large editorial words, generous open space and compact useful next actions rather than decorative cards. Create an original character with rounded volumes, deliberate pose and tiny face and edge details; personality must come from proportion and posture rather than an emoji-like blob.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use cream, olive and muted red accents, keeping the interface quiet enough for the illustrated character to carry the color. Mix humane serif emphasis with clear sans controls, keeping the closing words spacious and the actions concise. Give the character tactile modeled surfaces and a soft contact shadow that places it on the page rather than inside a card.

BEHAVIOR
If the character moves, preserve its silhouette and keep all reading text still; a one-way state change plays once and holds rather than jumping back into a loop. Let closing actions download a starting brief or copy an install command, and report each result accurately beside its control.
