COMPOSITION
A large field of repeated colored relief elements forms an architectural wave beneath a spare editorial heading. Small bottom controls adjust expression; large clear negative space separates the type from the sculptural rhythm.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm plaster ground, cobalt type and alternating saturated material faces with believable edge light and contact shadows. Model each relief element with thickness. Repetition supplies the visual identity through phase, spacing, depth and reflected color, not through identical flat rectangles.

BEHAVIOR
Quiet/Flowing/Sculptural presets and a continuous expression slider adjust the collective relief. Slow motion alters the rhythm while the heading remains fixed. Pause, reduced motion and preset posters preserve a complete composition.
