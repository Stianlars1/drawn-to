COMPOSITION
Build a large architectural wave from repeated colored relief elements beneath a spare editorial heading. Protect large clear negative space between type and sculpture and keep small expression controls at the bottom. Give every relief element thickness. Use repetition, phase, spacing and depth to create the identity rather than identical flat rectangles.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use a warm plaster ground, cobalt type and alternating saturated material faces. Use believable edge light, reflected color and coherent contact shadows to preserve separation between the thick relief units.
