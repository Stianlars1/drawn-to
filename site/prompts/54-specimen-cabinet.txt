COMPOSITION
A carefully photographed cabinet of six material specimens forms the main field, balanced by restrained editorial type and a small selected-specimen description. The grid is an actual material collection, with each object carrying its own surface vocabulary.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm chalk, stone, glass, metal and softer organic textures under one consistent studio light. Objects need coherent scale, contact shadows, fine edges and enough resolution to compare microstructure. Labels remain quiet and precise, like a museum drawer.

BEHAVIOR
