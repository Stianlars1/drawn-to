COMPOSITION
A full-bleed vermilion chamber surrounds one large polished sculptural chrome form. Oversized warm-white serif/sans words occupy a clear left zone. A compact detail action changes from the entire installation to an intimate material view.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Deep red #640f12, scarlet velvet, mirror-like silver and creamy type. Velvet needs directional pile, deep soft folds and diffuse red bounce. Chrome needs sharp highlight ribbons, dark reflected bands and curved distortion of the red environment. Contact shadows attach the hard form to the soft ground.

ACCEPTANCE
Inspect where chrome meets velvet and compare sharp versus diffuse light. Avoid a plastic silver blob or uniformly lit red background. Ensure the type remains readable in both crops.
Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant.
