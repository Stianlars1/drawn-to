COMPOSITION
A dark navy editorial stage holds an oversized woven sweep of many narrow iridescent strips. The luminous bundle crosses diagonally through the right and lower field while a compact left headline occupies stable negative space. Small tension controls sit low in the composition.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Deep navy #080b16, silver-lilac type and cyan/violet/pink thin-film metal. The current scene models 22 separately thickened ribbon strips, with smooth long-wave curvature, twisting edge normals, very narrow gaps and a common studio environment. Fine material texture, occluded folds, crisp edge reflections and broad soft highlights make it feel woven and dimensional. Three.js physical metal uses roughness .14 and iridescence with 130-660nm thickness variation in this implementation.

BEHAVIOR
A tension slider alters the actual weave geometry from Open through Gathered to Folded. Slow restrained orientation changes reveal reflected light; provide pause and honor reduced motion. Rebuild geometry only when the control changes, dispose replaced buffers and stop rendering when hidden.
