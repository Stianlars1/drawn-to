COMPOSITION
Place three substantial onboarding choice cards beneath a short welcoming heading and compact mode selector; give each a title, clear promise, concise checklist and choice action, with a small character peeking over the row. Use a small original bird with clay-like volume, eyes, feathers and a grounded overlap at the top of the choice row; keep it supporting rather than dominant.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use warm ivory and burgundy with carefully tuned pink and cream surfaces. Use dark brown editorial type with a concise, clearly stepped hierarchy from welcome to title, promise, checklist and action. Give the cards fine borders, controlled corner radii and barely lifted downward shadows, using small warm tonal steps rather than a strong border.

BEHAVIOR
Make Explore, Specify and Build real keyboard- and touch-operable choices. The chosen card reads through a distinct warm surface, label and action, and updates title, explanation and outcome together without moving the whole layout.
