COMPOSITION
Three substantial warm onboarding choice cards sit beneath a short welcoming heading and a compact mode selector. A small original bird peeks over the card row. Each card has its own title, one clear promise, a concise checklist and a choice action; the chosen card has a distinct warm surface.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm ivory and burgundy, dark brown editorial type and carefully tuned pink/cream surfaces. The clay-like peeking bird is a small tactile character, with soft volume, eyes, feathers and grounded overlap. Cards use fine borders, controlled corner radii and barely lifted shadows. Selected state must read through surface, label and action together.

BEHAVIOR
Explore, Specify and Build are real selectable states. Update title, explanation and outcome together without moving the whole layout. Keyboard and touch expose the same result.
