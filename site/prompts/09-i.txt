COMPOSITION
Centre one oversized command button beneath a short headline on a quiet warm instrument ground.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use warm ivory #f2f1ee, graphite and a tiny orange or green indicator. Give the monochrome label an engraved appearance while retaining clear legibility. Construct a machined outer bezel, recessed track, raised cap, separate indicator window and small fasteners, with visible thickness, inset shadow, edge bevels and directional highlights.

BEHAVIOR
Depress the cap slightly on press while preserving the button's dimensions through every state. On successful copy, update the indicator and an accessible status; never display success after a failed copy.
