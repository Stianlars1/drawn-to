COMPOSITION
Centre a calibrated semicircular instrument on a fine dark engineering grid, placing a large numeric reading inside the arc with a small unit label and compact secondary readout row. Build thick and thin tick ranks, an inner hairline arc and an exact lit-segment endpoint around the named scale.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use black-green #040607, warm amber #ffb43c and dim machined gray. Use tabular monospace for the main value, unit and secondary readings. Concentrate the amber glow around lit evidence while preserving a dark core inside the light.

BEHAVIOR
