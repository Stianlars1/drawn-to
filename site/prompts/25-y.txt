COMPOSITION
Many tiny specimens form a meticulously spaced contact sheet on a warm editorial page. A narrow serif heading introduces the collection; a compact legend decodes the specimen fills.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Cream paper, black ink, inset tiny neutral marks and a subtle ruled border per tile. Variations in fill encode light, dark and mixed source modes. Keep texture and registration details at print scale.
