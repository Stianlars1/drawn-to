COMPOSITION
Three very large numerical shares dominate a dark editorial page. Each number has a short label, slider and concise explanation. A segmented total bar and a few presets reconcile the three below.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Black-green #0c1010, soft ivory numerals and sage accents. Digits are individually clipped in rolling windows with tabular alignment; the page feels like typography in motion, not a dashboard of tiny metric cards.

BEHAVIOR
Adjusting one weight redistributes the remainder so the total stays exactly 100. Animate digit wheels while the accessible numeric values update immediately. Presets set all three shares coherently. Reduced motion changes digits without rolling.

Test 0, 100, all presets and rapid reversals. Numerals must never clip their final state or wrap on phones; bars and values always reconcile.
Render and visually inspect at 1440x900, 1280x720 and 390x844, plus the actual target size. Compare the output to the selected scene at both whole-composition and close-up scale. Check hierarchy, silhouette, density of detail, material roughness/thickness, reflection shape, light direction, contact shadow and crop wherever relevant.
