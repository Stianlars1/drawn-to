COMPOSITION
A light editorial intro leads to a small source control, a compact menu and a larger adjacent preview. Hover/focus/selection makes the consequences of an option visible before commitment. The hero preview has a protected text column beside one original architectural paper sculpture, with consistent perspective, edge thickness and a grounded shadow.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Warm white #f8f7f2, forest-gray ink and quiet green selection. Menu rows have distinct miniature composition icons, short descriptive text and a small arrow. The preview is a real miniature page with a browser rim and well-drawn content. Sage structural planes frame ivory folded paper; one restrained ochre accent balances the scene. Review the miniature at its delivered size, not only a magnified SVG.
