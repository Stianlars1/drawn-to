COMPOSITION
Centre one compact dark product fragment containing a quiet question title bar, four aligned choice rows and a final total; each row pairs a selection control, name and numeric share.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
Use a violet-black ground, muted lilac selection fill and small luminous ticks. Order the typographic hierarchy as question, selected rows, numeric total and then metadata. Place the choices on a slightly lifted dark surface with a restrained inset border and fine row dividers.
