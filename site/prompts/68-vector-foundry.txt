COMPOSITION
Balance a large narrow serif statement with an isometric ruled wave sheet on a paper-and-blueprint surface. Frame it with a fine ground projection, specimen index, measured-looking annotation and small seal. Construct the wave from crisp continuous vector paths in a carefully proportioned 2:1-like isometric projection. MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
