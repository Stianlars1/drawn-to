COMPOSITION
Four unequal but aligned light widgets form a tactile working surface beneath a large editorial heading: note paper, a contrast specimen, a radius instrument and a review checklist.

MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
White and pale sage with dark green ink. Combine paper grain/edges, an oversized Aa specimen, miniature ruler ticks, a visibly changing shape and a precise completion mark. Maintain one family of edges and shadows while each widget has a different anatomy.

BEHAVIOR
Notes can be kept for the session, contrast colors swap with a computed ratio, a range input changes radius and checkboxes update the count. Label session-only persistence honestly. All controls work without the mouse.
