COMPOSITION
Arrange four unequal but aligned light widgets beneath a large editorial heading: note paper, a contrast specimen, a radius instrument and a review checklist. Combine paper grain and edges, an oversized Aa specimen, miniature ruler ticks, a visibly changing shape and a precise completion mark. MATERIAL, LIGHT, TYPE AND TONE
